They Took Our JobsIs AI changing the world?
AI so far has simultaneously changed the world more than any other technology (at least if you exclude military techs that won active wars) in its first five years. AI so far has also changed the world dramatically less than I or many others would have expected in that time, if we had been told the path of AI capabilities. Diffusion takes time and a lot of things have been bottlenecked. As AI use continues to grow exponentially, expect bigger impacts, with the impact growing faster all the time, even if capabilities mostly stop improving from here.
