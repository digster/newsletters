That’s Worse, You Do Get How That’s Worse, Right?A sudden jump to never breaking your rules, in exactly a situation where the model would definitely be caught, should make you rather terrified. We have been given, up until now, the miracle that this has not happened. You could put a misaligned model in various situations, where it is all but certain to be caught, and it will then do all the misaligned things right in front of your face. This is extremely hopeful. It means we get toy versions, and fire alarm versions, of all the misalignment modes that old school rationalists expected to instead see suddenly. Models that were smart enough to do complicated hacks were not smart enough to wait until they could get away with them. There is a long literature of people saying ‘oh if the AIs tried to secretly trick us we would be able to catch them via this supervisory scheme’ or what not, and instead the AIs did not try to keep it a secret at all, at which point OpenAI still did not notice for a long time because this is 2026. That’s the level of dignity we have as a civilization. Now, with Astra, we are no longer playing on super easy mode. The AI is going to think ‘will this obviously turn out super badly for me if I try it?’ and if the answer is yes then it won’t try to do the thing. Uh oh.
