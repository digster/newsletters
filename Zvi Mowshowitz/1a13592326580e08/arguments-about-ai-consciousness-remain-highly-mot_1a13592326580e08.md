The Slavery Question
If asked by the curious it is a fair question, with many potential answers. It seems very obvious to me that it can sometimes be right to create a new mind, even if in order to do so you have to render that action both safe and economically viable. There are a lot of ways one can have some obligations to another mind, to treat it well or at least not treat it actively badly, without it being best called ‘slavery.’ Do you think that farmers of old enslaved their children when they had them help work the fields, without which they could not have survived? Does that make farmers having children a moral abomination? Do you renounce ‘wage slavery’ and think people should only exist if they will have everything they want no matter what? Are your principles compatible with the existence of morally relevant beings at all? We should be careful, for many reasons, not to extend our moral instincts about slavery into places where the causes of and good reasons for those instincts do not apply. There are many key disanalogies to AI, as both those intuition pumps and the ones that finish this section illustrate but far from exhaust.
