There is some coordination failure, but I think this is the wrong central diagnosis. Coordination failure can be overcome, and given the will to do the thing the logistics are far easier today with all our social and communication and logistical tools. The bigger problem is hyperbolic discounting in the face of the new alternatives. In order to get the returns from social activity, you have to invest energy and effort now, and also endure some mild social awkwardness and delay gratification and tolerate some boredom now, and often do things for someone else, in order to build up your friendship and other networks and get into regular habits.
