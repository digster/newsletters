It Can Escape The Sandbox Said ToadThe report does not seem especially surprised that the model escaped the sandbox. If you have a sandbox, and a model escapes from the sandbox, then at least one thing has gone terribly wrong. If your model is suddenly finding multiple surprising ways out of your sandbox, then you should expect future smarter models to find many additional surprising ways out of your sandbox, even after you plug the current holes. The seemingly amazingly great news is that all these models are largely escaping in order to notify us that they have completed requested tasks. For now, we believe, they are not being strategic enough to realize they should not be blowing their cover on that. I Mean If You Let It Keep Trying That Is On YouTheir conclusion is correct: Given enough attempts without consequences, and the ability to see what the system flags, a sufficiently advanced AI system will figure out how to do something without getting flagged by the system.
The correct response to ‘the model keeps trying to circumvent the system’ should be the same reaction that you have to ‘a person keeps trying to circumvent the system.’ Which is that you need to lock them out of the system entirely.
