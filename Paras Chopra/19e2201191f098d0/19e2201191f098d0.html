Thanks for subscribing!

Most of us are trying to figure out life, one day at a time. The Inverted Passion Community Newsletter condenses the best of what other people have already figured out, and brings insights around Startups, Career, Life and Product, Productivity and Decision-making.

Get every edition straight to your inbox on Thursdays at 7 PM, IST.

Who writes this, and what can I expect?

My name is Varun Choraria (Website here), but in the startup world, I’m known as vc. I'm 24 years old, and one philosophy that I think will allow me to lead a successful life is to constantly update my perspective on things. If you teach a man how to build a product/startup, he will be temporarily happy. 

But, if you teach a man how to think, he will dominate the 21st century.

So, with every newsletter, here’s what you can expect:

  1. Featured Story: One story, 8-9 minutes reading time, around life, career, startups and product.

  2. Featured Discussions: Our community members constantly share new insights and we’ll feature some of the most vibrant ideas from the past week. 

  3. Curated Content: Optimising for Signal-to-Noise ratio is something we believe in deeply. We’ll share some of our most insightful reads, tweets, videos, and podcasts you could wind down to over the weekend. 

You won’t have to worry about missing anything. Every new edition of the newsletter goes directly to your inbox.

Be a part of the community

Paras Chopra is the founder of Wingify aka VWO, and writes at invertedpassion.com. He’s an extreme generalist and tinkers a lot around understanding humans and systems from 0 to 1, and the focus of this newsletter and slack community is to bring you the best of that.

If you identify with being someone who leads life by curiosity, then you’ll find solace among the many able minds that have come to make this community happen.
