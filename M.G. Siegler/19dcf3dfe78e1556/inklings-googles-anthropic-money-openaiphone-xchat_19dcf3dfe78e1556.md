Thoughts On...💰 [Google's (Up To) $40B Anthropic Investment](https://spyglass.org/r/ef950e12?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – You know how I know that Anthropic is freaking out over being [compute-constrained](https://spyglass.org/r/71f31780?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)? In the past week, they've now [agreed](https://spyglass.org/r/5759ccb8?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) to sell (up to) $65B worth of equity to the investors which are already their two largest backers in Amazon and Google. And they're doing it – well, at least the first tranches – at the price of [the last funding round](https://spyglass.org/r/7e36c7ff?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), even though they have offers to raise at [far higher valuations](https://spyglass.org/r/dadaadad?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) already. This will mean Google has [around](https://spyglass.org/r/a3988329?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) $13B invested into Anthropic now, and up to $33B if milestones are hit. Amazon, meanwhile, [also has](https://spyglass.org/r/c973de92?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) $13B invested now, and up to... $33B. These numbers align so nicely that they don't seem like coincidences. But then again, Amazon has more money at lower entry prices, so their ownership stake should [still be higher](https://spyglass.org/r/e36c8241?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) than Google's. This would buy Amazon another 1.3% now and Google another 2.5%. [[NYT](https://spyglass.org/r/0366afdc?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)] 📱 [The OpenAiPhone?](https://spyglass.org/r/e5a31c18?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – First, are we sure this isn't a chip meant for another piece of OpenAI hardware? Obviously, Ming-Chi Kuo would check on that but still, the report is weird enough. Not because the idea is bad – [as I've stated before](https://spyglass.org/r/fb729d23?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), of course Amazon and everyone else would still want to control their own smartphone as it means controlling their own destiny. (This is why [I suspect](https://spyglass.org/r/6e81dace?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) Meta, the most annoyed about the Apple/Google stranglehold in the space, will try again at some point.) OpenAI, on the verge of [their own hardware](https://spyglass.org/r/7d1a8c2d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), would obviously want such [control](https://spyglass.org/r/4b38e3af?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) for [the same reasons](https://spyglass.org/r/38ff9808?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). The difference, of course, is that they're a "startup", one that's trying to [focus](https://spyglass.org/r/70d9d70b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) after years of "side quests". A smartphone would be the ultimate – and ultimately expensive – side quest. Maybe it's just something to explore if the true AI device – undoubtedly [tethered](https://spyglass.org/r/eadb9a50?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) to the iPhone – is a massive hit? Mainly I want to ensure we see that device, with so many billions yet to be burned on just model training and inference alone. [[@mingchikuo](https://spyglass.org/r/9b45ea8f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)] 💬 [XChat](https://spyglass.org/r/579b5f70?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – While I think I was asking for a stand-alone Twitter DM app in the past, that was at least [a dozen years ago](https://spyglass.org/r/a82645b0?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). I don't really understand why it exists now. There are way too many chat apps and this is seemingly just [a carbon-copy](https://spyglass.org/r/f2a1c77f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) of direct messages already within Xitter, but more buggy (I guess [more iOS-native](https://spyglass.org/r/601fbdec?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)). Further, Elon has long talked up the [notion](https://spyglass.org/r/1b045552?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) of X as the ["everything app"](https://spyglass.org/r/8230bd4a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) which seems more complicated when you need three or four apps for the everything app. Speaking of, [it sounds like](https://spyglass.org/r/a11b878b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) the X Money app is coming shortly too – though perhaps "a day late and dollar short". [Zero surprise there](https://spyglass.org/r/54cbc7fa?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). Porn-y names aside, this is like watching Meta's obsession with [bundling and unbundling](https://spyglass.org/r/775d3539?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) their apps, but in slow motion. Yes, XChat app hit #1 in the App Store for a day or so, but obviously you can do that on the back of the parent app with millions of users. The fact that it's fallen into the 20s just a couple days later seems like a bad sign. Who is this app for? Even the security folks are [skeptical](https://spyglass.org/r/46bb1140?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) of that angle.
