Thoughts On...💫 [OpenAI Missing Projections](https://spyglass.org/r/cc83f005?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – On one hand, you could read a startup missing internal projections as the company either not being great [at extrapolation](https://spyglass.org/r/0c4bd7bf?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) (more understandable during fast growth) or setting targets that are too optimistic (perhaps to help woo investors). These are internal numbers for a reason; they aren't held to the same level of scrutiny as, say, those of a public company. On the other hand, OpenAI is an awfully mature company at this point having raised more money than any other in history, with an experienced team, and one that is obviously trying to go public sometime soon. Plus, as far as we know, they haven't missed on such projections in the past, so... It's not a great look, but it's made worse by [their response](https://spyglass.org/r/2e993f0e?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) which includes the highly scientific measurements such as "firing on all cylinders" while calling the report "prime clickbait" – both of which, of course, are non-denial denials to a report, which has now been [backed-up](https://spyglass.org/r/d8b1d91b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). Even the partners, whose stocks are getting hit by proxy at the moment can't seem to align on the right rebuttals here. The actual actions of the company perhaps remain more indicative here – [killing](https://spyglass.org/r/1eecf3e4?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) ["side quests"](https://spyglass.org/r/d65cf539?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), altering deals to[ expand their market](https://spyglass.org/r/b2292804?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), and yes, bringing on [some help](https://spyglass.org/r/6c003ef1?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) in the marketing/PR front. [[WSJ](https://spyglass.org/r/d16a96bd?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) 🔒] 💨 [Amazon's 'Quick' Superagent](https://spyglass.org/r/a316da71?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – The branding for their [desktop-based AI assistant](https://spyglass.org/r/f60e601a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) isn't bad, but confusing. 'Amazon Quick' definitely sounds like a fast delivery offering, not their version of Claude Cowork. Then again, 'Q' [didn't really work](https://spyglass.org/r/af8dd1b0?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) for them – and was weird for [other](https://spyglass.org/r/59a25b53?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) reasons – so I guess just add some letters? More importantly, Amazon doesn't have the best track record when it comes to software. Almost all of it whether on the web, desktop, or mobile, quite frankly, sucks. So the hope will have to be that their approach – multiple models, obviously their own 'Nova' models and [presumably](https://spyglass.org/r/8f35b5d7?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) Claude, but presumably not GPT, [at least](https://spyglass.org/r/f8cea626?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) [not](https://spyglass.org/r/ca8bc3c4?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) [yet](https://spyglass.org/r/b4d04819?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – and their AWS connections will win the day. For $20/month, it better be fast. [[Information](https://spyglass.org/r/b96b0609?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) 🔒] [Cost-Per-Click AI Ads](https://spyglass.org/r/05b67ea7?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – It feels like OpenAI has little choice but to go down this path already for ChatGPT – they need to start showing results to advertisers and this is the way you traditionally show results. But [I'm still quite skeptical](https://spyglass.org/r/cf1e6078?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) this model will work all that well in AI. There's a very fundamental problem: such AI services, at least to date, have removed most of the need to click. That's how they've been [disrupting Google](https://spyglass.org/r/7583abe9?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), of course. With web search, users are trained to click. With AI, users are trained to not need to click. So, yeah. Good luck getting to [that $100B+](https://spyglass.org/r/f21a4265?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) advertising business – which would be the overall [biggest driver](https://spyglass.org/r/34c2c6b3?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) of the business at that point – in a few years this way. I just think they'll have to find a new model and methods for advertising in AI. Just like Google had to do for Search and Meta had to do for the feed. [[Digiday](https://spyglass.org/r/ec57b2df?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)] I Quote..."Stargate has now had three or four permutations, I don’t know what it is right now. I can’t tell you. Maybe it never really existed in the first place."
– An unnamed "someone familiar with Microsoft's thinking" which, of course, sounds a lot like someone who works at Microsoft and is undoubtedly chuckling [right now](https://spyglass.org/r/72daf90a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) over "Project Stargate". As it, of course, [actually started](https://spyglass.org/r/1b027855?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) life as an initiative between OpenAI and Microsoft, before "The Blip" derailed, well, pretty much everything [between the two](https://spyglass.org/r/23f965a5?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). Soon, Stargate was [reborn/repackaged](https://spyglass.org/r/26012bac?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) as a $500B mega-project unveiled at The White House between OpenAI, SoftBank, and Oracle. Why? Because obviously OpenAI as a massively unprofitable startup [couldn't](https://spyglass.org/r/af14976d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) raise the debt required to [finance](https://spyglass.org/r/073a724d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) such a project on their own. Yet they probably [needed to](https://spyglass.org/r/4e75ec9e?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) in order to [ultimately](https://spyglass.org/r/e0aa3991?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) compete with Google. But, well, the best laid plans and all that – which, to be clear, [this was not](https://spyglass.org/r/b662c51e?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). Now we have Microsoft [picking off Stargate scraps](https://spyglass.org/r/ccfed724?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) and "Stargate" itself [morphed](https://spyglass.org/r/9abdfe89?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) into just a catch-all PR term for OpenAI data center deals. "I don't know what 'Stargate' means at this point," says another "involved" buy unnamed source. [So say we all](https://spyglass.org/r/b21ddb4c?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c).
