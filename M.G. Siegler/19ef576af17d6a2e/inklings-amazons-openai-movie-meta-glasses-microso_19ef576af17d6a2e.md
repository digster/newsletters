Thoughts On...😎 [The 'Meta Glasses'](https://spyglass.org/r/2dfd4e30?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – I think their strategy is smart here. Meta started with a known/proven brand and style in Ray-Ban (and later Oakley) and are now branching out on their own (still with the help of EssilorLuxottica – in which Meta is now a large shareholder, remember). And they don't have Meta's dumb, [droopy](https://spyglass.org/r/00b48363?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) logo front-and-center (which must have been tempting given that the logo looks like goggles), but rather hidden on the back of the stems. And the glasses look... good? Pretty standard stuff. But one suspects it's easy to get this wrong given that they're front-and-center on your face. The most important aspect, of course, is what it means for the price: $299 – a full $80 cheaper than the latest Ray-Ban branded variety. It's a solid price point and a further poke in the eye of their old rival Snap, as this makes their um, $2,195, price point for Specs seem [all the more untenable](https://spyglass.org/r/9a5d7787?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). Yes, they're different – one is pretty full-on AR while the other are just glasses with AI – but my point would be that we're not ready for the AR variety yet anyway so... releasing the Specs at that price point just looks like a sort of silly move to many, including Wall Street. Anyway, the "Kylie" variety of Meta's glasses are obviously not meant for me, but I'm genuinely curious how well (or not) those sell in the market. Meta loves to try to get celebrities involved in their products (I mean, so do all the tech companies with marketing teams) but it [doesn't ever seem to work](https://spyglass.org/r/a0be3fe9?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) as [they'd hope](https://spyglass.org/r/4dbb0417?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). But hey, they have a gemstone here. And yes, her AI voice. Will their ['Muse Spark' models](https://spyglass.org/r/0698668b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) be fully up to the task? Can Meta fully [escape](https://spyglass.org/r/2c7b78c5?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) their brand baggage? The "[glasshole](https://spyglass.org/r/b783c6f2?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)" issue? [Your move, Apple](https://spyglass.org/r/ee0f53ba?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). [[Wired](https://spyglass.org/r/e307208e?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) 🔒] ☁️ [Microsoft’s Hybrid AI Dreams](https://spyglass.org/r/1e916cb0?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) — The strangest thing about Satya Nadella’s [ongoing blitz](https://spyglass.org/r/5d1b71d1?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) against Big AI — well, beyond the fact that Microsoft is deeply partnered with all the players, and yes, owns 25%+ of OpenAI — is that they just started [going full bore](https://spyglass.org/r/8b3db166?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) at [creating their own](https://spyglass.org/r/d6592385?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) frontier models. Is the goal not to get the most amount of people to use those? Or perhaps it’s an acknowledgement that [no matter the work](https://spyglass.org/r/a0a7d9e3?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) they may do at the frontier, they’re not going to be able to catch Anthropic and OpenAI at this point. Or perhaps it’s simply Nadella reading the room and tea leaves, thinking that [the ‘Fable’ situation](https://spyglass.org/r/9d8cf105?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) is another [DeepSeek moment](https://spyglass.org/r/64a958c8?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) where it causes everyone to rethink how they’re working on AI. An acknowledgment that Anthropic’s [tussle(s)](https://spyglass.org/r/3497410a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) with the US government is causing [many to think](https://spyglass.org/r/0811dfec?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) that they shouldn’t be all-in with one AI provider — even if it’s one of the Big Tech players. That instead, they should seek a diversified provider of models, like, say, Microsoft. Which, by the way, is happy to offer you DeepSeek models! Why? [Costs](https://spyglass.org/r/4632cbf7?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). The other elephant in this room Nadella may be reading. Even if Microsoft can catch up on the frontier, their customers may not want to pay for such compute — certainly not for everything. Here, open source models are their friend, even if they may have been distilled from their partner OpenAI… [[WSJ](https://spyglass.org/r/4e61cbb3?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) 🔒] 📉 [Is Google Behind In AI? Again?!](https://spyglass.org/r/d16982d2?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) — Speaking of, at least Wall Street seems concerned that the departure of both Noam Shazeer — [not even two years](https://spyglass.org/r/9a8928a8?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) after bringing him back on board for $2.7B, now bolting [for rival OpenAI](https://spyglass.org/r/33c761e7?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) — and now their Nobel Prize-winning DeepMinder John Jumper — who is John jumping to rival Anthropic — signals a problem within Google’s AI ranks. Again. That mixed with the fact that their latest Gemini flagship models weren't ready for [their I/O conference](https://spyglass.org/r/6e1d3d9f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), and still have yet to be released, and whispers that when they are, they still won’t be Mythos/Fable [caliber](https://spyglass.org/r/34660dc1?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)… It’s obviously hard to know exactly what is [going on here](https://spyglass.org/r/93e33b1c?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) until those models are out in the world but there are definitely some Meta Llama vibes out there at the moment. But if Nadella’s read per the above note is correct, how much should Google even care about being at the absolute bleeding edge? It’s optically a black eye, obviously when a company with [basically infinite resources](https://spyglass.org/r/8ef4bb38?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) can’t compete with the startups. But those have their own advantages too in [being more nimble](https://spyglass.org/r/8ec5b6c5?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) and not being bogged down [by the baggage](https://spyglass.org/r/c810d8cd?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) of billions of users across a wide range of products in a wide range of industries. But if costs start to matter more and there’s a push for more hybrid AI systems… Google should be fine. [Just like Apple](https://spyglass.org/r/ba3e0fec?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), which of course fumbled the AI bag far worse! But it is a weird vibe! [[Bloomberg](https://spyglass.org/r/c8cbfc1c?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) 🔒] 👁️ [OpenAI Takes Their Ads Pitch to the Ads Pitchers](https://spyglass.org/r/67c0e96a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) — Increasingly, this does [feel](https://spyglass.org/r/e656dcc1?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) like the single-most important thing for OpenAI at the moment. If they can jumpstart their ads business, they suddenly have a narrative that Anthropic can't — [because they won’t](https://spyglass.org/r/25c427f9?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) — match. It won’t totally negate [the fact that](https://spyglass.org/r/b4435304?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) Anthropic’s top and bottom lines now look more attractive, but given ChatGPT’s scale, it can be compelling. If the ads work, that is. And the jury is [still very much out there](https://spyglass.org/r/a39ea648?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). I’m [still of the mind](https://spyglass.org/r/61159ccb?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) that they need to come up with new formats that are AI-native as CPC and CPM models don’t seem to make much [sense](https://spyglass.org/r/36f20b88?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), at least with the way the chatbots are currently built and serve up information. For queries with commerce intent, you can probably see it — and OpenAI says 1/5th of the queries they see are in that vein? — but shopping has already had fits and starts for them. The fact that Google is already working on this too is actually good news for OpenAI. If anyone can figure it out… But the bigger concern may remain that OpenAI [clearly](https://spyglass.org/r/62a224d4?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) wants to be in the business that Anthropic is currently dominating and has [diverted](https://spyglass.org/r/432be831?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) most resources in [that direction](https://spyglass.org/r/3af88d0d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). Can they really do both here? [[FT](https://spyglass.org/r/72ce6538?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) 🔒] 🕹️ [The Chip War Could Kill Consoles](https://spyglass.org/r/f0a1340b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) — Valves new Steam Machine [sounds (and looks)](https://spyglass.org/r/72d9980e?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) interesting. As with the Steam Deck, it feels like they’re one step ahead of Microsoft. But the problem, of course, is price. It starts at $1,049 and goes all the way up to just under $1,500. The latter is double the highest end Xbox price. It’s triple the Switch 2 price. Yes, it’s a PC too, but if you wanted to use it as a PC, you should… buy a PC? The whole value proposition is the ease of use and access to gaming in the living room, but it’s not exactly plug-and-play — [at least not yet](https://spyglass.org/r/e92f09f9?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). And it’s apparently no more powerful than a PS5 — which is [over 5 years old](https://spyglass.org/r/9b31a9d4?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) (that itself is sort of wild). Anyway, it points to the bigger issue in the console market itself which is under siege due to the component prices. That basically makes any PS6 or ‘Project Helix’ Xbox a non-starter anytime soon. [Xbox may really want](https://spyglass.org/r/d9a562eb?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) to get back to being Xbox, but it’s [going to be tough](https://spyglass.org/r/8e2a8ec8?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) if the next Xbox is $1,000 or more.
