Thoughts On...💸 [OpenAI's IPO Hedge](https://spyglass.org/r/13315cf9?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – Beyond [the obvious optics](https://spyglass.org/r/225f0fdb?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) of leaking that you're going to file to go public right before your hated rival flips their S-1 public, assuming OpenAI does actually (confidentially) file today or early next week, it's clearly a maneuver to stay ahead of a would-be Anthropic listing. And a necessary one. If Anthropic goes out first, assuming their better growth and better margins hold, OpenAI's IPO is in a lot of trouble. The comp would have already been challenging from a bottom line perspective, [as I detailed last year](https://spyglass.org/r/aebafa35?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), but [now that](https://spyglass.org/r/098fe23e?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) Anthropic has [seemingly surged ahead](https://spyglass.org/r/373e51fe?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) in top line too... yeah. So the race is on. But as Sam Altman's comments to staff make clear, it's [not a sure thing](https://spyglass.org/r/db4066d0?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) they're going to go public this year – [many](https://spyglass.org/r/d431e89b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) elements will factor in, including [how SpaceX is received](https://spyglass.org/r/923e1c2d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), overall macro sentiment, [oh yes](https://spyglass.org/r/17c72233?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), and how [their actual business](https://spyglass.org/r/d6ca4c10?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) is doing – but this clearly gives them optionality just in case they need to beat Anthropic to market (or, perhaps, if it looks like the market is about to [collapse](https://spyglass.org/r/ebc25b80?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) which could slam the IPO window shut for a long time, and as such, limit access to continued capital, which obviously [would be very bad for OpenAI](https://spyglass.org/r/790223fe?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)). [[Information](https://spyglass.org/r/b79a814d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) 🔒] 🎞️ [IMAX Explores a Sale](https://spyglass.org/r/17eb12f9?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – Last summer, I had a new idea for Skydance following their purchase of Paramount: [buying IMAX](https://spyglass.org/r/25b9e62b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). As it turns out, they [took my](https://spyglass.org/r/08f301c0?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) older, [much larger idea](https://spyglass.org/r/6a737214?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), and bought Warner Bros Discovery instead. Still, given IMAX's relative price ($1.8B market cap before the pop on today's news), it could makes sense. Or it could make sense for the company that Paramount just paid a $3B breakup fee to in order to secure WBD. You know, the one that [suddenly](https://spyglass.org/r/9c60d237?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) and [surprisingly](https://spyglass.org/r/01c78a90?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) ([except to me](https://spyglass.org/r/bf0e402e?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)) loves movie theaters: Netflix. Certainly selling to someone right now makes some sense for IMAX, they're [continuing to](https://spyglass.org/r/fac831b4?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) eat into [box office share](https://spyglass.org/r/ebdaf395?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) and 2026 is [likely to be](https://spyglass.org/r/32ddbb04?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) the best year for movies in quite some time (and perhaps for quite time after that). Sell high, as it were. [[WSJ](https://spyglass.org/r/8e7105f1?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) 🔒] 👶 [Can Baby Yoda Save Star Wars?](https://spyglass.org/r/90e30a1c?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – Speaking of the box office. Sadly, signs are pointing towards "no" here as we head into the holiday weekend. An opening in the $80M range would be great for pretty much any movie, but sadly a Star Wars movie is not one of them. For context, Solo did $103M over the same weekend 8 years ago – and that movie was considered a disaster for Disney ([it was fine](https://spyglass.org/r/0b2f3722?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), [but unnecessary](https://spyglass.org/r/7a304a44?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)). Also, $103M is almost $140M in 2026 dollars. A bigger disaster for Disney (though not at the box office, but perception-wise) was what came next: The Rise of Skywalker. The last of the last trilogy and the last Star Wars movie we've seen until now [was a retconning mess](https://spyglass.org/r/e185a62c?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). Forget pent-up demand, it's wild that Disney let that after-taste linger [all this time](https://spyglass.org/r/8565d4a9?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). And it's just going to be a hard sell trying to cleanse the palate while also trying to translate a Star Wars movie from streaming to the big screen for the first time. Anyway, [Kathleen Kennedy is gone](https://spyglass.org/r/3c536ed1?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) (though this is still obviously her Baby Yoda) and Dave Filoni is in place; a good transition as [he co-wrote this film](https://spyglass.org/r/4c93c665?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). His animated work has been generally excellent (including the current Maul!) but it's also a bit [too in-the-weeds](https://spyglass.org/r/ee71abfe?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) for [the mass audience](https://spyglass.org/r/fa69d320?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). Can he keep Star Wars [truly mainstream](https://spyglass.org/r/4d6c8378?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)? Next summer's Starfighter – well-timed for the 50th anniversary of the original movie, seems to have a [decidedly](https://spyglass.org/r/739bdbda?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) better vibe around it. Could it be the start of a new trilogy? [Something else](https://spyglass.org/r/1c46ba1d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)? [Moar](https://spyglass.org/r/21501404?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) Andor! Filoni ([and Josh D'Amaro](https://spyglass.org/r/6bff3dee?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)) is going to need to tackle it [at some point](https://spyglass.org/r/a6eb93b5?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)...
