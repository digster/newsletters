I Note...

🦸🏻‍♂️ OpenAI's "Super App" [https://spyglass.org/r/205d3b24?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] – An interesting side note of this strategy: it shifts us back from a mobile-first and focused world, to a desktop-centric one. That's not surprising given the coding focus of Codex (and Claude Code), but it's decidedly not where the masses are these days, and ChatGPT is at such a scale – 1B actives any day now – that mobile will presumably always end up as the most-used surface for them (even if/when [https://spyglass.org/r/68b342c1?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] their own devices hit [https://spyglass.org/r/25abbe18?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]). The strategy – get Codex in front of non-engineers through ChatGPT and morph it into a general purpose agent – isn't a bad one, but there are real risks [https://spyglass.org/r/08910435?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] in execution. And given that OpenAI is full-on chasing Anthropic here, what might they be focusing on – shoring up Claude Code/Cowork or something new? [Sources [https://spyglass.org/r/257c8233?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] 🔒]

🔀 OpenAI Shuffles Leadership [https://spyglass.org/r/a7e2da0a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] – Holy Good Friday news dump. COO shifting to "special projects". CMO stepping down (to focus on health). CEO (of AGI Development) taking a leave of absence (to focus on health). This all comes on the heels of the $122B fundraise [https://spyglass.org/r/4a7fe074?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c], a questionable M&A deal [https://spyglass.org/r/f10e5fa0?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c], and a mandate to kill off "side quests" and focus [https://spyglass.org/r/6bb1bf10?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] on the task at hand: taking on Anthropic. The latter two were spearheaded by aforementioned CEO of AGI (now on leave) Fidji Simo. Clearly they waited to announce all of this until the Friday before Easter. Will Simo come back as COO (since it seemed like [https://spyglass.org/r/e8d994f0?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] that's what she essentially was anyway?). The company is saying no (that they won't appoint a new COO). Hard to give up a CEO title, I guess – even a secondary one [https://spyglass.org/r/943b05eb?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. Never a dull moment at OpenAI. [Bloomberg [https://spyglass.org/r/f66d995e?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] 🔒]

📺 YouTube Leans Back [https://spyglass.org/r/8c0f478e?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] – While "Stations" are essentially YouTube's answer to FAST [https://spyglass.org/r/3106db9f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] channels elsewhere, it has nothing to do with price – since, of course, YouTube is already free (though you definitely should consider YouTube Premium to remove the insane amount of ads if you watch a lot of YouTube). It's all about the experience of just being able to put something on and not worry about it. Yes, like old school TV or cable. Not having to select something else when it ends. Just putting something on in the background and letting it wash over you. Endlessly. For hours. Sometimes, people simply like not feeling alone in their homes. I feel like I've been writing about this notion [https://spyglass.org/r/899ccc10?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] for a decade. Because I have [https://spyglass.org/r/3a41fbc4?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. [Verge [https://spyglass.org/r/64d9dd68?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]]
