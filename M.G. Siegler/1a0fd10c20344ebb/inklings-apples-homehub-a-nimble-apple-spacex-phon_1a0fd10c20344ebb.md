I Note...🍎 [A More Nimble Apple](https://spyglass.org/r/0ae87d3b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – While Apple has had some layoffs here and there over the past few years, the reality is that unlike their Big Tech peers, they haven't done the big shakeup, both post-COVID and now to reorient for the Age of AI. As unfortunate as it is for those losing jobs, this is the most obvious and [natural time](https://spyglass.org/r/bd5dcb3d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) for Apple to do this [with](https://spyglass.org/r/fef7bb61?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) John Ternus [taking charge](https://spyglass.org/r/50176662?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). That said, Apple has never operated like the other Big Tech companies, so the most interesting element will be if they can actually operate at a more nimble cadence, moving away from once or twice-a-year [big launches](https://spyglass.org/r/2b51b32b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) and more towards constant iteration. The body may well reject such organs! But yeah, you sort of have to try if you truly believe AI is going to fundamentally alter the way business is done.
