I Note...✨ [The Gemini Mac App](https://spyglass.org/r/6e1bb7e9?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – Pretty [barebones](https://spyglass.org/r/5fc0f847?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), but still nice to have. And necessary to have for Google, if they truly hope to rival ChatGPT and now Claude, which is rising fast not just in the coding world, but in the day-to-day of my life. A tab on the web just isn't good enough. While [baking Gemini directly into Chrome](https://spyglass.org/r/7df16049?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) may be (and you have to imagine becomes problematic for regulators at some point), there are a few people here and there that don't use Chrome day-to-day ([including me](https://spyglass.org/r/21401db2?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)). One fun/funny bit: Google opted to use option+space as the default keyboard short cut to launch Gemini, which is interesting since it's the exact same shortcut that OpenAI uses for ChatGPT (Anthropic goes with double-tapping option for Claude, and Apple itself uses command+space for Spotlight and double-tapping command for Siri). That doesn't not seem like a coincidence. As OpenAI looks to [bring more into](https://spyglass.org/r/18c6058d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) ChatGPT to [make it a "super app"](https://spyglass.org/r/8f8a02af?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) what might Google layer in here? One idea: a native Gmail client. How killer would that be – especially [if email is going to be](https://spyglass.org/r/19eeab39?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) one [key agentic use case](https://spyglass.org/r/66fbb990?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)... [[9to5Google](https://spyglass.org/r/b997c3d3?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)] 🎬 [Reed Hastings (Fully) Leaving Netflix](https://spyglass.org/r/548b58c4?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – End of an era, but also not surprising. In terms of exits, this is about as graceful as you will see, with a long goodbye in the form of board chair role. (Undoubtedly what Apple would like to copy [for Tim Cook soon enough](https://spyglass.org/r/aec6487b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c).) By contrast, Bob Iger's final farewell from Disney – which, to be fair, was far more prolonged for [other reasons](https://spyglass.org/r/f66e3bdd?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – will be much more abrupt at the end of this year. It sure feels like Netflix is Ted Sarandos' company now. Yes, technically Greg Peters is co-CEO, but as the [failed](https://spyglass.org/r/07b65881?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) Warner Bros deal made clear, there's a sort of Sith master/apprentice situation going on. And Hastings clearly [wasn't exactly gung-ho](https://spyglass.org/r/5fbc8d94?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) about the prospects of the Warner deal, but largely swallowed his tongue and ego to be a good board member for Sarandos. Now he won't have to do that anymore. And I look forward to his thoughts on future Netflix endeavors!
