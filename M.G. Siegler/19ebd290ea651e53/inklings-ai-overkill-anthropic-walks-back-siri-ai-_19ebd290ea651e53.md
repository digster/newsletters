Thoughts On...😈 [Anthropic Walks Back Their Sabotage Strategy](https://spyglass.org/r/24e19d1a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – Because we can't go a week without an [AI strategy and comms crisis](https://spyglass.org/r/374f8ab8?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), after some [major pushback](https://spyglass.org/r/7313dfb0?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), Anthropic has decided that, you know what, maybe they will tell those trying to use their new Fable model for their own training purposes that they can't do that – rather than just quietly undermining their code! Look, we get that they don't want rivals (and potentially bad actors) trying to distill their work, but who thought this strategy would work for a company that is trying to grab the ethical high ground and help with [AI's trust issues](https://spyglass.org/r/2c50b8ca?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)?! Now that Anthropic is seen as the AI front-runner, we should and they should expect [even more scrutiny](https://spyglass.org/r/fef75243?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) over some of their um, [interesting](https://spyglass.org/r/993f4600?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) [strategies](https://spyglass.org/r/61e10d65?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) and [comms blunders](https://spyglass.org/r/4a74d044?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). Upside: I now have [some Beastie Boys](https://spyglass.org/r/6ce075de?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) stuck in my head. And now you do too. [[Wired](https://spyglass.org/r/1ae7a35f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) 🔒] 🗣️ [Siri AI Actually Seems To Work](https://spyglass.org/r/3bd7bde7?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – The early reviews are in! Well, previews. This is just the first of many betas before a roll out – which will still be in beta – in the Fall. But these [first impressions](https://spyglass.org/r/02e9229d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) seem pretty universally positive across the board. Clearly, the bar was low – perhaps so low so as to be impossible to go under. Most takes seem to be "well, this is simple" or "yeah, other systems have been able to do this for a while" but still... [it works](https://spyglass.org/r/19cb2a3f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)! I have Siri AI on two systems currently, a backup MacBook and an old iPad. It's a bit buggy (again, as you'd expect) and slightly slow on the iPad which is several years old. But still, it works! You just couldn't say that really about Siri, and not just in the past couple of years with Apple Intelligence [debacle](https://spyglass.org/r/02986698?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), but really since Siri first launched [15 years ago](https://spyglass.org/r/57a98eea?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). There will obviously be times still where she doesn't work or misfires – and we'll see how she scales – but the hits seem to happen far more often already. It's early, but I've been [impressed](https://spyglass.org/r/f21d1f81?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). If anything, she might be too cold? [[Verge](https://spyglass.org/r/aa756066?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)] 💎 [Elon's Diamond Hands](https://spyglass.org/r/415b55c0?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – A trillion dollars. It's an insane amount of money, obviously. But [as with Hollywood box office results](https://spyglass.org/r/e9a7c140?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), there's some caveats and context required. First and foremost, [Bill Gates](https://spyglass.org/r/96072c0a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) and [then Jeff Bezos](https://spyglass.org/r/00564c23?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) would have hit the number first if they had just held on to all of their shares in Microsoft and Amazon, respectively. Instead, they either sold off over time or in the case of Gates, famously gave most of it to charity alongside Warren Buffett. It's a very uncharitable way to look at things, quite literally. The divorces haven't helped either of course, but Musk has plenty of those too. The difference is that Musk has held (or bought up/been granted more shares) in each of his companies. Diversification is for wimps, [one might say](https://spyglass.org/r/77c844fb?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). Actually, Musk's method of diversification is to start new companies. Whereas almost all of his net worth used to be tied to Tesla, with today's IPO and $2T+ valuation, his 50% stake in SpaceX now dwarfs his Tesla holdings (and he's obviously going to [merge them at some point anyway](https://spyglass.org/r/f35e13d7?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)). Bezos is perhaps learning this lesson with [his new](https://spyglass.org/r/7511d47d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) Prometheus AI startup, currently valued just under $30B [and rising fast](https://spyglass.org/r/a9ef3fb4?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). But really, while the piece notes that Musk's trillion would now trump John D. Rockefeller's billion back in 1937 in terms of share of US GDP (3% versus 1.5%), globally, Musk controls a mere 0.9% of GDP (honestly, that is maybe the most insane stat). Augustus Caesar controlled something more like 25% - 30% during his time atop the Roman Empire. [Mansa Musa](https://spyglass.org/r/2f65e85e?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) may have commanded more during the Mali Empire. Everyone needs goals to aspire to, even Musk. [[NYT](https://spyglass.org/r/c90ea44a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)] 📺 [Apple TV Really is the New (Old) HBO](https://spyglass.org/r/1808ef8f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) — From a quality-perspective, [I’ve been saying](https://spyglass.org/r/7fa92e9d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) this [for years](https://spyglass.org/r/b6af6a19?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) at this point, but Janko Roettgers' point here is a slightly different one: that Apple TV is relying on a few tentpole shows to drive their subscriptions and word-of-mouth, just like HBO used to do in the days of cable. But streaming has obviously changed everything, and as a result, Apple TV remains a non-player to the point of still not showing up on overall charts despite some of their high-profile content. [They started with no library](https://spyglass.org/r/ac424c46?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), of course, but years later, it’s still tiny compared to Netflix and the like. If and when they push into an ad-tier, that’s likely to change. They’ll start licensing more content on top of what they produce. This is inevitable from a [Services-perspective](https://spyglass.org/r/22361a9f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – [because ads are](https://spyglass.org/r/f87bbef1?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). Because while being the new HBO is nice and the content is for the most part great – big fan of their latest show, [Widows's Bay](https://spyglass.org/r/7dfc6070?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – it still isn’t moving many — [if any](https://spyglass.org/r/3dd64235?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) — needles. There’s some [halo effects](https://spyglass.org/r/7832636a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), for sure. But Apple is a $4T company. [[Lowpass](https://spyglass.org/r/86f7adb7?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)] I Wrote...I Quote..."The faster the potential RSI takeoff looks like it could be, the more it could be advantageous to delay an IPO. Technology and the world may change in surprising ways, and there might be good reasons to be a private company during that time."
– Sam Altman, [telling OpenAI employees](https://spyglass.org/r/cd7eccdf?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) why the company [may not rush to IPO](https://spyglass.org/r/9abe5e2a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) even with SpaceX now public and Anthropic on the path. There's been a lot written about [that race](https://spyglass.org/r/128fa21f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) and while I do think it would have been good for OpenAI to go out before Anthropic for all the [obvious optics and narrative reasons](https://spyglass.org/r/8e049738?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), with that ship [having sailed](https://spyglass.org/r/17b9bd25?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), it may actually make more sense to wait. Then the question becomes if they can afford to, quite literally. Given their burn, they'll need to raise money again before achieving profitability – yes, even after [the largest fundraise in history](https://spyglass.org/r/6340b6c3?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – and the public market is going to give them more avenues to do so. That said, there are tradeoffs, as Altman is alluding to. And given where OpenAI seems to be on the path to profitability versus Anthropic – which is to say, far behind – [there's a world](https://spyglass.org/r/f4abdd91?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) in which the market punishes OpenAI more than their peers. And that's not a good place to be. [Just ask Snap](https://spyglass.org/r/1f2d9a09?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c).
Lastly, it's interesting to see [the seemingly coordinated pivot](https://spyglass.org/r/c3308c45?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) around "RSI" – [recursive self-improvement](https://spyglass.org/r/e444b0c3?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – as the key talking point in terms of the next big thing in AI. Obviously, it points towards the path to AGI, but all of the labs clearly going to believe it's either the first step, the next step, or the major step. And coming soon – [earlier this week I noted](https://spyglass.org/r/80ef67a4?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) that OpenAI put a very specific date out there: March 2028. [Anthropic clearly thinks](https://spyglass.org/r/5bacfeb5?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) we're on the cusp. [Google](https://spyglass.org/r/f8132e5f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c).
