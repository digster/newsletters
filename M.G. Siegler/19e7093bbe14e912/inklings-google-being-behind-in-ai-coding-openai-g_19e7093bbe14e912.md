Thoughts On...🤖 [Google Admits Being Behind in AI Coding](https://spyglass.org/r/a66e7f68?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – A refreshingly straightforward answer from Sundar Pichai when asked by Kevin Roose and Casey Newton to assess where Gemini is with regard to the all-important coding capabilities just after this year's Google I/O keynote: "I think we are a bit behind at this moment." He seems sheepish in the entire interview which I would chalk up to [I/O being poorly timed](https://spyglass.org/r/a76224a8?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) with regard to announcing major breakthroughs – especially given that Anthropic is [now the one](https://spyglass.org/r/1fec7d02?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) keeping their peddle to the metal with Claude Opus 4.8 [out the door](https://spyglass.org/r/ec554f42?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), apparently even better at coding, and the first public-facing [Mythos models](https://spyglass.org/r/2fe673c7?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) approaching within ["weeks"](https://spyglass.org/r/b9336586?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). I would say that Google should go all-in on ["world models"](https://spyglass.org/r/d58fcd02?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) as Demis Hassabis clearly wants to do, but they also clearly believing the coding capabilities may be a key to unlocking something, be it agentic uses, [or RSI](https://spyglass.org/r/646f4b2d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), or AGI... [[NYT](https://spyglass.org/r/fc7f4e45?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)] 💸 [OpenAI Going After Mom & Pop Advertisers](https://spyglass.org/r/3dde2ffb?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – It feels like OpenAI is throwing basically everything against the wall when it comes to their ads business at the moment. What started as massive brand CPM buys is now getting extremely granular [with CPC](https://spyglass.org/r/df1fa1f1?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – working with local car washes? Obviously, they [need](https://spyglass.org/r/f3485353?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) this business to work both for their IPO aspirations but also because it's a major point of [differentiation](https://spyglass.org/r/75363d2a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) against Anthropic, which has said it will not go down the advertising path – or at least made fun of it to the point of such a backtrack being all but impossible anytime soon. It's the most obvious narrative battle that OpenAI can win and own in this race right now – if they can get it working. And that's a massive "if", because [I continue to believe](https://spyglass.org/r/f6aec28c?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) that they face a major click problem and that it will take some new kind of format/model to make advertising work at scale. The good news for OpenAI is that they're not alone here: Google needs this to work too, and could end up doing a lot of the heavy lifting to figure it out, which could pass down to all the other players, including OpenAI. [[Information](https://spyglass.org/r/01f19b06?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) 🔒] 💰 [Samsung's Bonus Situation](https://spyglass.org/r/c9da5ac1?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – Sort of a fascinating battle within the company at the moment on a few fronts. First and foremost, because of the massive success they're now seeing with their chips business – pushing them well [past the $1T market cap mark](https://spyglass.org/r/96a95283?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – that group's union has demanded a payout based on profits. The company quickly agreed, but it caused the other units to revolt even though they're not responsible for such profits and in some cases, are floundering. But the point of the say, [TV division](https://spyglass.org/r/b44067e4?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), is that for years, they were the ones carrying the company. It's just a wild situation that's playing out across [many Asian companies](https://spyglass.org/r/f4e1837b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), it seems. Without question, profit-sharing is compelling in principle, but the second-order effects are proving to be a nightmare! [[Reuters](https://spyglass.org/r/162d8da9?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)] 📽️ [Baby Yoda Slightly Scores](https://spyglass.org/r/fe1bb988?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – Better than the (clearly sandbagged) $80M [expectations](https://spyglass.org/r/bfd6ca00?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), Mandalorian and Grogu came in almost exactly where Solo did 8 years ago. Of course, inflation is a bitch here, so Solo's $103M is about $138M today. Still, Disney can work with this given that Mando cost about $100M less to make (again not including inflation – so far lower than Solo). Will it make money? Hard to say now (AI extrapolates it out as a coin-flip). As always, will depend on the second week hold – and there are a lot of movies coming out soon, most notably Masters of the Universe (decent enough buzz, [but I'm sort of](https://spyglass.org/r/a91f4515?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) [skeptical](https://spyglass.org/r/6d24de1f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)) and Disclosure Day (great buzz, presumably not just a Spielberg bump). [[NYT](https://spyglass.org/r/6c948d73?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)] 🍿 [Hollywood's Coming YouTube 'Obsession'](https://spyglass.org/r/f26fdd94?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – Related to the above, the big movie opening this coming weekend isn't Big IP or Big Director focused, it's [Backrooms](https://spyglass.org/r/e691d941?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), a horror movie made by [Kane Parsons](https://spyglass.org/r/88ee27bf?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), who got his start on YouTube and may now have a $50M+ opening weekend at the box office. And the 20-ish year old is hardly the only one charting that path now. Curry Barker, at the ripe old age of 26, is said to be getting 8-figure offers to produce his next movie after [Obsession](https://spyglass.org/r/0e83ff69?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) blew up at the box office. This whole wave is both great and interesting that it's happening right before AI was presumed to [do the same](https://spyglass.org/r/95c9e5bb?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – truly [democratizing Hollywood](https://spyglass.org/r/dc596f07?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). How long until YouTube tries to capitalize, quite literally, directly?
