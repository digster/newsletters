I Note...

💰 NVIDIA's Regains the Profit Crown [https://spyglass.org/r/b494fd4c?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] – While some headlines [https://spyglass.org/r/2ba95c8c?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] focused on the company closing in on $100B in revenue a quarter, plenty of companies do that – Walmart is nearly double that number, Amazon actually is. A far bigger deal is the $60B in profit NVIDIA just posted for the quarter. It has been bonkers [https://spyglass.org/r/f68e894c?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] for a while now, but it keeps getting more bonkers [https://spyglass.org/r/cdbc9687?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. And while Samsung briefly surged ahead [https://spyglass.org/r/4b7f826f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] last quarter due to the memory chip boom, these results puts NVIDIA back on top. And it's almost entirely actual profit, money NVIDIA gets to keep, not the silly [https://spyglass.org/r/0ae3acce?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] (GAAP) net income figure which includes moment-in-time paper gains on investments [https://spyglass.org/r/589fa732?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] – accounting which pushed Google's "profit" to a comically stated $112.2B last quarter. Their actual profit? Again, the money they keep? Closer to $30B. Roughly half of what NVIDIA just took home. [NYT [https://spyglass.org/r/3c271dc2?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]]

🤗 NVIDIA's $12.9B Hugging Face Deal [https://spyglass.org/r/1f88359b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] – Poolside [https://spyglass.org/r/f7780339?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. Perplexity [https://spyglass.org/r/ac283272?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. And now this. If you wanted a hint of how much profit NVIDIA was about to post, these deals – even if just rumored – were a good sign. I joked that NVIDIA clearly can't spend their cash quickly enough, but also, they clearly can't. Between bankrolling [https://spyglass.org/r/20643b42?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] and backstopping [https://spyglass.org/r/397cb955?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] data centers and investing [https://spyglass.org/r/bc00096a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] in seemingly every [https://spyglass.org/r/065b8af9?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] company even tangentially related to AI at the moment, NVIDIA is essentially a bank. Yes, so was Cisco back in the day, but I digress... This particular deal actually makes quite a bit of strategic sense. I'm not sure the price does but again, does that really matter when you're making $60B in profit a quarter? Not if there was competition for the deal and you could keep it out of the hands of others to further your "open" AI narrative [https://spyglass.org/r/5708845b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] – not to be confused with OpenAI, which is also tied closely [https://spyglass.org/r/437767a3?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] to NVIDIA, but is not open, and seems hell-bent on competing [https://spyglass.org/r/c7c3c088?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] with NVIDIA in chips with the apparently quite spicy 'Jalapeno' [https://spyglass.org/r/2264b27d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. Or maybe you better know them as the company which just [https://spyglass.org/r/9a73effe?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] inadvertently hacked [https://spyglass.org/r/75559ee6?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]... Hugging Face. Wild times. [Information [https://spyglass.org/r/6d4bbc90?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] 🔒]

🦆 Hugging Face's $400 Duck Robot [https://spyglass.org/r/992a465f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] – Speaking of... while I don't think the 'Microduck' is contributing much to the $13B price tag here, it's also pretty fun and clever! Given the focus on robotics as a next logical extension of AI, I'm surprised nearly everyone seems focused on either the humanoid [https://spyglass.org/r/8821b379?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] or warehouse variety. Why not just have a little fun while igniting some imaginations? Clearly they are over in Asia [https://spyglass.org/r/63363e96?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c], and Amazon tried this to some extent – but as always [https://spyglass.org/r/0e919efe?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c], they were too early – but I bet there's a growing market in the US now too for people who want [https://spyglass.org/r/50db642a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] a Star Wars droid-like device, not an Ultron or Terminator-like robot – at least not yet [https://spyglass.org/r/a8b6f7c4?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. Especially as voice AI [https://spyglass.org/r/76705ae4?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] keeps leaping forward [https://spyglass.org/r/d8d25360?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. Start small and fun [https://spyglass.org/r/96f01021?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. What's the old saying about big things that start out looking like toys [https://spyglass.org/r/016e0205?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]? What if they actually start out looking like toys? Apple should be [https://spyglass.org/r/95817a14?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] all over this [https://spyglass.org/r/0e6f9c67?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. If for no other reason than it now seems like their longtime nemesis [https://spyglass.org/r/49377161?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] NVIDIA will be! [Bloomberg [https://spyglass.org/r/5041a7ea?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] 🔒]

🐣 Meta's 'OpenClaw for Normies' Nears [https://spyglass.org/r/1be6e560?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] – I mentioned [https://spyglass.org/r/7f96ddae?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] 'Project Hatch' earlier today as Meta's big bet on productizing AI for consumers. AI "Personal Assistants" are so hot right now – at least in the eyes of VCs [https://spyglass.org/r/7db01137?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c], which, speaking from personal experience, at best doesn't mean much and at worst is negative signal. Everyone obviously wants [https://spyglass.org/r/8cdb5774?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] agentic AI to work in consumer [https://spyglass.org/r/0c0e3145?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c], but we need actual killer use cases beyond sorting your desktop icons or drafting email responses. Again, I would focus more on fun. What can these things do that is actually a delightful experience? Sure, easing the nightmare of booking travel sounds great, but, at least in my experience, that's also incredibly bespoke given various family requirements. Which is to say, I'm honestly not sure I'd ever trust AI to do that, even if it could. I realize that's a dangerous thing to say, but really I just want some obvious use cases for regular people and I have yet to hear any. Meta can shove [https://spyglass.org/r/7fa2b076?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] this in front of 3 billion people, but without those use cases, it won't matter [https://spyglass.org/r/c0993fb3?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. Certainly if Meta is really going to charge money for this [https://spyglass.org/r/acdb600a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. Though I will say, being able to leverage WhatsApp [https://spyglass.org/r/49278535?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c] as the potential UI here is interesting... Perhaps that's the real play for 'XChat' – Xitter's superflous chat app [https://spyglass.org/r/0d2c5b98?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]. Maybe it can be the Grok Bot interface? They just need to gain about 3 billion users first. [BI [https://spyglass.org/r/cb1187ae?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c]]
