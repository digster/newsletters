Thoughts On...🦞 [Meta Hatches an OpenClaw Competitor](https://spyglass.org/r/3f705b67?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – Once OpenClaw's founder [turned down](https://spyglass.org/r/72e71ce2?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) Zuck's offer, it was clear he would set out to boil the lobster alive. The fact that Peter Steinberger chose to join OpenAI just ensured it would be an all-you-can-eat buffet. [I had assumed](https://spyglass.org/r/5e20d3a2?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) Meta would [leverage Manus](https://spyglass.org/r/0b73ee8f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) to try to take on and out OpenClaw ([and they started](https://spyglass.org/r/8fa75e51?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) the work there) but, well, there are [some](https://spyglass.org/r/2817150e?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) [issues](https://spyglass.org/r/931bf951?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) with that now. [So we get 'Hatch'](https://spyglass.org/r/ef5e82bf?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), which apparently, much like early OpenClaw, is dependent on Claude at the moment, but the obvious aim is to shift it to [Meta's own Spark models](https://spyglass.org/r/48a3ebee?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). And yes, to build a sort of "OpenClaw for normal people" – [which makes sense](https://spyglass.org/r/f3bc1f85?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) and [was/is inevitable](https://spyglass.org/r/3d318068?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) to actually productize and scale such capabilities. Though Meta has their own unique challenges: "There’s a trust deficit as wide as the Grand Canyon," is the money quote in the piece as to why they may not be on better footing to build an agent with access to all of your data. To be clear, this is not [the ZuckBot](https://spyglass.org/r/8d34501a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). [[FT](https://spyglass.org/r/693c96df?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) 🔒] 🕹️ [A Quick Vibe Shift at Xbox](https://spyglass.org/r/ebdc6a89?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – Remember when everyone was [terrified](https://spyglass.org/r/26dcf5db?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) that Asha Sharma, the new CEO of Xbox, was sent as an agent from the AI group (not to be confused with an actual AI agent) by Microsoft leadership to destroy the gaming division once and for all? You should, it was a whole two months ago. [I took the other side](https://spyglass.org/r/7ecc5646?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), thinking that getting a fresh face in there would actually help the division. And while he AI background was interesting, it perhaps spoke more to her being good at being in the right place at the right time and adaptable to new roles and ideas. And, well... it's still early but [the early reviews](https://spyglass.org/r/bdae02d0?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) [are now](https://spyglass.org/r/e5e89150?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) [a 180](https://spyglass.org/r/76065ed2?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) as Sharma puts her own team (of not just non-Xbox people, but people who, like her, haven't been at Microsoft itself for long) in place. They're wasting no time with big changes [from Game Pass](https://spyglass.org/r/996c0ed5?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) to [overall branding](https://spyglass.org/r/2f27e707?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). Again, it's early, but they're seemingly saying all the right things from Sharma on down about [a return](https://spyglass.org/r/2c5cd6d7?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) to actual gaming and taking actions like [ripping out the Copilot AI nonsense](https://spyglass.org/r/5a98b093?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c), which they just put in place. The division [remains](https://spyglass.org/r/a86bf71a?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) a fiscal mess, so more hard decisions are undoubtedly to come, but still... this is a good start? [[CNBC](https://spyglass.org/r/216d09bd?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)] 🦾 [Apple's Choose-Your-Own-AI Adventure](https://spyglass.org/r/7786f86e?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – Not [a surprise](https://spyglass.org/r/48b5f688?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) that Apple is going to let you pick-and-choose which AI service you wish to use via "extensions" – this is just an, um extension of what [they were doing already](https://spyglass.org/r/da268736?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) with ChatGPT from two years ago. But presumably they're going to implement it differently so that it's far less buried. Then again, it's still presumably going to be the secondary AI to their own model ([now powered by Gemini](https://spyglass.org/r/d7953903?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)) – unless they let you change the default? This report doesn't say that explicitly, but how great would that be? You could [truly have](https://spyglass.org/r/0d865561?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) a ChatGPhone ([OpenAiPhone?](https://spyglass.org/r/0da199c5?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)) or a ClaudePhone. Regardless, it does sound like we'll be getting a much more malleable Siri, to the point where you can have different models using different voices, which would be great! And it might help Apple showcase when they don't specifically vouch for whatever the AI of your choice is coming back with (which was obviously long a fear in opening this up to third parties – get ready for disclaimers galore). One more thing: this approach should actually help Apple greatly in the EU, where they're [already looking into](https://spyglass.org/r/4b5db1d8?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) issues with [AI defaults](https://spyglass.org/r/a6be4d26?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) and Google... [[Bloomberg](https://spyglass.org/r/d738eb83?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) 🔒] 📽️ [AMC to Screen Live Concerts](https://spyglass.org/r/4b5493dd?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) – I mean, what took them so long? Movie theaters are in trouble for a whole host of reasons, but at the highest level, [it's a secular decline](https://spyglass.org/r/0fdded72?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). There is simply more to do and entertainment is far more accessible than it was in, say, 1950. I know Hollywood doesn't want to admit this, but it [doesn't make it not true](https://spyglass.org/r/a7e9a97b?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). At the same time, seeing movies in movie theaters – when those theaters are great, actual movie palaces and not dingy small rooms with [screen slightly larger](https://spyglass.org/r/51dc093d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) than your living room – [remains](https://spyglass.org/r/53264f81?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) [magical](https://spyglass.org/r/a1ad549f?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). To keep this industry alive, [new thoughts are needed](https://spyglass.org/r/f299a8b5?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c). And this means [breaking beyond](https://spyglass.org/r/bb124a5c?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) the showing every movie released several times a day, even when there are zero people in the audience model. So yeah, show live concerts. And theater as is done in the UK. And sporting events. And premieres/[finales](https://spyglass.org/r/dd354327?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) of television shows (the horror, I know)! I don't even mind [the higher prices](https://spyglass.org/r/8e6f51f9?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) as long as it's a good experience and driven by demand, not [propping up](https://spyglass.org/r/83037934?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) box office numbers! Do what it takes to keep the business viable – windowing is fine to an extent [but don't overdo it](https://spyglass.org/r/a9ab77a7?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) as it will not actually solve the secular issue – rather than pretending that one good year ([as this year clearly will be at the box office](https://spyglass.org/r/c45c5ced?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c)) signals [a return](https://spyglass.org/r/85738e6d?m=1ed23d55-8119-4b10-b903-392c96d1198c) of the industry.
