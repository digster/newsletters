[The Great Cousin Decline](https://substack.com/redirect/1473c29a-a541-4d70-92d7-1509beda3354?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by Faith Hill
American family trees are turning into beanstalks. Cousins were the lowest-stakes, highest-optionality relationship in the family. We’re about to find out what society looks like without them.
[How American Dads Became the Parents Their Fathers Never Were](https://substack.com/redirect/da4c67a8-176a-47b2-8f76-216c9ce72e7c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by[ Derek Thompson](https://substack.com/@derekthompson) and[ Aziz Sunderji](https://substack.com/@azizsunderji)
Millennial dads more than doubled the childcare time of their boomer fathers. The gap is widest among college-educated dads. Every other form of housework scales down with income but childcare scales up. Raising kids is now a luxury good.
[The Collapse of Terminal Value](https://substack.com/redirect/6edb9fb9-81b6-4486-b16e-b8cba954e7ce?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by[ Chamath Palihapitiya](https://substack.com/redirect/3c053101-83cf-487f-83a6-534feffb2c4d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o)
Every public company’s equity value is mostly terminal value. If AI raises the pace of disruption so no business can credibly project FCF past year 5, every stock prices at 5x FCF instead of 22x earnings -- a 75% drawdown on the entire S&P 500.
[The Curious Case of the Disappearing Lamborghinis](https://substack.com/redirect/fc36534c-76e7-4411-8847-ae6cf69d044c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by[ Craig Silverman](https://substack.com/redirect/92b0b5c7-6c19-4769-848d-fbffd1d3ae39?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o)
Roughly 8,000 luxury cars went missing in transit in the last 18 months. Over $1 billion in stolen Lamborghinis, Rolls-Royces, and Bentleys. Every time an industry shifts from phones and faxes to an online marketplace, fraud out-innovates the system.
[Perplexity chief business officer Dmitry Shevelenko](https://substack.com/redirect/75e544e2-2c32-4472-9f6f-7c5bfe8b2732?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) on [Summation](https://substack.com/redirect/d190915e-32f0-435c-96b4-495536227b36?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o)
Dmitry Shevelenko scaled Perplexity past $500M ARR with only 34% headcount growth, made a $34.5B bid for Google Chrome, and believes the binding constraint on AI right now is curiosity, not capability.
Bonus (college counselor): [if you can’t get a job in 2026, it’s your fault](https://substack.com/redirect/45cdf4ce-e629-4225-b43f-9ab53e8d94ae?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o)
