Population Decline Is Real. So Is the Reversal [ https://substack.com/redirect/88960c4b-dd10-419e-a58a-6ee260b5c89d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ]. [ https://substack.com/redirect/88960c4b-dd10-419e-a58a-6ee260b5c89d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ] by Fabrice Grinda
US households earning $500k+ now have more kids than households earning $100k. Kids are becoming a luxury good the same way lobster went from bait to delicacy. The fertility reversal is real, but Korea could hit replacement rate tomorrow and still shrink for 50 years.
Weapons of Mosquito Destruction [ https://substack.com/redirect/1b1bc851-c400-478b-b4d5-720862632c9d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ] by Tomas Pueyo
A CRISPR gene drive wiped out every mosquito in two lab cages in 2018. The first field test isn’t scheduled until 2030. Malaria kills 600k people a year and the bottleneck is no longer science. It is fear that the fix works too well.
How a Gang of Thieves Pulled Off a Multimillion-Dollar Data Center Heist [ https://substack.com/redirect/a586f4ba-4c62-41bd-b351-f6a3838905cd?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ] by Nathaniel Rich
In 2007 a crew dressed as cops talked their way into a Verizon data center and walked out with 80 servers in under an hour. The press reported that nothing valuable was taken. Data center heists are the rare crime where the victim helps with the cover-up.
Why Are Berries Everywhere, in Every Season? Driscoll’s. [ https://substack.com/redirect/42d56cf9-624b-40b5-a68a-953199fdb6c1?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ] by Julia Moskin
Driscoll’s is the second-highest-earning brand in American supermarkets, behind only Coca-Cola. Instead of owning the farms, it owns the genetics. A strawberry used to be a commodity and now it’s an IP business.
Helion CEO David Kirtley [ https://substack.com/redirect/2becaf9e-b914-457b-be68-0c9815933d2a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ] on Summation [ https://substack.com/redirect/e8408a74-f099-4389-8097-a436d235497f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ]
Helion has raised $1.5B at a $15.5B valuation, with Sam Altman as its biggest investor. David explains how Helion skips the steam turbine and pulls electricity straight out of a star, why a 150-million-degree machine is regulated like a hospital proton beam, and what happens when AI stops being limited by electricity.

Andre Agassi famously hated tennis. At 55 he’s beating everyone at pickleball and still can’t turn off the obsession: “I don’t understand enjoying something without caring about it.”
