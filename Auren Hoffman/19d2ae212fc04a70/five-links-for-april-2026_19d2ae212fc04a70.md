here are five links worth reading in April…
[Three Modes of Cognition](https://substack.com/redirect/2adb718a-3b6c-4a57-8190-ce05b35ba508?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by Kevin Kelley
There are three types of intelligence: knowledge reasoning, world sense, and continuous learning. Current AI has only conquered the first (thus far). AI will really take off when it cracks continuous learning.
[Why Gen Z is flocking to SEC universities](https://substack.com/redirect/d2e585f3-3355-4eb3-9f61-5db04cedd69b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by Kyndall Cunningham
Northeastern enrollment at SEC schools jumped 91% from 2014 to 2023. Alabama, Tennessee, and Ole Miss saw 500%+ increases from the Northeast. The driver is Gen Z picking schools like they pick brands.
[Make TVs Great Again](https://substack.com/redirect/03b7cf62-ce3e-4728-8fc1-f626ab658dfe?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by M.G. Siegler
As TVs became more important to daily life, the actual products got dramatically worse. M.G. makes the case for Apple to finally build a television.
[The World Is Full of GPS Dead Zones. Here’s What Comes Next.](https://substack.com/redirect/045e942a-1cdd-4d08-a141-7f0d4d79601b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by Christopher Mims
Everything from telecoms to banking to smart weapons depends on GPS and you can knock it out in a city block with a $500 device or across an entire city for $100k. Jamming is now widespread across Europe and the Middle East.
[Tod Sacerdoti (Flex GP and Pipedream CEO) on seed at scale and SaaS mispricing](https://substack.com/redirect/f73610b0-b705-45de-bd1b-b43f7b0b6518?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) on [Summation](https://substack.com/redirect/6881ea60-42a3-4336-9b0d-4ae41a57fafa?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o)
Tod sold BrightRoll to Yahoo for $640M, sold Pipedream to Workday, and has invested in 400+ companies. He explains why seed investing has the highest annualized returns of any venture asset class and why first-gen AI companies are the most vulnerable to AI disruption.
Bonus (SaaSpocalypse): [Long Slides, Short APIs](https://substack.com/redirect/084c6c4b-935f-49bb-a2d5-22ea52f9c3d9?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by [Alex Campbell](https://substack.com/@campbellramble)
A human generates a dozen API calls per hour. One AI agent generates hundreds per minute.
Bonus (promiscuous): [We are in polyamorous relationships with AI models (and why that means Perplexity wins)](https://substack.com/redirect/d9a61337-c100-487a-be57-e5a4baa82931?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o)
No one is monogamous with AI models anymore. This is a nightmare for enterprises and a massive opportunity for wrapper platforms (like Perplexity) that route to the best model automatically.
Bonus (useful): [Summation: Rules for Life & Work](https://substack.com/redirect/61839669-afc0-4c0e-9822-145ddce4c50d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by Sushant Bhardwaj
Being scammed a few times is a good sign. It means you trust people and stay open. My colleague Sush at Flex Capital read through my writing going back to 2003 and turned it into a short book you can open to any page.
