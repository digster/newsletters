[Why Am I Left-Handed?](https://substack.com/redirect/8ffa82dc-9d03-4c2f-9105-0f24524d3e12?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by Natalie Wolchover
10% of humans are left-handed and nobody knows why. The best guess: handedness was locked in during a phase of human evolution when swinging a weapon with your right hand let you aim for the heart on your opponent’s left side while protecting your own. The 90/10 split is a fossil from when we were still learning how to stab.
[This Simple White Line Is America’s Greatest Unsung Innovation](https://substack.com/redirect/ff07d5bb-60c0-44cc-b039-c37b8d9151bf?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by Ben Cohen
John Dorr spent his 80s crusading for a single white line on the right side of the road. The federal agency in charge of road safety spent years warning against edge lines because they might confuse drivers. The best innovations are cheap and get rejected for years by the exact people paid to notice them.
[Writing by Hand is Good for your Brain](https://substack.com/redirect/f8696244-7fa0-4fb9-b89f-464f52911e3e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) by Neal Stephenson
People think handwriting is a slower version of typing but it’s a different cognitive tool entirely. The friction of pen and paper forces you to think before you write and produce clearer ideas instead of faster words.
[Anthropic’s Felix Rieseberg](https://substack.com/redirect/913aa330-b876-47f0-ba8c-f37c120c0bec?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) on[ Summation](https://substack.com/redirect/edd38a68-504d-4905-85f4-cd382d65de1f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o)
Felix leads engineering for Claude Cowork and Claude Code Desktop at Anthropic. He gets into how Cowork was built in a couple of weeks and now mostly writes itself, the leap from telling AI to “fix this bug” to handing it a standing job and why “here’s what your team can learn from the Navy SEALs” is the worst advice in tech.
[A Real but Selective Crackdown on Cambodia’s Scam Industry](https://substack.com/redirect/6da7a176-c297-4dfe-af75-5888af80219f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) from The Economist
Cambodia extradited one of Asia’s most powerful criminal bosses to China in January. Within weeks tens of thousands of trafficked workers walked out of the scam compounds, gates left open by bosses who fled. The global scam industry is resting on a handful of protected men.
Bonus (useful):[ ](https://substack.com/redirect/06987d5c-6ccc-489b-91aa-20fb397efaa8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o)[you are introspecting too much](https://substack.com/redirect/06987d5c-6ccc-489b-91aa-20fb397efaa8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o)
Self-focused attention is one of the strongest correlates of depression and anxiety in the entire literature. The goal of introspection is a weekend job -- your strengths, your weaknesses, what you want. Everything past that is rumination the wellness industry keeps selling you more of.
