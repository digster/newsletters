** 20 Favorite Children’s Books from 20 Years of The Marginalian (https://www.themarginalian.org/2026/10/25/20-childrens-books/?mc_cid=d6d3a1f895&mc_eid=UNIQID)
------------------------------------------------------------

“Anyone who writes down to children is simply wasting his time,” E.B. White insisted (https://www.themarginalian.org/2015/02/26/e-b-white-writing-for-children/?mc_cid=d6d3a1f895&mc_eid=UNIQID) . “You have to write up, not down. Children are demanding. They are the most attentive, curious, eager, observant, sensitive, quick, and generally congenial readers on earth.”

Both as a reader of them and as a writer of them (http://themarginalian.org/books?mc_cid=d6d3a1f895&mc_eid=UNIQID) , I have never considered “children’s books” a separate category of literature: The best children’s books are works of philosophy in disguise, shining a sidewise gleam on the fundamental questions we live with, articulating in the language of children, which is the language of great simplicity and great profundity, the most complex truths about being human. But reducing a lifetime of reading to just twenty favorite books (https://www.themarginalian.org/2026/10/24/20-books/?mc_cid=d6d3a1f895&mc_eid=UNIQID) to mark twenty years of The Marginalian (https://www.youtube.com/live/qKd8UMjQ2Mo?mc_cid=d6d3a1f895&mc_eid=UNIQID) was so tortuously hard that I am sneaking in another twenty under the dubious label “children’s.”
