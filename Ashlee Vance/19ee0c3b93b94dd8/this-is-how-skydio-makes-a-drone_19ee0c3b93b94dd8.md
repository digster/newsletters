This week we take you inside the drone manufacturing operation of Skydio to see how they make their flagship X10 product. Skydio is the largest drone maker in the U.S. and produces its hardware right here in the great state of California. Does it make as many drones as China’s DJI? It most certainly does not. But does it make very sophisticated drones packed full of the best computer vision and AI technology? Yes, it does. In this episode, we show you what goes into making a drone step-by-step as part of our This Is series.
