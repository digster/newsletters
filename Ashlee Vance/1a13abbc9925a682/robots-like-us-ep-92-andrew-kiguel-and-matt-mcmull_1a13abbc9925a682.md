Our guests this week are Andrew Kiguel and Matt McMullen from Realbotix.
Realbotix makes humanoid robots that actually look like humans. McMullen, who founded the company, has spent years refining the animatronics and materials needed to create lifelike machines. His past creations have received tons of [media ](https://substack.com/redirect/3307a13c-596a-43d3-8ff8-7397de85f8ff?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o)attention both because they’re striking and because they veer into companionship and sex territory.
Lars and the Real Girl? Yep, that’s Matt’s work.
[Realbotix](https://substack.com/redirect/95621052-a0e1-43b8-9087-1041d0008292?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o), though, is an attempt to take the technology in a different direction. The company has made AI-infused bots that it wants to put to work out in the world. The bots could greet guests at hotels, provide information at hospitals or perhaps tutor students at schools. The Realbotix bots can carry on conversations and use vision systems to recognize people and take in their surrounding
We conducted this interview in Las Vegas where Realbotix has its headquarters. In a future video on our [YouTube ](https://substack.com/redirect/36a4b013-6e17-4039-8cdd-94f1f0c46bb1?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o)channel, which is awesome and you should subscribe to, I head out into Vegas with a bot named Aria, and we have a delightful afternoon together.
Some of you will think these robots are weird. And I get this.
I will, however, add that most of the humanoids out there are very boring. Companies have focused on what the robots can do rather than what they look like. The folks at Realbotix, by contrast, have put a lot of time and energy into trying to figure out how humans and robots will interact and progress together. We get into all of this on the show.
