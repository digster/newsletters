Apple chooses Amazon satellites for iPhone, years after rejecting Starlink offer (6 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Farstechnica.com%2Ftech-policy%2F2026%2F04%2Famazon-to-merge-with-globalstar-become-iphones-primary-satellite-provider%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019d90aa8cc1-c7cb8000-0172-47bc-9b10-56194ba9f3f2-000000/Zztm30miuXWxQW-4nVJompJKfoMz2WNytAz8g8Jg6KU=452)
Amazon has entered into an agreement with Apple to provide satellite service for its devices using the Amazon Leo network. Amazon recently signed a merger agreement to buy satellite operator Globalstar, which had already partnered with Apple for satellite messaging on the iPhone. Buying Globalstar will help Amazon enter the Direct-to-Device market, in which satellites provide connectivity to mobile phones. Apple is working on a framework for third-party apps to let developers add satellite connections to their apps.

Max Hodak's Science Corp. is preparing to place its first sensor in a human brain (5 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F04%2F14%2Fmax-hodaks-science-corp-is-preparing-to-place-its-first-sensor-in-a-human-brain%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019d90aa8cc1-c7cb8000-0172-47bc-9b10-56194ba9f3f2-000000/RC4poCzdaXpKSAsSmFMGBJIUqhNoOSYgfFJfKw6KTqk=452)
Science Corporation plans to start human trials for its biohybrid brain-computer interface. It has hired a top neurobiologist to surgically place a sensor for a future interface into a patient's brain. The sensor will be implanted into the skull, but rest on top of the brain. The final device will be embedded with lab-grown neurons that can be stimulated with pulses of light and are designed to naturally integrate with the neurons in a patient's brain.

Anthropic adds routines to redesigned Claude Code, here's how it works (2 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2F9to5mac.com%2F2026%2F04%2F14%2Fanthropic-adds-repeatable-routines-feature-to-claude-code-heres-how-it-works%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019d90aa8cc1-c7cb8000-0172-47bc-9b10-56194ba9f3f2-000000/jZsNNRcSJwht6lON36LJxaGGUFynBjUUa6fc4t5GmuY=452)
Anthropic's new repeatable routines feature for Claude Code runs tasks on Claude Code's web infrastructure, so users' Macs don't need to be online for each task. It is now available as a research preview. The Pro plan supports running 5 routines per day, the Max plan supports running 15 routines per day, and the Team/Enterprise plans support running 25 routines per day. Users can now run multiple Claude sessions side by side in a single window and manage them all from the sidebar.

Matt Mullenweg Says “The Wheels Have Fallen Off” in Wide-Ranging WordPress Critique (15 minute read)

Inside GitHub's Fake Star Economy (16 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fawesomeagents.ai%2Fnews%2Fgithub-fake-stars-investigation%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019d90aa8cc1-c7cb8000-0172-47bc-9b10-56194ba9f3f2-000000/rlW9H_0jSgNMz5Xf9RZMqYsK6nkDwX_1ERI_DfXPDZQ=452)
A study found 6 million fake stars across 18,617 repositories. AI/LLM repositories were the largest non-malicious category. There are at least a dozen websites selling stars for between $0.03 to $0.85 each. The FTC introduced a rule in 2024 banning fake social influence metrics with a penalty of $53,088 per violation. The SEC has already charged startup founders for inflating traction metrics during fundraising.
