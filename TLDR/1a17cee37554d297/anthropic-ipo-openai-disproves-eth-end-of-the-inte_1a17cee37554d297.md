The Biggest Takeaways From Anthropic's IPO Prospectus (8 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FuAgNbm/1/010001a17cee30c6-5d83ada9-0072-4696-b3da-8551d1fd868f-000000/dkPNZcLmPS0WVzXWRoRIa5Gqo-QtdubYbljIr4hMsGo=452)
Anthropic unveiled its IPO paperwork yesterday, putting it on track to go public as soon as the week before Thanksgiving. The 400-page prospectus tells a story of how the company could usher in a new era of progress that will transform how people code, conduct research, build financial models, and automate routine tasks. It also warned that AI could lead to the development of new bioweapons, destabilize the geopolitical order, and evolve beyond the ability of humans to understand and control. Anthropic plans to trade on the NASDAQ under the ticker 'ANT', with a valuation expected to land between $1.5 trillion and $2 trillion.

Working with academic communities to build scientific understanding (12 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FyowLBa/1/010001a17cee30c6-5d83ada9-0072-4696-b3da-8551d1fd868f-000000/lZmWK1MrGiX_DYdoNLQbDajhU5VTSa-ZPKiP3wiYDkM=452)
OpenAI has announced three new discoveries in mathematics, computer science, and physics. For the math result, which is about harmonic analysis, the AI lab will release the AI output, so researchers are free to organize their own efforts to explain the result. OpenAI will form an external group of collaborators to explain the computer science result, which is about complexity theory, and will make the AI output publicly available. The lab will also form an external group of collaborators for the physics result, which is about quantum field theory, but defer to them on the release of the materials.

How the Claude Code team does code review (18 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fclaude.dev%2Fblog%2Fsolving-code-review-at-anthropic%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a17cee30c6-5d83ada9-0072-4696-b3da-8551d1fd868f-000000/HE_wApAquZTGFadN_v6BYt2yuJs5PO9AO12ZMl0Aduo=452)
Claude writes about 90% of the code in Claude Code. This post explains how the team reviews the code at the volume its agents produce. The team moved most of the reading in the review stage to machines, optimized for human time and attention, and invested heavily in making it fast and easy to undo mistakes. This resulted in merged pull requests to Claude Code's codebase more than doubling while the share that had to be reverted fell by more than 40% between June and September.

The Counting House (49 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fyegge.ai%2Fessays%2Fthe-counting-house%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a17cee30c6-5d83ada9-0072-4696-b3da-8551d1fd868f-000000/J46qpkA8lN6_-vT7XFKEzOInYPLcT7eVcS5RssBvRIw=452)
The Counting House pattern adds AI to systems that handle large volumes of human work, notably customer service departments. It can be used to control and improve costs, quality, and human ramp-up time using comparatively cheap AI models. A Counting House uses classic data science and engineering to put lower-tier models on hardened rails to support human workflows. It allows teams to measure and report how well models do against their human counterparts.

The End of the Internet (18 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fstratechery.com%2F2026%2Fthe-end-of-the-internet%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a17cee30c6-5d83ada9-0072-4696-b3da-8551d1fd868f-000000/r1Q-NZTdGSpJQXm5RUEdwP-4zrPSheaaPT-nLUnbMYk=452)
Blogging began to die when Google started aggregating the internet. Social media made it much easier to create and share content, but this caused the amount of content to increase by orders of magnitude. The latest series of security vulnerabilities unveiled by AI will further shrink the public internet.
