Zuckerberg's Biohub Partners With DOE, NIH to Invest $1.8 Billion in Biological Data for AI Models (1 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FAvzMzJ/1/010001a11b209c25-691d3850-581a-4e68-b116-6853e18bbf9f-000000/smsT-wwDT_BJmNPlnji_h41x7ZRBzLXaB5plipp6vnI=452)
Biohub, a nonprofit co-founded by Mark Zuckerberg, is working with the Department of Energy, the National Institutes of Health, and other funding partners to invest $1.8 billion in building biological data ready for AI models. The investment is aimed at providing data sets that allow researchers to use AI models to find new ways of preventing and treating diseases. The DOE will invest more than $500 million across five years in lab measurement, modeling, and computation to build an AI-ready open data resource; the NIH will coordinate the contribution of relevant datasets, repositories, and knowledge bases; and Biohub will work with the NIH to standardize the datasets for AI model training.

In Vienna and Beijing, the First Nuclear Clocks Begin to Tick (6 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2F08ykNi/1/010001a11b209c25-691d3850-581a-4e68-b116-6853e18bbf9f-000000/_yiNPpbUkQrLNKUwpqpoYYKNLKfINXxBCwIBu0tTfVs=452)
Two independent teams in Vienna and Beijing have simultaneously built the first clocks that keep time by counting the squishing and unsquishing of the atomic nuclei of thorium. The clocks are so precise that they only lose one second once every few million years or so. Nuclear clocks have the ability to search for certain kinds of dark matter. If a particle of dark matter passes through the thorium nuclei, it could register as a wobble in the nuclear clock's otherwise steady tick-tock.

Influence without authority (4 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.gabrielvaldivia.com%2Fnotes%2Finfluence-without-authority%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a11b209c25-691d3850-581a-4e68-b116-6853e18bbf9f-000000/nSiLYLmzoXLhCJOhpTdffB3pGvXGXXfIhD5lwLWOKBQ=452)
Super ICs gain influence by repeatedly doing the unglamorous things. Over time, making the work better, making the story clearer, and making it easier for other people to succeed changes how people behave around you. Authority is a natural side effect of being the person others rely on. Focus on sharpening your craft, turning ideas into stories people can retell, packaging your thinking so it travels without you, and helping your peers hit their goals.

Anti-Patterns in Software Blogging (12 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Frefactoringenglish.com%2Fblog%2Fanti-patterns-software-blogging%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a11b209c25-691d3850-581a-4e68-b116-6853e18bbf9f-000000/Pl8aPbpAB3CTUGb1GsUh2lvLFpldSd1gCXOjHq8Gi1I=452)
Readers need a compelling reason quickly to continue reading. The reader should be able to read an article from start to finish without clicking links or hovering for tooltips. Avoid presenting articles as a follow-up to a previous article. Drop the formality and write the way you speak in real life. Test articles in mobile view, and use browser testing tools to find low-contrast text to improve visibility.
