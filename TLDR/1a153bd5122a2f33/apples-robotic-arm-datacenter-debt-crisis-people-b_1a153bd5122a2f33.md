Decoherence (48 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fmaevyn.codes%2Fdecoherence%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a153bd4fe1-f47a031d-37d7-40b2-a841-16a8ed5f0289-000000/voUzZk09TzHsdaSJ5TF7jRxxIADt2_lolgZRv6lJ3eI=452)
Decoherence is the accumulation of locally valid but mutually incompatible or redundant models within a system. It causes the cost of change to be dominated by translating among those models and preventing unintended side-effects. Decoherence grows relative to the rate of change of a system, and it increases faster the more decoherent a system is, resulting in runaway exponential growth. It rules out the specter of runaway recursive self-improvement, as decoherence both prevents AI from improving in a bubble and limits the rate at which it can improve outside of it. However, it also introduces a new kind of disaster called Decoherence Collapse.

](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fterrytao.wordpress.com%2F2026%2F10%2F18%2Fwill-it-be-a-golden-age-or-a-dark-age-for-mathematics-and-science%2F%23more-18496%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a153bd4fe1-f47a031d-37d7-40b2-a841-16a8ed5f0289-000000/h0kAD0r-JV3s_ZzBzG-DftAO5ZnWBBkbg7KWRzphbPc=452)
