SpaceX's Bumpy Ride on the Stock Market May Get Bumpier (6 minute read)

SpaceX is set to acquire 130,000 acres of marshland in southern Louisiana (4 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Farstechnica.com%2Fspace%2F2026%2F08%2Fspacex-is-set-to-acquire-130000-acres-of-marshland-in-southern-louisiana%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019fcc55f721-f3654601-97ca-435e-9267-a1c3f94a4175-000000/jcFy7Rz_sfaKr4aFHfHJA1aeRmURqkKHmhiHWsfzwDk=452)
SpaceX is close to finalizing a deal to acquire about 130,000 acres along the northern coast of the Gulf of Mexico. The deal is expected to be officially announced later this month. The site, known as Pecan Island, is an 18-mile stretch of marshland. It became available as part of a legal settlement that resolved dozens of lawsuits blaming ExxonMobil for pollution and coastal land loss.

Base Power raises $1B to roll out its giant new home battery (3 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Felectrek.co%2F2026%2F08%2F03%2Fbase-power-raises-1b-to-roll-out-its-giant-new-home-battery%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019fcc55f721-f3654601-97ca-435e-9267-a1c3f94a4175-000000/Rd71dxxVmz4KxhJOfiKvy9PpvjJLUm9-ukw1V9uKQtQ=452)
Base Power's Core is a 39.2-kWh home battery that can keep a house running for up to 36 hours. Core, which can be installed in under an hour, can be connected to solar and has a built-in port for recharging from a portable generator. The lithium iron phosphate battery is designed to operate between -22F and 122F and has an IP67 rating for submersion in up to 3 feet of water. Core will only be available as part of a subscription service, and the company will retain ownership of the battery.

Drug Discovery Has No Magic Wands (18 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.a16z.news%2Fp%2Fdrug-discovery-has-no-magic-wands%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019fcc55f721-f3654601-97ca-435e-9267-a1c3f94a4175-000000/1fmIbS6MnoDG12TxraojY505Oy1ZfUldeBUBlhLnSZc=452)
AI is set to transform human health. However, it will not be a magic wand: we don't understand human biology well enough for a clever enough reasoner to find the cures hidden in what we know. Aimed at biology we barely understand, AI will mostly help us generate failures faster. The path to real cures is to measure biology in the right way and use AI to derive novel insights from those measurements.

Cloudflare Computer (7 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fblog.cloudflare.com%2Fcloudflare-computer%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019fcc55f721-f3654601-97ca-435e-9267-a1c3f94a4175-000000/srPcFmkh4bW3X0aWkGR7hWOqUVtgp6gH0xJqOwQmyIc=452)

The Endgame Of Vertical Integration (8 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.akashbajwa.co%2Fp%2Fthe-endgame-of-vertical-integration%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019fcc55f721-f3654601-97ca-435e-9267-a1c3f94a4175-000000/bHpIibYO6nL1sbssU32J1bsSYM69JpPytJFSF0NNueU=452)
Model labs and agent labs are converging. Model labs continue to develop first-party applications that compete with their customers, while agent labs are starting to train models. The gains from co-designing models with harnesses is why agent labs need to move into model training. The challenge is for agent labs to offer the best intelligence per dollar at margins that the model labs can't match.

What Are Companies Getting for All That AI Spending? (9 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FuegIWW/1/0100019fcc55f721-f3654601-97ca-435e-9267-a1c3f94a4175-000000/-vypJQg73h4WMjbjthOS7NfflFKZuoyKZ_pn5Rlj4r4=452)
The cost and benefits of AI are difficult to weigh. There are now thousands of models, and there is no consensus around how much energy a token requires, which model is best to perform a given task, or how many tokens it should take. The Linux Foundation established the Tokenomics Foundation in June to create common parameters for AI providers to disclose. Being able to measure the costs and benefits of AI will help researchers understand how AI is going to change everything.
