SpaceX declares Starship ready for orbit, sets launch date next week (3 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Farstechnica.com%2Fspace%2F2026%2F09%2Fspacex-sets-launch-date-for-first-starship-orbital-flight%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a0a9d22dbe-53fd2a1c-0158-4275-8842-922c72c790f1-000000/yAo3FR7wK2MyPSPyBzVBBM_-aCn4n9HES4aFSSBMK2o=452)
SpaceX plans to launch its 14th Starship mission as early as September 22. It will be the first time SpaceX attempts to launch the experimental vehicle into orbit. The super heavy lift rocket will be carrying 26 of the larger V3 Starlink satellites into orbit 275 kilometers above Earth. The upper stage will seek to complete six orbits around Earth before completing its mission after about 10 hours. SpaceX will not attempt a return of the upper stage to the launch site in Texas on this mission, as it plans to gather more data about heat shield performance.

Is There Anything a Fruit Fly Brain Can't Do? (6 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FhpYEEi/1/010001a0a9d22dbe-53fd2a1c-0158-4275-8842-922c72c790f1-000000/2GWNdFRsF40c4_BXr6noir7DRjaXXCS8FtmVwlp-Xl8=452)
Researchers at the Janelia Research Campus at the Howard Hughes Medical Institute in Virginia, in partnership with Google, recently published a new map of the adult male fruit fly's brain. The effort, which involved hundreds of scholars, resulted in a complete map of the fly's 166,000 neurons that can be trained to complete tasks like solving a Rubik's Cube or playing poker. Internet engineers and artists have made the model complete many seemingly impossible tasks for a fruit fly, like parallel parking a car, playing Doom, and trading cryptocurrencies.

Agility's new humanoid robot will stop, squat to avoid harming human coworkers (5 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Farstechnica.com%2Fai%2F2026%2F09%2Fagilitys-new-humanoid-robot-will-stop-squat-to-avoid-harming-human-coworkers%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a0a9d22dbe-53fd2a1c-0158-4275-8842-922c72c790f1-000000/pj17V7Jp0-77NTVLgo7TEbhqoLWpwbqHioZ3DHMgGm8=452)
Agility Robotics has debuted a humanoid robot engineered to work safely near humans without risking harm to their co-workers. The robot autonomously takes precautions when it detects a person at a distance, such as moving to avoid the person or standing still so the person can pass without getting closer. It can even choose to squat and assume a seated position if a person is getting too close. The robot will be available in early access within the first half of 2027 and become generally available by the end of the year.

Mark Zuckerberg Takes Aim at Anthropic in Debate Over AI Slowdown (3 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2F8fG3dv/1/010001a0a9d22dbe-53fd2a1c-0158-4275-8842-922c72c790f1-000000/3FHctDG_kdwVYfXeGkNHrjDs4HYuAcQiLwgWN_UtRUg=452)
Mark Zuckerberg made a social media post on Tuesday, saying that calls to pause AI development industrywide were the wrong approach. He said that every lab has the responsibility and incentive to move at the pace required to train its models safely. He acknowledged the risks of AI, but said companies were already incentivized to keep users safe and slow progress on their own. Meta has been aiming to catch up in the AI race after falling behind Anthropic and OpenAI last year. It plans to release a new model, internally called Watermelon, in the coming weeks.

Senate Votes to Block Crypto Bill in Major Blow to the Industry (4 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FD9bgGZ/1/010001a0a9d22dbe-53fd2a1c-0158-4275-8842-922c72c790f1-000000/oPA1Jm2vVYYKqyjg_WAQcoP7szu2SCBN0UDp1aKb5Zw=452)
The US Senate has voted to block consideration of the Clarity Act, a piece of legislation that would create business-friendly rules for cryptocurrencies. The vote makes it unlikely the legislation will pass anytime soon, if ever. Many Republicans embraced the bill, but Democrats largely opposed it. The Democrats wanted stronger language to prevent public officials from using crypto to make money. President Trump generated $1.4 billion from a network of crypto businesses last year.
