Apple quietly lowered the daily spending cap on AI models for its employees last month. Some engineers had their budgets cut from $300 to $200, and the company is planning further cuts for some engineering teams that may potentially reduce their daily allowances to as little as $100. Employees say that few workers were using their full $300 daily allowances, making the previous limits unnecessarily generous.

SpaceX's Starfall just went from secret to storefront (16 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.teslarati.com%2Fspacexs-starfall-just-went-from-secret-to-storefront%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a1ab7f686a-ab5d6dcf-dfbd-4544-ae04-b5740139932d-000000/Z4t7B4fxfcik7qHV5cTfW2IXDdtSpw_qiMSIAJlqRWI=452)
SpaceX has launched a commercial page for Starfall, a disk-shaped reentry capsule that can carry up to 1,000 kg of payload. The capsule can supply 1 kW of power and connect to the internet through Starlink, allowing researchers to pull data from experiments while in orbit instead of waiting for splashdown. It can be used for manufacturing, research, or testing and is aimed at biomedical customers, pharmaceutical companies, and materials science research. Starfall's timeline will depend on Starship - SpaceX is preparing for Flight 15, where it aims to catch both stages with the launch tower.
