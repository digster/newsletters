Anthropic Joins the AI Agent Race With Claude Duet (3 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2F0l3eSc/1/010001a1632fffb0-ec76a83f-1104-4559-9cb2-cc1789241474-000000/-j6YvPYZmOVZjRSC9BoG6Yn5RsYVfHeZ3enySfYwkxw=452)
Anthropic's Claude Duet joins a fast-growing field of AI assistants that take actions and perform tasks on their own. Unlike OpenAI's dots and Meta's Muse, Anthropic's agent was built for work and is less capable at personal errands. It can connect to more than 6,000 outside apps and make purchases through Link, Stripe's digital wallet, and Shopify's Shop Pay. The agent shows users exactly what it plans to do before performing actions, and some actions, like trading stocks or moving money between accounts, are blocked even with user approval. The wait list to use the agent is now open to Anthropic's Pro and Max plan subscribers.

Musk Tries to Lock In His Tesla-SpaceX Dream Before Trump's Exit (11 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FzxfkAY/1/010001a1632fffb0-ec76a83f-1104-4559-9cb2-cc1789241474-000000/DyylGeUYXvFZZDtWeLvwRkK4h_tp_0NllWojo4KDjYw=452)
Elon Musk is fully behind a combination of SpaceX and Tesla, his two most valuable companies. He is pushing to announce a deal by the first half of 2027 so it closes during the Trump administration. The timing will depend on when Tesla's board gets comfortable with the details - SpaceX shareholder approval is less of an obstacle, as Musk controls more than 80% of the voting power. Combining the companies before the end of this administration would consolidate Musk's corporate power before a potentially less accommodating political and financial environment takes hold.

Tesla starts Optimus robot production in California as profit falls 43% (3 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F10%2F21%2Ftesla-stars-optimus-robot-production-in-california-as-profit-falls-43%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a1632fffb0-ec76a83f-1104-4559-9cb2-cc1789241474-000000/l2OxDUmZKdQCGPdOZALWzIxVfCpNksT2Ld5L61XKBlQ=452)
Tesla has started production of its Optimus humanoid robot at its factory in Fremont, California. While the company admits there is still more work to be done before the robot is fully safe and reliable, it says it has proven that the current manufacturing process for Optimus is suitable for a commercial product. Elon Musk says Tesla will be able to make 20,000 units per week by the end of 2027. Tesla's profits fell 43% in the third quarter due to heavy investments in its Optimus robots, AI, and autonomous driving.

Physicists trap and tame single particle of antimatter for months (12 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fphys.org%2Fnews%2F2026-10-physicists-particle-antimatter-months.html%23google_vignette%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a1632fffb0-ec76a83f-1104-4559-9cb2-cc1789241474-000000/T155Yry0FA_gjH0BRTx1utvOkDYDsp7wUajh9BHXhYc=452)
Physicists at Northwestern University have coaxed a single positron (the antimatter counterpart of an electron) into an exquisitely controlled quantum state for the first time. The scientists cooled the fast-moving particle to nearly absolute zero after capturing it from radioactive decay, and then suspended it in a near-perfect vacuum, resulting in a state where the positron behaved much like an artificial atom with quantum energy levels scientists can precisely control and observe. The technique could enable the most precise test yet of a fundamental symmetry between electrons and their antimatter counterparts.

The Topology of an LLM Agent Loop With Tool Use (7 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fblog.exe.dev%2Ftopology%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a1632fffb0-ec76a83f-1104-4559-9cb2-cc1789241474-000000/Otoxd1-o4RyG79n6UhtNuPU5Dp5IcYJeEKV52eMmw3M=452)
An agent is three things: a model, an agentic loop, and a set of tools. Where these run has implications for cost, capability, effectiveness, and security. If you focus on topology, you can break down any agentic product and reason about what a product can and cannot do. There isn't one perfect loop - good UX for a general-purpose chatbot is not the same as good UX for a coding agent.

The internet is having a very bad cybersecurity day (2 minute read)

How Mark Zuckerberg Pushed Meta to Shield Him From California's Proposed Wealth Tax (6 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2F85Uqjp/1/010001a1632fffb0-ec76a83f-1104-4559-9cb2-cc1789241474-000000/sjzDaPyId7aRSJ36VzxwJEMSizvkeiN625cu3Ia6Yrk=452)
California's proposed tax on billionaires prompted Mark Zuckerberg to leverage the board at Meta to get the company to protect him from it. He issued an ultimatum to the board on September 9 that he would sell his home in Palo Alto, and the listing would be public, if a deal wasn't struck by September 13. The board decided to issue a new class of stock to shareholders that Zuckerberg could sell to pay the potential wealth tax without cutting into his 60.6% voting power. California's proposal would assess a one-time 5% tax on all holdings of billionaires who lived in California as of January 1, 2026.
