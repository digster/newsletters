28 Miles and Counting: NASA's Perseverance Rover Sets a Mars Driving Record (7 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FnwkCT4/1/010001a13f2e48b6-8c6f260e-de8e-4030-b29a-08763b591c07-000000/QnmRDR5tTnkRPjdHUYBBWy1LI28t4hnZZLzDRteMLa0=452)
NASA's Perseverance rover has now driven 28.1 miles on Mars' surface, surpassing the distance that Opportunity covered from 2004 to 2018. Perseverance landed on Mars in February 2021 and has been traveling in and around the Jezero Crater since. It was set to surpass the driving record back in August, but it came across some interesting rocks, so it dawdled for two months. Perseverance most likely still has many miles to go in the coming years. This article contains some of the highlights from its drive so far.

Things are finally looking up for NASA's first missions to Venus in a generation (13 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Farstechnica.com%2Fspace%2F2026%2F10%2Fnasas-embattled-venus-missions-are-back-on-track-after-cancellation-threat%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a13f2e48b6-8c6f260e-de8e-4030-b29a-08763b591c07-000000/gPmYC75Ms8YYlfdceOO3dtm8YD6FZK-Fb3npY8d15Qs=452)
Two NASA missions are on track to launch to Venus in the early 2030s. The Deep Atmosphere Venus Investigation of Noble gases, Chemistry, and Imaging (DAVINCI), which will send a parachute-assisted descent probe through Venus' atmosphere, is now scheduled for launch in December 2030. The Venus Emissivity, Radio science, InSAR, Topography, And Spectroscopy (VERITAS) mission will launch in November 2032 to map the Venusian surface by radar from orbit. The European Space Agency is building an orbiter for launch to Venus in 2032, and Rocket Lab plans to launch a privately funded probe as soon as next year to search for signs of organic material in the planet's clouds.

Agentic Verification (18 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Faddyo.substack.com%2Fp%2Fagentic-verification%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a13f2e48b6-8c6f260e-de8e-4030-b29a-08763b591c07-000000/Evw2eqxC1s-WIE0xPGPJlZanI3Mw-q9yjWPLbCFpAJ4=452)
Giving AI agents a way to independently verify their work heavily improves the quality of output. Write end-to-end tests that simulate real user flows and act as the ground truth. Use property-based testing to specify what should never happen and generate thousands of test cases that attempt to break those constraints. Require tests to run quickly and deterministically so that agents retry when the loop is slow or unreliable.
