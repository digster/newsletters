Google announces Gemini 4 Argon AI model, but you can't use it yet (3 minute read)

Google's Gemini 4 Argon model offers industry-leading performance in coding, knowledge work, and cybersecurity. Google's engineers are already using the model extensively, and Argon-powered agents have been working to migrate C/C++ codebases to Rust across Google. The model supports an output limit of 1 million tokens. It will be priced at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens for a limited time. The model is still in limited testing, and there is no timeline for when it will be available to enterprise and consumer customers.

Science & Futuristic Technology

Google figures out how to watermark AI-designed proteins (11 minute read)

The DeepMind team at Google has developed a technique that can create watermarks on protein sequences without compromising the protein's function. The watermark is impossible to remove and can survive basic exporting, resizing, and other editing. The system could be used to give DNA synthesizers greater confidence in their sources.
Most powerful obesity drug yet: People lost up to 25% of weight in trial (5 minute read)

Late-stage clinical trial data on the obesity drug retatrutide showed an average loss of 25% of body weight after 90 weeks. More than a third of the participants taking the drug in the trial lost 30% or more of their weight. The drug also helped reduce knee pain and sleep apnea events, and it improved cardiometabolic measurements across the board. At the start of the trial, more than a third of trial participants had prediabetes - the condition had resolved in more than 90% of those participants by the end.
