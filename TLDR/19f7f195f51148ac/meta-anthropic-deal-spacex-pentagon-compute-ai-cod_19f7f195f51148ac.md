](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Farstechnica.com%2Fspace%2F2026%2F07%2Findias-first-privately-developed-rocket-reaches-orbit-on-dramatic-debut-launch%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019f7f195dc5-b49fe245-0c54-48d2-801a-12e9c312c49b-000000/8I6_t_ZQDVXK0R3NW_t9bMxaNuTeW_SqtDwhpH_puNY=452)
Skyroot Aerospace's Vikram-1 rocket, India's first fully commercial satellite launcher, launched into a 280-mile-high orbit from an island spaceport in the Bay of Bengal on Saturday. The launch was delayed for more than half an hour to resolve a last-minute technical problem, but the flight was deemed a grand success. The Vikram-1 is India's first private orbital rocket to reach orbit on its very first attempt. A second Vikram-1 launch could happen before the end of the year.
