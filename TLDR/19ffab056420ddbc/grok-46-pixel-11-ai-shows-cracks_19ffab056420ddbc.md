The most notable hardware innovation for the new Pixel lineup is the return of a notification LED for the Pro phones. Northrop's robot space mechanic is a new way to keep satellites at work longer (5 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F08%2F12%2Fnorthrops-robot-space-mechanic-is-a-new-way-to-keep-satellites-at-work-longer%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019ffab0559a-19666028-6db7-4427-82c5-64a06cb46a2c-000000/zDguLWfPBp87xyjUFmjAiqmT6hzEVlhxGHQDO_bD_wE=452)
Northrop Grumman launched a Mission Robotic Vehicle (MRV) and three Mission Extension Pods (MEPs) into orbit on a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket in July. The MRV is a powerful satellite equipped with two advanced robotic arms. MEPs are simpler satellites that are essentially modular propulsion systems. In 2027, the MRV will use its robotic arms to attach one of the MEP pods to a telecommunications satellite, which should keep it in orbit for years to come.

AI is removing the middle class of software engineering (5 minute read)

Why Tiny JPEGs Look Different in Chrome (4 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fguillaumetech.github.io%2Fposts%2Fjpg-scaling-chrome%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019ffab0559a-19666028-6db7-4427-82c5-64a06cb46a2c-000000/Nka76VwIxC9zQNbbRqDX5M2mRpbeSCdaRsDtwQRsnlw=452)
What looks like a rendering bug is actually a clever JPEG decoding optimization in Chrome. Chrome delegates image decoding and rendering to Skia, which does not always decompress the full image and scale it afterward. It computes the closest fraction with a denominator of 8 and decodes the image at that scale, then scales the image further using a more traditional downsampling algorithm until it reaches the desired size. This is why small images can look thicker on some machines.

Why you can't copy Palantir (38 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fethanding.substack.com%2Fp%2Fwhy-you-cant-copy-palantir%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019ffab0559a-19666028-6db7-4427-82c5-64a06cb46a2c-000000/HIMvnoteqoqYPaOckXLqjhCLkMJuw68Efp9-mObYI70=452)
Palantir is a vertically integrated outcome-based software solution provider. It offers tech consulting and software services with a unique outcome-based model that lets it bypass the traditional trade-offs of both traditional consulting and software companies. It is vertically integrated because it has an extreme elitist attitude towards using its own software and services, unlike the rest of the market, which is much more polyamorous. There's little hope of any company being able to catch up to Palantir as investors allow the company to do R&D over an extremely long and unspecified time horizon.
