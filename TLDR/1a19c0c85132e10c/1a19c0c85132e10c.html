OpenAI Raises Less Than Half of $30 Billion Target in Latest Round (3 minute read)

OpenAI's latest funding round received tepid interest from investors. The deal values the AI lab at $1.25 trillion, less than the $1.4 trillion valuation it had earlier hoped to achieve. Investors appear to have grown pickier about which companies they want to back and on what terms. The industry recently experienced a brutal selloff tied to disappointing earnings results from giants, including Google, Amazon, and Intel.
Cisco gives 27-year-old engineer unlimited AI tokens to rewrite 98% of the company's legacy code in 24 months (2 minute read)

Cisco has given Padmanabhan Krishnamurthy, a 27-year-old ex-startup founder who joined the company through an acquisition, the job of refactoring 98% of the company's legacy code within 24 months. Krishnamurthy has four weeks to produce a proof of concept. He has been given a budget of unlimited tokens to achieve the goal. Cisco is looking to hire more employees that fit Krishnamurthy's mould.

Science & Futuristic Technology

SpaceX no longer needs NASA, and that's a problem for America's space agency (34 minute read)

NASA is no longer an indispensable customer for SpaceX. SpaceX is now increasingly disinterested in the US government for most contracts outside of data services. The company is still fulfilling its existing contracts with the government, and at a lower cost and generally higher reliability, but it is no longer signing many large new contracts for spaceflight and services in the traditional launch and in-space flight domains. It sees the vast bulk of its future revenue coming from AI.
Meta Reportedly Built A Humanoid Robot Prototype That Can Do Household Chores (2 minute read)

Meta's Max robot is designed to help out around people's homes. The prototype was built with the help of a Chinese robotics company in roughly three months. Powered by Nvidia's Jetson Thor chips, the robot weighs roughly 106 pounds and is about 1.5 meters tall, and has a display that can show facial expressions, a built-in speaker, and hands designed to pick up small objects. Meta is already working on a second prototype with updates to the design of the robot's torso and hands, as well as a projector and additional sensors to help it handle stairs better.

Postgres 19 is late, and it's AI's fault (5 minute read)

Postgres 19 most likely won't be released until November and could possibly be postponed further. Several large features were reverted quite late in the beta period. AI has made it easier to identify bugs, and this has resulted in a lot more bug reports that the PostgreSQL committers have to work on. This has taken the focus off development of new features, as the project cares about quality.
A proposal: how to hire junior engineers in the age of AI (11 minute read)

With product engineering, figuring out what the requirements are is still most of the job. This post presents an interview model where LLMs can be used in interviews to roleplay as customers, and engineers talk to the models to extract requirements. It's relatively cheap and can be used in conjunction with a standard human interview.

Digital minimalism is about being intentional about how you access digital technology. It is about making a conscious choice rather than something that you get sucked into by default.
