Early Data Indicates an AI-Generated Drug Could Slow Aging (2 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FCQRbJy/1/010001a0809a4d42-593756d0-79ef-421c-be45-5b493f9b53f3-000000/uMdahDJG_9klO8q201gRE9yzOS5cjwnvYsZ4bNxEHpM=452)
Rentosertib is a drug created with the assistance of AI. The AI originally indicated that the drug could help treat patients suffering from a chronic lung disease, but data from clinical trials show it could also slow the aging process. The drug reduced the biological markers of age as measured by six 'aging clocks', a different kind of AI technology designed to predict a person's morbidity and mortality. While the drug shows promise, it is still years away from regulatory approval, even for use in sick patients.

How well do agents use test/verification techniques? (68 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fdanluu.com%2Fagentic-testing%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a0809a4d42-593756d0-79ef-421c-be45-5b493f9b53f3-000000/AcS4uHNexYo9O5OA1A47t_oYUK6Dhn-GVs4AOYn0Zbk=452)
It's easier than ever to hit a particular quality bar by having coding agents use effective test techniques. However, software quality seems to be getting worse. This indicates that whatever defaults developers are using might not work very well. This study tests whether simple instructions to agents to use particular techniques or libraries improve implementation correctness. It found that agents tend to either just write the tests they would normally write, but inside a framework for a different type of test technique, or they'll use a technique superficially but not really do the things that get the value out of the technique.

Four Questions About AGI (28 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fvoiceinthemachine.com%2F2026%2F09%2F07%2Ffour-questions-about-agi%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/010001a0809a4d42-593756d0-79ef-421c-be45-5b493f9b53f3-000000/g2zuyUUBIseQk7YyV9AO0yYDW4cblNVSJn1dDxmIqYw=452)
Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), as the term is commonly used, points to nothing really tangible or concrete and points us at the wrong thing. Recent announcements about AGI are just companies saying they've nearly arrived at a destination they defined themselves. Instead of trying to define AGI, the industry should focus on making a long series of specific things reliable enough to trust, and let the destination take care of itself.
