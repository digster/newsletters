The Signal Chats That Cost OpenAI Researchers Their Jobs (13 minute read)

Three OpenAI safety researchers were fired after security officers discovered Signal chats with disappearing messages turned on on their personal devices. OpenAI claimed the researchers were fired for violating clear policies for handling sensitive information and not for raising safety concerns or speaking out. The researchers had spent recent months at the company pushing for more industry collaboration on safety. They claim their firings will serve as an excuse for OpenAI to close a channel to independent third-party insight.
Amazon's New Tablet Is a Credible iPad Rival for Much Less Money (11 minute read)

The Alexa Tablet lineup offers a more open approach to software than Amazon's previous Fire brand that lets consumers use the devices like any other Android tablet. Users can download apps from the Google Play Store and access popular services like Gmail and YouTube. Amazon previously restricted access to its own app marketplace. The 12-inch Alexa Tablet Pro is well constructed and has a smooth 12-hertz display, making it competitive with Apple's iPads.

Crown Castle is likely also in talks with Amazon and other satellite-based telecom providers regarding leasing deals. Elon Musk has openly discussed creating a standalone wireless carrier from scratch. LLMs are making formal software verification feasible at a much larger scale (23 minute read)

We need ways to prove that code is free from bugs. Formal software verification, which could provide this guarantee, has always required years of expert effort, but LLMs can now produce formal proofs at large scale. This enables new software development workflows that include proven security guarantees. Formal verification will likely become an essential part of secure software development as model capabilities continue to improve.

‘If Anything Happens to This Guy … Everyone Is Screwed' (13 minute read)

Ondřej Surý is one of the volunteers who helps maintain and automate patches for PHP. He alone is responsible for most of the world's distribution of PHP. His work, along with just a handful of others, has allowed countless websites and organizations to fix the recently revealed PHP vulnerabilities quickly. He's become arguably the unsung hero of the entire internet, despite the fact that the only compensation he receives for his work is from donations. This article features an interview with Surý where he discusses the unfolding AI-cybersecurity crisis, how the internet's infrastructure is maintained by small crews of volunteers, and how it feels to save the web.
An artist's take on AI in math (9 minute read)

Mathematics seems to be a field where the outputs are celebrated less as examples of subjective expression and more as advancements in knowledge and understanding. A mathematician's work may be informed by their experience, but that's not what gives their contributions value. Mathematical knowledge remains objectively true whether it is observed by humans or sea sponges or computer programs.
