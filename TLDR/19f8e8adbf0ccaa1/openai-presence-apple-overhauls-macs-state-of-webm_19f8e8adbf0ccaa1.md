OpenAI unveils Presence, a new platform that lets enterprises launch and manage realtime voice agents and chatbots (12 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fventurebeat.com%2Forchestration%2Fopenai-unveils-presence-a-new-platform-that-lets-enterprises-launch-and-manage-realtime-voice-agents-and-chatbots%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019f8e8ad8ce-1fcba769-13a2-4dd0-bdcc-e6ac0bd70029-000000/yOwDSZfKO_syRpa3RnvpgVFWjD5rcPl79XM9sQFi3xc=452)
Presence is a new enterprise product from OpenAI for deploying and managing voice AI agents and chatbots across customer-facing and internal business workflows. It is designed for eligible enterprise customers who want agents that operate under company-defined policies, permissions, and evaluation standards. Presence is now available through a limited general availability program led by OpenAI forward-deployed engineers and select global systems integrators. OpenAI has not disclosed pricing, geographic limits, contractual terms, or expected costs for integration.

Science Corporation's vision-restoring chip wins EU approval (5 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F07%2F22%2Fscience-corporations-vision-restoring-chip-wins-eu-approval%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019f8e8ad8ce-1fcba769-13a2-4dd0-bdcc-e6ac0bd70029-000000/fWz_qEPJca7Sclt5QUd7oghuG8gW8yfkuS9mcJww0Vg=452)
Science Corporation has won approval from Europe's medical device regulator to begin selling its PRIMA device, which restores vision lost from age-related macular degeneration. The device has also received a designation from the US FDA, the first step toward an expedited regulatory review. It requires an hour-long outpatient procedure that plants a small chip in the back of the eye. The product gives functional vision through camera-equipped glasses that transmit a view of the world to the chip.

Travis Kalanick's robotics company, Atoms, has raised $1.7 billion in a funding round joined by Uber, reconnecting Kalanick back with the company he founded. Atoms is a rebranded holding company created from a ghost kitchen project Kalanick has been working on since he resigned from Uber. Kalanick has yet to go into specifics about what he wants to build with Atoms, but he has indicated he wants to build a wheelbase for robots and make everyone more productive. A large portion of the funding will likely go toward hiring.

The State of WebMCP (16 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.spronta.com%2Fblog%2Fstate-of-webmcp-july-2026%2F%3Futm_source=tldrnewsletter/1/0100019f8e8ad8ce-1fcba769-13a2-4dd0-bdcc-e6ac0bd70029-000000/PWiJrJsc7ysXE4fDfGB-o8QNMKSCv7VvGkGQtDPLiIQ=452)
WebMCP lets web pages register typed JavaScript functions that browser AI agents can call. While there is strong industry support behind the spec, site adoption is close to zero. No mainstream agent consumes the tools yet, but Google says Gemini in Chrome will be the first. The API changed locations in a recent draft, which means early adopters already carry migration debt.

Durable Objects are Made for Agents (11 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FXcrQDk/1/0100019f8e8ad8ce-1fcba769-13a2-4dd0-bdcc-e6ac0bd70029-000000/GWeyFujqftRK6agK1fzHHQYdB2vR8ThVSZvK-BMUNjk=452)
Cloudflare Durable Objects are a primitive that combines a serverless V8 isolate for running code, a paired SQLite instance, and request routing to a particular isolate. They allow developers to start thinking about their service as a set of many event-driven objects rather than as a bunch of services talking to a database. Durable Objects run explicitly in a single-threaded manner and come with native support for WebSockets. They are cheap and extremely token-efficient.

Calm Technologies That Excite Me (14 minute read)

The problem with hypergrowth AI startups (7 minute read)
](https://tracking.tldrnewsletter.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flinks.tldrnewsletter.com%2FUG5mWJ/1/0100019f8e8ad8ce-1fcba769-13a2-4dd0-bdcc-e6ac0bd70029-000000/n8IHPzpVqrAKxD8AE07h23IVaRXCGfY_l8X2BtWpj3I=452)
Startups that have had explosive AI-driven revenue growth may be in trouble. Most of these companies are scaling revenue by reselling inference at very low (and sometimes negative) margins. The value of the products or services they're providing isn't adding anything on top of the intelligence they are reselling. While VCs seem excited about this growth, the business model is unsustainable.
