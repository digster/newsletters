Modern cynicism often masquerades as sophistication, but it's frequently just a mask for fear.
Here's why choosing to care matters.
We live in a culture that treats ironic detachment as the default defense mechanism for the intellectual. By keeping an emotional distance from our efforts, we protect ourselves from the sting of failure, but we also paralyze our creative spirit. This video examines why this posture of apathy is a form of cowardice rather than genuine insight.
I explore the French concept of elan vital, a spirited forward motion that acts as a powerful antidote to our current cultural malaise. Drawing on the work of David Foster Wallace and Theodore Roosevelt, we examine how to pivot away from the safety of the sidelines. The goal is to move past the knowing smirk and embrace sincere commitment, even when it feels uncomfortable or risky.
