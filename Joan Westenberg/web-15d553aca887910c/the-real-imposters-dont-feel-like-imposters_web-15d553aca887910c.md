Imposter syndrome tends to punish the people least likely to be imposters.
The competent rehearse, revise, double-check, and lie awake wondering when they’ll be found out, while the truly underqualified often move through the world with terrifying confidence.
If you feel like an imposter, that feeling may not be evidence that you don’t belong. It may be evidence that you care enough, and see clearly enough, to keep getting better...
