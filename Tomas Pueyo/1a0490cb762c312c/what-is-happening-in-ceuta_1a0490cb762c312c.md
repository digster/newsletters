Of course, the debate becomes: Who should Ceuta belong to? As a Spaniard, I’m biased, so take my opinion with a grain of salt. Historically, Ceuta specifically was: A Carthaginian colony for a couple of centuries. Roman for nearly 600 years.⁷ It fell to the Muslim conquerors of the Umayyad Caliphate around 700 AD. For the next 700 years, it belonged to different Muslim emirates, sultanates, and kingdoms. In 1415, it fell to the Portuguese, who controlled it until 1580. The Kingdom of Fez recognized this ownership. Since 1580, it’s been part of Spain.
So who should it belong to?
