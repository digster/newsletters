Though three weeks ago I Notice some patterns. The ancient and modern eras were pretty different, and each of these eras can be split into early and late versions, which have mildly different sources. The ancient world changed from early, when tradition, family, and community peaked, to later, when priests, aristocrats, and warriors peaked. The modern era changed from early, when states, law, scholars, schools, and public intellectuals peaked, to later when hearts, activists, celebrities, news, and medicine peaked. Community (via social media) has also peaked again in the late modern era. Civic clubs peaked in a very early modern period. Note that most ancient sources are still important in the modern era; what changed was that the modern era added many more sources. Thus sources have become a lot less concentrated over time. In the first period there were only 6 sources >50, while in the last there were 12. Only once source, artist/musician/storyteller, didn’t change much in strength over history. In my prior post I said that to fix cultural drift we will need to rely a lot more on speculative market prices as sources of moral authority, but I didn’t know how that could happen.
