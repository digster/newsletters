I did a web search on the above phrase, in prep for a podcast, and in ten pages of results didn’t find anything I thought reasonable. Which promotes me to write this, hoping it can show up for other when they so search. This content When considering purported evidence for meteors or ball lightning, we are much more forgiving than when considering purported evidence of telepathy or ghosts, as reasonable priors for the former are much higher than for the latter. That is, we more easily believe that the former happened. The assumptions we’d have to make to accommodate thinking that we’d seen examples of the latter are far more extreme. When considering purported evidence for aliens-on-Earth, we similarly ask how extreme are the assumptions we’d need to make to make sense of it. Many have dismissed and ridiculed such sightings, even when many independent sober folks all say they saw roughly the same amazing things, largely on the basis of claiming that aliens-on-Earth requires extreme assumptions, and so has a very low prior. I disagree. To evaluate a prior re aliens-on-Earth, we should collect the key facts in need of explanation, seek the most plausible assumptions required to predict them, estimate an a priori chance for each assumption, and combine those into a total prior chance. Here are those key facts, for a minimal aliens-on-Earth concept. The universe looks dead, untouched by life or civilization, both far and near. We are still alive on Earth, and so were never killed off by aliens. Any aliens on/near Earth don’t see to have changed, built, or accomplished much noticeable. While advanced aliens could be either totally invisible or very obvious, many aliens are seen on Earth, but are instead near the edge of visibility. Most aliens seen are impressive, but not doing much useful, or helpful, or hostile.
I’d respect others who tried similarly to list the key data to be explained, and then offered their highest prior explanation of this data, even if they disagreed with mine. Here are my best guess highest-prior assumptions (with priors) to predict the minimal aliens-on-Earth concept facts, in addition to our usual consensus cosmology. (0.3-1) [Grabby aliens](https://substack.com/redirect/3860df1f-4c4a-4118-a821-2c0d3b2be1bc?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o)model, which estimates such aliens appear ~ 1/M galaxies, at median date of now, then expand at near light speed.(0.01-0.1) Life started at Eden, which panspermia seeded Sun’s ~10K # stellar nursery, then a Sun sibling ~Klyr distant made advanced aliens many Myr ago. (0.01-0.1) Aliens made no-colonize rule. As siblings might break, sent team to Earth w/ goal: persuade us to follow rule; kill us as last resort. (0.3-1) Team given low discretion, simple plan: domesticate Earth via be near, impressive, not too hostile, don’t show more that might give us ick.
The total prior here is 10^-5 to 10^-2, mid estimate of ~3x10^-4. This is higher than the typical ~10^-6 prior in a murder trial, so it is high enough to take seriously. Here are some added facts that would also need to be explained under less minimal concepts of aliens-on-Earth. Aliens often grab humans, do stuff to them, so that humans notice and remember. Alien ships often break and crash Alien ships often contain bio-squishy residents
To explain these added facts, I’d add more assumptions, like: (0.03-0.3) Alien team at Earth has somewhat escaped home control. (0.03-0.3) Alien tech/ability has rotted, they are now often incompetent. (0.1) Alien made rule forcing use of bio-squishy forms, at least at Earth.
