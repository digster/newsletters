I asked Claude Fable 5 to, for each 25yr period starting in 1825, find the top 5 authors arguing for strong world governance, then to collect the top 3 reasons that each offered, then to integrate those into a comparable set of reasons, and then to graph how the importance of those reasons changed over time: As you can see, advocates started out with reasons of war, trade, morality, and inevitability, but have drifted over time toward big weapons, inequality, and the environment.
