3: I’m continuing to work on modeling (and maybe one day automating) some parts of AI grantmaking (you can see previous efforts here [ https://substack.com/redirect/497fc680-1213-4c77-accd-cd8555c5c0eb?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ]). I would once again like people who are well-versed in the AI/grantmaking worlds to take a survey on how much they value various things. This one involves assigning utility weights to ~80 different AI safety accomplishments. Take it here [ https://substack.com/redirect/f9aeec8c-c745-42af-959b-120f4a03425b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ]. After you’ve taken that survey, if you still have more time/energy it would be extra helpful for my data collection if you would take this alternate version [ https://substack.com/redirect/71e667b2-c104-4fdf-a236-ca90150490ea?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ], which asks you to predict how I would answer the first survey (so I can separate out worldview differences from weight-choosing random noise).

5: Grantee and Manifold Markets co-founder Stephen Grugett announces his latest project: MNX [ https://substack.com/redirect/605c4e97-5fb1-43b3-8457-881a9e5b84d5?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ] is a real-money prediction market on AI. It lets people sell futures representing AI company stocks, bet on AI company revenues directly, and a few other things like Epoch Capabilities Index targets. Interestingly, despite the talk of Anthropic’s API falling from from $2T to $1.5T, both Polymarket and MNX still price it around $2T.
6: Friend of the blog Goodheart Labs is working on Common Notes, aka “Twitter Community Notes, but for everything”. They’re implementing it through a combination of AIs (who write most notes), users (who write others, and vote on which ones are helpful), and a browser extension (which makes the system show up on everything, including this blog). Check it out, and install the browser extension,  here. [ https://substack.com/redirect/4820dedb-3d71-41c3-8cc3-f1c7d5960c08?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ]
