Maria is funny and a great writer…and a practicing Stoic. She combined all these into a lovely new novel that I’m sure is going to be a huge hit. [Go Gentle](https://bc5923e4.click.convertkit-mail4.com/k0upeqxw67b6h5vwdzmhlhopvwpppa8hn8wlx/z2hghnhexrevoecp/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cudGhlcGFpbnRlZHBvcmNoLmNvbS9wcm9kdWN0cy9nby1nZW50bGU_X3Bvcz0xJl9zaWQ9NGUzNzU5NWViJl9zcz1y) follows Adora Hazzard, a Stoic philosopher and single mother in New York City, as her well-ordered life is turned upside down. It’s funny, smart and full of plot twists that include black-market art deals and a bunch of Stoic themes. The funny thing is that in the intervening years, I’ve read many books by Halberstam, including [The Children](https://bc5923e4.click.convertkit-mail4.com/k0upeqxw67b6h5vwdzmhlhopvwpppa8hn8wlx/z2hghnhexrevoxfp/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cudGhlcGFpbnRlZHBvcmNoLmNvbS9wcm9kdWN0cy90aGUtY2hpbGRyZW4_X3Bvcz01Jl9zaWQ9Mjk2YzAzNzE4Jl9zcz1y) (amazing. But now I was finally ready to appreciate [The Best and the Brightest](https://bc5923e4.click.convertkit-mail4.com/k0upeqxw67b6h5vwdzmhlhopvwpppa8hn8wlx/owhkhqhwdlwv49tv/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cudGhlcGFpbnRlZHBvcmNoLmNvbS9wcm9kdWN0cy90aGUtYmVzdC1hbmQtdGhlLWJyaWdodGVzdD9fcG9zPTEmX3NpZD01ZDAwMDVhZDcmX3NzPXI=) as the absolute masterpiece that it was. I just don’t know how he does it–not just the reporting, but his ability to get to the essence of the people he writes about. It’s not journalism, honestly, it’s literature. Like if you told me [The Best and the Brightest](https://bc5923e4.click.convertkit-mail4.com/k0upeqxw67b6h5vwdzmhlhopvwpppa8hn8wlx/owhkhqhwdlwv49tv/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cudGhlcGFpbnRlZHBvcmNoLmNvbS9wcm9kdWN0cy90aGUtYmVzdC1hbmQtdGhlLWJyaWdodGVzdD9fcG9zPTEmX3NpZD01ZDAwMDVhZDcmX3NzPXI=) was a novel about a fictional war, I’d still be recommending it! (Both because it’s literature and because it would teach you about human folly). How did we blunder into this war? How did we mismanage it once we did? How did the personality flaws–but also the virtues of Kennedy, LBJ, Dean Rusk, McNamara, Maxwell Taylor, George Ball, John Kenneth Galbraith–make that not only possible, but in a way, make it a foregone conclusion? As always, the way to understand the present is by studying the past. I chose to read this book instead of watching the news and it’s made me a more informed citizen. It also gives me a pit in my stomach about our recent foreign interventionisms (General Mattis said the same thing at Stanford).

Speaking of Stoicism and Stanford…[I also got to speak to Jim Collins this month](https://bc5923e4.click.convertkit-mail4.com/k0upeqxw67b6h5vwdzmhlhopvwpppa8hn8wlx/58hvh7hg2og854t6/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cueW91dHViZS5jb20vd2F0Y2g_dj1veXV4QWFrZ01rYw==), who famously popularized the “Stockdale Paradox” in [Good to Great](https://bc5923e4.click.convertkit-mail4.com/k0upeqxw67b6h5vwdzmhlhopvwpppa8hn8wlx/25h2hoh3w03z7xu3/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cudGhlcGFpbnRlZHBvcmNoLmNvbS9wcm9kdWN0cy9idXNpbmVzczk_X3Bvcz0yJl9zaWQ9OTEwMDlmODZjJl9zcz1y), which resulted from his time at Stanford with Admiral Stockdale (and is based on Stoicism). His new book is really good and classic Jim Collins. It reminded me a lot, in its set up, of Plutarch’s [Parallel Lives](https://bc5923e4.click.convertkit-mail4.com/k0upeqxw67b6h5vwdzmhlhopvwpppa8hn8wlx/qvh8h7hdp2d58xil/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cudGhlcGFpbnRlZHBvcmNoLmNvbS9wcm9kdWN0cy9jbGFzc2ljczQ_X3Bvcz0xJl9zaWQ9MjZlZTY5OWRlJl9zcz1y) in the way that it profiles two similar historical or pop culture or business figures and how they dealt with the problems and opportunities that life gave them. He studies the diverging paths taken when faced with similar, life-altering “cliff moments,” and uses those patterns to explore how we can most meaningfully answer the question of what to make of our lives. Jim sent me an early edition of the book back in January, in which he included a note that read: “Ryan, this is the best work of my life to date. It transformed me over 12 years. I’ll be curious how it changes how you look at life. Enjoy, Jim.” And it did. It changed how I look at life. It’s a [lovely book](https://bc5923e4.click.convertkit-mail4.com/k0upeqxw67b6h5vwdzmhlhopvwpppa8hn8wlx/kkhmh6hnv2nq8xfl/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cudGhlcGFpbnRlZHBvcmNoLmNvbS9wcm9kdWN0cy93aGF0LXRvLW1ha2Utb2YtYS1saWZlLWNsaWZmcy1mb2ctZmlyZS1hbmQtdGhlLXNlbGYta25vd2xlZGdlLWltcGVyYXRpdmU_X3Bvcz0xJl9zaWQ9OTEwMDlmODZjJl9zcz1y).
