This past month was full of storytelling insights, and we’ve now arrived at the final one. Enrollment for Thinking In Stories closes in just 2 hours, and after that, the cohort is finalized.
In this final message, I want to briefly talk about the future. People often say that the future is more uncertain than ever, but the truth is that it’s always been this way.
Whenever I find myself fearing uncertainty, I like to think about what’s timeless and explore that realm further.
In my personal life, I know what produces contentment regardless of what the future holds: quality time with family, hangouts with friends, and a place to call home. These things won’t change regardless of where culture or politics takes us.
In my creative life, I know what produces purpose regardless of what the future holds: an emphasis on self-expression, a love for learning, and a desire to communicate what I know to others. These things won’t change regardless of where technology or media takes us.
Storytelling is where I’ve chosen to invest so much energy because it embodies the timelessness of a creative life. Regardless of what the future has in store, there will always be a need to express yourself and to find the people that resonate with your ideas. There will always be a hunger for stories that inspire people to be better versions of themselves, and for those that are looking for sources of solace to help ease their pains.
If you know how to tell a story, you’re contributing to the web of culture. You’re putting a megaphone to the values you want to see in the world. The changes you want to see manifested.
There’s nothing more powerful than an idea that resonates with another, which is why storytelling is such a timeless skill. No matter where the future takes us, the ability to convert an idea into a story will be always be valued.
I’ve spent a lot of time thinking about how I can share what I’ve learned about this craft, and Thinking in Stories is the result of converting these thoughts into action.
