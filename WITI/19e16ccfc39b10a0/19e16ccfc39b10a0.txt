Tell us about yourself.
I am Marc, and I run Fabricateurialist [ https://substack.com/redirect/a701be32-d2b5-428d-86af-e54b37edc06f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ] to help people better understand the clothes they are buying. By sharing technical garment reviews, I aim to increase product literacy so people can shop smarter while holding brands accountable — because we are in a post-empty-marketing-promises era where people want to know they are getting what they pay for.
Describe your media diet.
Relatively diverse. Alongside established publications like the NYT, Monocle, and Bloomberg, as well as 404 Media and The Verge, there are Fairchild Media’s fashion publications, sprinkles of Skift, trade titles like insidedenim.com and Drapers, and a variety of newsletters.
What’s the last great book you read?
David Szalay’s Flesh.
What are you reading now?
The screenplay of a friend who has been writing for TV for two decades. It’s a romcom — something the internet has been insisting audiences are craving — and being halfway through, I would genuinely love to see it get made.
What’s your reading strategy when you pick up a print copy of your favorite publication?
Front to back. The layout exists for a reason, and I trust the editors in charge.
Who should everyone be reading that they’re not?
Not sure about everyone, but the IEEE newsletter is one of the best sources of cutting-edge science and technology news out there. If you’re serious about the future of technology, subscribe.
What is the best non-famous app you love on your phone?
As someone with outsized screen time who uses their phone to produce and share content, its sole purpose is work. Nothing else.
Plane or train?
Depends on the distance. I recently took the train from DC to New York, and it is simply more convenient than dealing with overcrowded airports — that small edge is worth trading the now-often-worthless miles for. That said, I peaked at 180 legs in one year, nearly a flight every two days, so at this point a flight feels like taking a train or a taxi. The views, though, will never get old.
What is one place everyone should visit?
Portopiccolo Sistiana — conceived as a man-made eco resort, first anchored by a Luxury Collection property, with a few dozen apartments chiseled into its stone walls. It’s a cozy, walkable small resort town: a bakery, a beach club, a fully outfitted pool and spa complex with an outdoor terrace where you can watch the sun set over the Gulf of Trieste.
Tell us the story of a rabbit hole you fell deep into.
The correlation between fertilizer supply and pricing, and beef futures. From my previous life in CPG, the geopolitical impacts on food and supply chains remain one of the most fascinating webs I know. I love sharing it with people to illustrate just how fragile the global economy is — because we have eliminated every redundancy in favor of efficiency, and the consequences are hiding in plain sight.
