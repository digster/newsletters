After artists watched the Twin Towers fall, they knew New York needed a way to honor its losses. But they never expected their installation—two beams of light projected into the clouds­—to last 25 years.
The Nonfiction Book Market is Collapsing [ https://substack.com/redirect/2bc7c715-9d62-40be-9e0b-b9a992f34565?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ] (Dear Head of Mine)
Interesting, though as WITI-friend Steve Bryant added: “You’ve gotta mention that we’ve never before had so much access to real-time info, which satiates in part (but obviously not fully!) the desire to read nonfiction. If men are “monitoring the situation” as a hobby, there’s less need to read a book. And of course books aren’t as social as being online.”
