Housing

Manufacturing
Space-based data centers seem to be taking up most of the oxygen for what sort of things become possible if Starship or other technologies makes launch costs much cheaper, but it’s interesting to consider what else might be unlocked from vastly cheaper access to space. Here’s one: incredibly high-quality fiber optic cable. [IEEE Spectrum [ https://substack.com/redirect/b1cf818f-4567-424d-8166-79bbf8f8ba06?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ]]
Last week we looked at a report that noted the US produces a very small amount of aluminum, despite our huge demand for it. Apparently one big reason is that we import a large amount of aluminum from Canada, which can produce it thanks to an abundance of cheap hydropower. “...the enormous power requirements for making aluminum have meant that Canada, where abundant hydropower has kept electricity prices from rising as sharply as in the U.S., has supplied about 70% of U.S. aluminum imports in recent years.” [WSJ [ https://substack.com/redirect/8d628820-a19d-4f7a-b477-8fc1e89f9dfe?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ]]
Defense startup Anduril wants to make a laptop in the US. [Anduril [ https://substack.com/redirect/3504647e-d1fd-4ac4-91c7-a17773ea8b91?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ]]
The Wall Street Journal on the crisis in US explosives manufacturing. “Following the widespread closures of government-owned plants in the 1990s, the entire production of the powerful explosives HMX and RDX came from a single supplier, Holston Army Ammunition Plant. The 84-year-old plant makes about 3% of what it did during peak production in 1944, according to Hacker’s research, and it has been beset by explosions and pollution violations. There is a one- to two-year wait for products coming out of Holston, said industry experts.” [WSJ [ https://substack.com/redirect/e76b4428-e48a-498d-830f-6a899fe89e87?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ]]
The ultra-competitive Chinese car industry is apparently struggling in the domestic market, thanks to a drop in demand — domestic sales are down 22% compared to last year. This is part of what’s driving the huge export push from Chinese manufacturers. [WSJ [ https://substack.com/redirect/68191ce4-2106-4dac-bcfc-4685047acc8d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ]]
