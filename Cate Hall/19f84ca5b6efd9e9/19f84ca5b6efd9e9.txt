We tried really hard to write a book that is easy to read, relentlessly practical, and not one word longer than it needs to be — so I’ll keep this short.
It’s a good book. Anyone who works in the publishing industry will tell you, however, that writing a good book isn’t the half of it — you have to promote the shit out of it.
Well, I hope they’re wrong. Because a couple months ago I discovered a massive gap in the world, and I’ve spent the time since building a company to fill it, rather than shilling the book.
As a consequence, we would deeply appreciate your help getting the word out. Some things you can do:
Like and repost this post
Post on social media about the fact that it’s out today
Forward this email / post to a friend you think might enjoy the book
Write a review on Amazon [ https://substack.com/redirect/5ac5109e-b84c-4ad4-9db4-cfa6f75bdb56?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ], Goodreads [ https://substack.com/redirect/586e2514-3b6b-4f68-85e6-a47035833d0b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ], or wherever you bought it (this is mondo helpful for visibility) — it can be about why you’re excited to read it, if you haven’t yet
