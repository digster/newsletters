Mantegazza’s coked-up enthusiasm was contagious. He believed that coca was a drug that would fundamentally transform human life, and his evangelism inspired people to try coca chewing — and to isolate the key compounds in the coca leaf. By 1860, a German chemist named Albert Niemann (who later invented mustard gas!) had published his dissertation on the extraction of cocaine. The drug soon spread through Europe.
