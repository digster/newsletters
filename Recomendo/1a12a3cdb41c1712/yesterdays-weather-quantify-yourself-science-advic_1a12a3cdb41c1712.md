Most weather apps don’t show you yesterday’s weather. Who cares, right? But it’s useful information if you want to know whether today will be warmer or colder than yesterday.[ Yesterday](https://substack.com/redirect/53f82b55-f929-466b-bb06-fcd755ea8718?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) is a free iPhone app that shows a chart of the hour-by-hour temperatures for yesterday and today. If it’s late in the day, you can see how tomorrow’s weather will differ from today’s. It also gives you a simple one-sentence summary, like “Today will be noticeably hotter and sunnier” or “Daytime high noticeably warmer and rain unlikely.” It was created by Matt Webb, who publishes[ Interconnected](https://substack.com/redirect/da6e5358-fafe-41f6-a561-05e172205257?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o), one of my favorite newsletters. It’s a great example of vibe coding. — MF
Eighteen years ago Gary Wolf and I started the Quantified Self movement – the idea that you could use technology to track yourself. Gary hosted thousands of meetups where people shared what they were tracking and what they learned, and from this vast experience over decades, he finally wrote up the results of what we know about self-tracking in a new book, [The Quantified Self](https://substack.com/redirect/770d9ec6-fee9-4b36-af22-f5f4aad6d09d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o). The book is fabulous, perceptive, useful, and illuminating. Turns out self-tracking is more about observing and having the right questions, than it is about wearable technology. Self-tracking can be a form of what Gary calls “personal science” – you are doing experiments on yourself (N=1), and the methods, criteria, protocols are different than what you would do for N=millions. I highly recommend The Quantified Self for everyone; we are all doing personal science on ourselves, and this will help you do it better. — KK

There are many reasons to live somewhere else, or to have the option to live somewhere else. [The Nomad Capitalist](https://substack.com/redirect/46173d9b-bca2-4f23-b948-f39e0db4c1d8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) is a YouTube channel that takes a market approach to the question of where you can live. It researches, compares, evaluates how much it costs to purchase a passport, or a residency card, or buy real estate around the world, and gives advice on what are the best bargains for “citizenship through investment” and so on. What is the cheapest price for a passport and what do you get? Where are taxes minimal? Many of the options he examines are not cheap, but for some, it can still be worth it. It’s good to know what your options are. — KK
