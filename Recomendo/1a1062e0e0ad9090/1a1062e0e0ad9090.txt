Listen to a Jurassic forest
Scientists have reconstructed what a Jurassic forest might have sounded like [ https://substack.com/redirect/75bdade5-bcb6-458b-806d-64c8764bdae6?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ] 165 million years ago, and the one-minute audio is a really cool soundscape to imaginatively step into. They created it by analyzing and modeling the sound-producing structures preserved in 20 fossil insect wings from nine species that lived together in what is now Inner Mongolia. It’s a diverse chorus of low trills and high-pitched chirps, and I wish I could loop it all day as background noise. — CD
Inexpensive exercise headphones
If I'm doing vigorous exercise, my earbuds fall out. I didn't want to spend a lot of money on a second pair, so I bought these inexpensive Joywise Bluetooth headphones [ https://substack.com/redirect/ba1a08bc-830d-4cc3-b3bd-223828a227fc?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ]. They are wireless, have loops that fit around the ear, and are connected to each other with a wire. Unlike most small earbuds, they charge via USB-C directly rather than in a case. The sound quality is great for the sub-$20 price, and the batteries last up to 16 hours before needing a recharge. — MF
Analog math
For mathematical nerds, Henry Segerman [ https://substack.com/redirect/c8c03f2a-f3a7-46ae-94b8-c0b107b14018?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ] is your channel. Segerman 3D-prints weird mathematical shapes, invents curious mathematical toys, constructs intricate mathematical models, and turns math into analog things you can 3D-print yourself. His YouTube channel demos all this with expert explanations of the math behind it. — KK
Free online utilities
Normal Tools [ https://substack.com/redirect/a3dfa977-7cd9-437c-9868-d22f42881621?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ] is a free collection of 193 single-purpose tools for all kinds of random small tasks, including calculators, color pickers, image resizing, and file conversion. It has more tools than I will ever need, but useful to bookmark. Most tools are private by default and process everything locally in your browser, with no account or download required. — CD
