The India Notes newsletter

India is a pluralist state. The Indian mind is a result of remarkably diverse forces. Everything exists in multiple variants - religions, languages, faiths and ideologies. One size fits all fails under the Indian context. With the growing access to mobile technology and the internet, there is a pressing need to find mental models that work for the country's diversity.

The mindset with which we designed technology for the top 100 Million English-speaking users will fail for the rest of the country. This newsletter uncovers Indian consumer psychology through research.

Published once a week.
