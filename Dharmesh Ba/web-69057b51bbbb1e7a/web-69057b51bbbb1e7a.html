Mind-blowing! UPI records a whopping 36 crore transactions per day as quoted by RBI governor Shaktikanta Das. The market is ruled by PhonePe (50.1%), Google Pay (34.2%), and Paytm (10.8%), the big three in the game.

It's the new normal! I whip out my phone to pay, and the shopkeeper automatically directs me to the UPI QR code without a word. This could be the most significant behavioral change caused by digital intervention in my lifetime!

This week, I took on the challenge of deconstructing the scan and pay flow of the top three UPI apps. Don't be fooled by its simplicity; the apps have put some serious thought to create a seamless user experience. I'm in awe of how it works like a charm for someone in bustling Bangalore or a remote village in Bihar!"
