Good Morning Ishan,
Almost every morning around 8 a.m., Karthik Gurumurthy takes a call from “SRT Sir”.
That’s him, yes. Sachin Ramesh Tendulkar. The “Chief Inspiration Officer” at Ten x You might want to discuss a product idea with the CEO of the sportswear brand, suggest a tweak to an outsole grip, or argue for a colourway to be reconsidered. He is, as he was on the field, relentless.
“He reminds me of my days at Instamart, where I would call my team constantly,” Gurumurthy says. Only now the “demanding boss” on the end of that line is the “God of Cricket” himself.
Gurumurthy spent nearly four years turning Swiggy Instamart into a quick-commerce juggernaut. When he founded Ten x You in 2024, he brought along Karan Arora, Instamart’s former head of supply chain management. Together with Tendulkar, they are building a sportswear brand focused on cricket, with products tailored for Indian feet and local surfaces. The inspiration is Anta and Li-Ning, the homegrown brands that dismantled Nike’s dominance in China.
The genesis of Ten x You lies in a speech that Tendulkar delivered in the parliament in 2017, urging India to move from a “sports-loving nation to a sports-playing nation”. For Gurumurthy, that distinction exposes a gap in the country’s $6.2 billion athletic footwear market.
“There is a lot being made on the sneaker side, there is very little happening on the performance sports side,” he says.
