[What does Razorpay know about agentic AI that OpenAI, Amazon, Ebay don’t?](https://the-ken.com/story/what-does-razorpay-know-about-agentic-ai-that-openai-amazon-ebay-dont/?utm_source=weekly_email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly_recap_card)
The same week OpenAI rolled back its in-chat shopping agent, Razorpay launched what it called the biggest disruption to payments since UPI. But is it really?
Razorpay is betting big on agentic payments in India. At FTX26, it showed a Zomato demo where a user could order by voice and pay without opening a checkout page or UPI app. This could weaken the power of payments apps like Phonepe and Google Pay. In an agentic model, AI handles discovery and the initial transaction, while payments apps risk being reduced to a backend authentication layer. In-app agents, like Zomato’s, mostly optimise shopping within one platform, but neutral cross-platform agents could be more disruptive by comparing sellers across marketplaces. Agentic commerce raises unresolved questions around trust, fraud, consent, data use, and liability, making regulation crucial before these systems scale widely.
