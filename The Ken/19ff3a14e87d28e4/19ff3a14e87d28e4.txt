Being indispensable to India’s two-wheelers should be enough for Dhoot Transmission to drum up interest in its Rs 3,070 crore initial public offering (IPO). 

The Maharashtra-based company makes almost 40% of the wires and cables that connect electrical and electronic components in the country’s scooters and motorcycles. And it does this while being one of the most profitable companies of its kind. In FY26, it turned a profit of roughly Rs 400 crore on revenue of Rs 4,500 crore. 

The share sale of the Bain Capital-backed company, which has also raised funds from asset manager Blackrock and Abu Dhabi’s sovereign-wealth fund, was fully subscribed early on day two of the three-day window. Investors can submit bids until 11 August.
