Jio Platforms’ Rs 30,200 crore initial public offering (IPO), India’s largest-ever, was the first from the house of Reliance Industries in 20 years.
The next one cannot and will not take as long.
Reliance Retail Ventures, also a Reliance Industries subsidiary and India’s largest retailer, raised almost Rs 47,300 crore from nine global investors in 2020, mere months after Jio Platforms raised over Rs 1.5 lakh crore. Both companies share several common backers, including private-equity majors KKR and General Atlantic, and Saudi Arabia’s and Abu Dhabi’s sovereign-wealth funds.
...
For years, Reliance Retail’s stock-market benchmark was Avenue Supermarts, whose Dmart is India’s second-largest supermarket chain. The lead Reliance Retail enjoys over Dmart is significant. Reliance Retail’s FY26 profits of almost Rs 14,000 crore were almost 3.5X as high as Dmart’s.
But Dmart is no longer the company to beat.
