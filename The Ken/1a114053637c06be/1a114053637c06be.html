In terms of media and investor attention on public-market fundraises in September, Rentomojo’s initial public offering was a mere footnote, and understandably so. At Rs 1,250 crore, the share sale was the second largest last month, but it was just over 5% of the National Stock Exchange of India’s enormous IPO. 

Even so, the furniture-and-appliance-rental company’s offering was subscribed 73 times, and the firm ended its first day of trading 20% higher than its issue price. 

Less than three weeks later, investors once again can’t get enough of Rentomojo. Yesterday, on Tuesday, they drove up the company’s market value by 8%, to Rs 5,800 crore, in response to its financials in the June quarter. (Rentomojo’s IPO filing only had numbers until March.)

Its revenue in the period rose 50% from the previous year, to Rs 126 crore. And its operating profit, adjusted for the impact of a fire at one of its warehouses in Uttar Pradesh, expanded at a similar pace, to Rs 29 crore. 

Those weren’t the only reassuring signs in its investor presentation.
