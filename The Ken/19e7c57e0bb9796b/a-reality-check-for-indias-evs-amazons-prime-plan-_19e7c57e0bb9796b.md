While Tata is trying to build a self-contained chipmaking ecosystem from the ground up, Murugappa is acquiring disparate pieces and stacking them together
India’s semiconductor ecosystem is getting built, layer by layer, player by player. The two leading players are using different strategies to build a chipmaking ecosystem in a country that barely has a foundation for it. Murugappa Group’s endeavour, through CG Power, is about assembling a stack of disparate but functioning units. It has acquired a fabless design facility and struck partnerships to build an assembly-and-testing facility. Tata Electronics is building at scale, deploying capital and inhouse expertise to build a self-contained ecosystem.
