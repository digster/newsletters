India needs Tata, Mahindra EVs to cut the oil bill. But EV owners are stuck at workshops

The lack of a robust EV ecosystem, from poor after-sales service to paltry infrastructure, is fraying consumer interest

The oil shock caused by the US-Israeli war against Iran is making a switch to EVs an increasingly appealing idea for India. The government has rolled out incentive packages totalling Rs 1 lakh crore to push EV adoption, not least to reduce its crude oil import bill. But poor after-sales service, insufficient infrastructure and lack of domestic manufacturing of critical components are making an EV-transition harder to pull off. EV owners say they love what their vehicles offer in comfort and performance but are frustrated by the daily negotiation between the promise of technology and the reality of infrastructure. Cipla had one of its worst quarters. Investors still rewarded it

India’s fourth-largest drugmaker lost two of its most-profitable drugs within months. Its new pipeline is what investors care about

Cipla has had one of its worst quarterly profit drops in Q4 FY26, aided by both expected and unexpected setbacks. Two of its most-profitable drugs lost out their sales dominance in the US, a market that contributes more than a fifth to Cipla’s revenue. Yet, the day after it announced the results, Cipla’s stock ran up 8%. Turns out investors are not concerned about what’s in the past, but are looking forward to what the pharma major has to offer. Tata and Murugappa’s chip race starts with what India doesn’t have

While Tata is trying to build a self-contained chipmaking ecosystem from the ground up, Murugappa is acquiring disparate pieces and stacking them together

India’s semiconductor ecosystem is getting built, layer by layer, player by player. The two leading players are using different strategies to build a chipmaking ecosystem in a country that barely has a foundation for it.  Murugappa Group’s endeavour, through CG Power, is about assembling a stack of disparate but functioning units. It has acquired a fabless design facility and struck partnerships to build an assembly-and-testing facility. Tata Electronics is building at scale, deploying capital and inhouse expertise to build a self-contained ecosystem. [Read the full story]

Bajaj Finance mastered self-disruption. Succession may be harder

An early stage ultimatum—meet this target or I’ll shut you down—still reverberates across the company, propelling it forward

Every year, the senior leadership of Bajaj Finance pokes holes in their company at the offsite. This is part of a wider culture of discomfort, which questions the status quo and propels the company forward. This fintech arm of the Bajaj group has managed to balance extreme cost efficiency with rapid growth. It has also perfected the art of breaking the company into ever smaller parts in order to grow it. [Read the full story]
