Five-decade-old auto-parts giant Samvardhana Motherson has spent years honing a segment it can’t loosen its grip on, even if it tries. Its FY26 earnings review proved as much. 

It had a record year with Rs 1.26 lakh crore ($13 billion) in revenue and a $96-billion order book, with the main chunk coming from everything that goes inside a car—wiring harnesses, mirrors, polymer modules. Only 3% came from its non-automotive business. 

But Chairman Vivek Chaand Sehgal, the man behind this company, has long known that the car business alone cannot drive revenues forever. 

Samvardhana Motherson needs to find new baskets for its eggs. Which is why in November 2020, the company announced https://www.livemint.com/companies/news/motherson-sumi-sees-non-auto-biz-at-25-of-revenue-by-2025-11606182964670.html its plans to build a non-automotive business that would drive a quarter of its topline by the end of FY25. This includes consumer electronics, aerospace, health and medical devices, and logistics. 

But a year after the “Vision 2025” deadline, things are not going exactly according to plan.
