Three days ago, on 28 June, Sigma Advanced Systems—an integrated aerospace and defence manufacturer that claims its order book includes a seven-year Rolls-Royce agreement https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MEGASOFT_27042026080408_PRESSNOTE2704.pdf valued at 300 million pounds—approved a preferential issuance of equity shares worth Rs 460 crore. 

The Hyderabad-based company has earmarked 25 per cent of the proceeds for general corporate purposes, while the rest is designated for further global expansion and acquisitions.

This new kid on the defence block was having a field day with two massive rallies before the preferential allotment.

The first from March to October 2025, saw the stock quadruple, and then again from March this year to its recent announcement, saw it quadruple again, delivering over 500% returns in just over a year. 

That momentum has somewhat cooled over the last two days, as the stock has been hitting its lower circuit amid what appears to be profit-booking after an extraordinary run.
