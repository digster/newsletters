“Maximise Manufacturing,” screams the homepage of Zetwerk’s website, followed by “Make for India, Make for the World.” Then, “technology-led, asset-light manufacturing platform” makes an appearance too. 

These phrases are along the lines of what investors will be thinking of when they subscribe to Zetwerk’s Rs 5,000 crore IPO, which opens on 15 October. 

To be fair, an investment in Zetwerk is a bet on manufacturing. Since 2018, Zetwerk, backed by Peak XV and Accel, has aggregated a number of small manufacturers to supply customers large and small with a range of products, including transformers, solar panels, smartwatches, and electric-vehicle chargers. It has orders worth Rs 13,000 crore in the pipeline, twice what it had two years ago.
