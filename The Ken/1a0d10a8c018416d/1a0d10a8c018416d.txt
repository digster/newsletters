“MTF is one area where the business growth is scaring me,” Nithin Kamath, founder of Zerodha*, confessed to his customers in an annual letter last month. Kamath had resisted MTFs for a long time, calling it in 2021 “probably the worst financial product for retail investors.” Today, Zerodha runs a Rs 9,200 crore MTF book, which accounts for 10% of the firm’s revenue.

India’s total margin trading facility or MTF loan book has been on a run and has more than doubled since 2024, the year of Zerodha’s entry, to an all-time high of 1.5 lakh crore this September, with an estimated Rs 19,500 crore a year in interest payments flowing from investors to brokerages.
