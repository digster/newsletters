In an unprecedented milestone, India’s peak electricity demand crossed the 270 GW mark today at around 15:30 hours. What industry experts predicted we would only see by mid-June has arrived weeks early, fueled by intense, nationwide heatwaves. 

The country’s power ministry was forced to appeal https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/india-battles-power-cuts-heatwave-boosts-electricity-demand-record-2026-05-22/ to its citizens’ frugality: “Although we are prepared to supply electricity as required, due to the intense summer, let us all try ​to use electricity wisely and judiciously.” 

I’m in Mumbai, and the infernal heat has made me a misanthrope. I admit millions around the country have it far worse. But to a section of the economy, our misfortune is, naturally, a reason to cheer.

Since January, the BSE Power index, made up of 18 electricity producers, grid operators, and makers of power equipment, is up 23% in an otherwise gloomy market. The BSE 100 benchmark is down 9% in the same period. Over the past five years, BSE Power has outpaced the latter by three times. In fact, the index touched an all-time high last week.
