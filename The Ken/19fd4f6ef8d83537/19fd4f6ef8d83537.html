Design over discovery.

That’s the tagline Aridni Shah of ImmunitoAI doesn’t want you to glaze over. In drug discovery—where thousands of molecules are screened, tested, and mostly discarded, and where 90% of what does survive eventually fails in clinical trials—Shah and her co-founder Trisha Chatterjee are trying to tilt the odds. Calculatedly, because India punishes this pursuit. Economically, because domestic risk capital won’t touch it.

“In less than five months, in a single shot of design, we were able to get functional drug candidates tested in the lab,” said the 35-year-old Shah. “Globally, this is not something anyone has been able to show commonly. Which is why we say design over discovery—before we even start, we know what the final molecule looks like.”

What ImmunitoAI is attempting is one of the hottest hunts in science right now, with AI talent, compute, and capital flooding in from every direction. Once a backwater, drug discovery has become a coveted frontier. 

…

The math is brutal. “Compared to five years ago, it’s not very difficult to raise Rs 10 crore for drug discovery today,” said Narendra Chirmule, a former big-pharma executive who teaches drug discovery at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay and the University of Pennsylvania.

“But Rs 100 crore is extremely difficult. What you actually need is Rs 1,000 crore.”

Nobody in India can raise that. So why, for heaven’s sake, do it?

In my opinion, we are not asking the right question.
BEL has enjoyed, and may be still continues to enjoy, a single source status for large part its product portfolio, which has been created thru ToT from DRDO (Production Partner status) or through service nominated ToT partner of foreign OEMs.
Pricing in contracts for such products is largely based on formulae that protect profit margins (at gross, EBIDTA and net basis. Note that defence PSUs are largely debt free). Pricing for Follow-on orders also follow certain norms to protect margins.
What has changed in the last decade? It is the entry of private sector. They have consistently challenged order on nomination basis that were flowing to BEL.

Profit margins do get stretched when you participate in competitive bidding. More so when private sector would do anything do gain an entry into this ever-green sector.
So the correct question to explore would be to find out what is the order mix which BEL is presently executing? What is the percentage (in terms of value not numbers) of orders won through competitive bidding? Portion of revenue from these orders won thru competition will be much lower than that which could be accrued from orders obtained on nomination basis.

Indigenous Content (IC) could be factor, but only for matured systems in production. In the initial stages, IC enhancement leads to additional costs for developing items and multiple sources.
