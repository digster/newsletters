Good Morning Ishan,
“Your workplace will be in the stadium.”
That was all Gavin wanted to hear. He had grown up playing, loving and, as he put it, worshipping football. Now he would be making his living off it.
Gavin is a sports-data scout in Guwahati, Assam, and his job sounds like a hobby: go watch a live match and log whatever happens on the pitch. That’s about it.
He got the gig after applying on Linkedin just over a year ago. He had a week of training and started out by covering Durand Cup matches at the Indira Gandhi Athletic Stadium in Guwahati.
On matchdays, Gavin takes a seat in a quieter section of the stadium and sets up a pair of smartphones. One records. The other is his work terminal. From there, he watches the pitch and tags almost everything that happens. Goal, penalty, free kick, corner, throw-in, shot, yellow card, or red card. The trick is to do it as soon as it happens. Or, at the very latest, before the livestream catches up. Red cards are the most important, then corners and shots, in that order. Miss an event or log it a mite late, and he may not get paid.
