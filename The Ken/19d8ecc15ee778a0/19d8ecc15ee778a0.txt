We are now well into ‘earnings season’, and strange things will happen.

Here are some headlines broadcasting the March 2026 results of the country’s second-largest fund manager on Monday evening.

– India’s ICICI Prudential AMC quarterly profit jumps on strong domestic inflows

– ICICI Pru AMC revenue jumps 23% YoY in Q4, profit at Rs 763 crore

– ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company posts 24% PAT jump in FY26

– ICICI Pru AMC Q4 results: Profit rises 10% YoY to Rs 763 cr; co declares Rs 12.4 dividend

Now, none of these are wrong. But they don’t give the full or even the real picture. 

Beneath the rosy headlines, ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company has had an underwhelming quarter, to put it mildly. Compared with the December 2025 quarter, on a sequential basis, that is, the fund manager’s revenue from operations was flat, its total income dipped 12%, and its net profit fell as much as 17%. Yes, that’s bad.

Why does this matter? 

Simply because ICICI Prudential AMC is no ordinary fund house. It has the second-highest market share in overall assets managed by the 50-odd mutual funds in India, next only to SBI Funds Management. More importantly, it has the highest market share in the categories that really matter and bring in the chunk of the revenue for fund managers—actively managed mutual funds, equity schemes, and equity hybrid schemes. Overall, ICICI Prudential AMC tops the charts in both revenue and profit. In short, it’s the bellwether of the Indian mutual fund industry.
