Good Morning Ishan,
On 29 August, I almost attended India’s first peptide party.
What was originally meant to be 50 guests in a venue in Bengaluru that was “NOT a Hospital”, per the invite, was cancelled one day before the event when the regulator, Karnataka Food Safety and Drug Administration, started asking questions.
In the lead-up to it, the party had quickly become the talk of the town. After all, this private gathering where guests openly try cocktails of “Chinese peptides”—another name for “research-grade” peptides bought on the grey market—was bringing Silicon Valley culture to India for the first time.
The excitement was palpable, in spite of the fact that peptides are nothing new.
A short chain of amino acids that’s essentially a smaller, lighter version of proteins, “peptides have been around in gym circles for years,” said an industry insider with a decade of experience across the pharma, nutrition, and wellness sectors. Beginning as trainer-recommended recovery drugs for tendon injuries, muscle damage, and gut healing, they have since graduated to something closer to biohacking.
But a lot of these wellness peptides sit in the regulatory grey zone—proliferating informally and even on D2C websites and B2B marketplaces like Indiamart without any certification…
