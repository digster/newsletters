Good Morning Ishan,
It was Mensa Brands earlier. Now it’s called Brnd.me. (Don’t ask me how one alphabet went missing in this rebranding.)
Different name, but the same business—a roll-up. Buy dozens of consumer-product brands, apply digital-marketing chops, grow them to a certain scale, and build a revenue-generating unicorn worth its valuation. That was what Ananth Narayanan, former CEO of Myntra and Medlife, set out to do when he started Mensa Brands in mid-2021.
The company has raised at least $300 million and holds the title of being the fastest startup to become a unicorn, in six months flat.
Much water has flowed under the roll-up bridge since then. To the extent that no one wants to be associated with “roll-up”. Narayanan makes sure Nuha, the author of today’s story, gets to hear from him several times. That it’s not a bouquet of brands, but a chosen few.
In FY25, four brands—aromatherapy-focused Majestic Pure, health-food firm Myfitness, personal-care products maker Botanic Hearth, and decoration-kits supplier Partypropz—drove nearly 60% of Brnd.me’s Rs 1,400 crore revenue, claimed the founder.
With an IPO on the horizon, how is the company tightening its belt? Importantly, why the rebranding?
