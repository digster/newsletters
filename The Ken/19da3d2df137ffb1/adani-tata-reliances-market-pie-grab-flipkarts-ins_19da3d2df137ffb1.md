The six largest ones control a fifth of the NSE500 profit pool, and the skew will only get worse
Just six family business groups in India account for nearly 20% of corporate profits among the NSE500 for FY25. In the last five years, all groups have expanded into newer businesses far more aggressively and comfortably than in the previous years.
