Good Morning Ishan,
The third time is the charm.
In late June, Prism finally filed its updated draft papers for a Rs 6,650 crore ($700 million) initial public offering (IPO) with market regulator Sebi. This is the Oyo* parent’s third attempt at going public after unsuccessful outings in 2021 and 2023.
What’s changed? Well, the entire pitch.
Prism, unlike its brand Oyo, is no longer about “standardising budget hotels” in India. It’s now an “asset-light” and “technology-driven” global hospitality platform that makes over 80% of its revenue from international markets.
Its numbers seem to justify its transformation.
In the nine months ended December 2025, Prism reported a top line of roughly Rs 7,000 crore, according to its updated draft prospectus. For the full year FY26, its revenue surged 50% from the year before to Rs 9,300 crore, said people familiar with the company’s financials. Its bottom line, too, has nearly quadrupled to over Rs 920 crore, they added. The Ken couldn’t independently verify these numbers.
It’s a great turnaround, especially for a company that recorded losses of over Rs 13,000 crore not so long ago. But Prism’s impressive financials are largely built on its debt-fuelled acquisitions.
