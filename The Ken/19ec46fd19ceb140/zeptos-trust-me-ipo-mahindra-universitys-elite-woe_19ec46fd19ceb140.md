Harshvardhan Lunia and family have filed an NCLT petition alleging foul play by Temasek subsidiary Fullerton. The case will be heard on 18 June
The founders of Uwards, the fintech company Lendingkart acquired in 2024, are now pursuing legal proceedings against Lendingkart, seeking compensation of Rs 80–90 crore. Between Q1 and Q3 of FY25, Lendingkart’s valuation plummeted from $700 million to less than $100 million. As a result, Fullerton, its largest investor, ousted founder and then-CEO Harshvardhan Lunia and proceeded to acquire the company. The company had prioritised scale above quality of lending during its rapid expansion during Covid. Founder Harshvardhan Lunia in a petition filed with NCLT has requested the tribunal to hand over control to a court-appointed committee, alleging Fullerton’s motive to be “mala fide” and “oppressive”.
