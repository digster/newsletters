[Bain Capital bet Rs 4,000 crore on gold. The RBI just walked all over it](https://the-ken.com/story/bain-capital-bet-rs-4000-crore-on-gold-the-rbi-just-walked-all-over-it/?utm_source=weekly_email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly_recap_card)
The regulator’s new rules that came into effect on 1 April undercut the country’s largest private-equity bet on gold-lending to date
Bain Capital is completing a Rs 4,385 crore bet on Manappuram Finance just as new RBI regulations threaten to reshape gold-lending economics for NBFCs. Manappuram’s gold-loan book grew 51% in three quarters, but its customer base stayed flat. The expansion was driven largely by rising gold prices, not new borrowers. The new RBI rules affect the bullet repayment loans that form a big chunk of NBFCs’ gold-loan AUM, and might end up squeezing their margins. Banks are rapidly gaining market share, new NBFCs are flooding in with 3,000 branches planned, and gold itself has corrected 20% from its January high.
