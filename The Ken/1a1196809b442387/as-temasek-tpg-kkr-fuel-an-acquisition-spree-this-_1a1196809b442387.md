Good Morning Ishan,
Exactly a year ago, The Eye Foundation—a 40-year-old eye-care chain based in Coimbatore—raised external equity for the first time. Its choice of investor, though, was rather unusual.
Most of its competitors had gravitated towards healthcare-focused private-equity funds, such as Temasek, Blackstone, TPG, and KKR, which have collectively invested over $15.5 billion in healthcare in India over the last five years. Of this, 60% went to hospitals.
The Eye Foundation, on the other hand, chose the consumer-brands-and-retail VC fund Verlinvest, which has backed the likes of yogurt brand Epigamia, home-and-sleep-solutions company Wakefit, and beauty-and-personal-care platform Purplle.
In October 2025, Verlinvest invested Rs 650 crore for a 20% stake in The Eye Foundation.
The eye-care chain’s founder and CEO, D Ramamurthy, acknowledged that choosing an FMCG fund was “unusual for the sector”. “But we did not want a fly-by-night operator—somebody with a definite timeline who wants to exit in five years,” he said.
The chain had quite a few “suitors”, as Ramamurthy put it, courtesy of its robust balance sheet. But it chose Verlinvest because it offered “a very good valuation”…
