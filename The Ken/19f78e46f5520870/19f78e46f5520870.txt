Good morning Ishan, hello. This is edition #156 of the First Principles newsletter. It is also the one where I need your help.

Because we need to rebuild FP.

Each edition now regularly spans many thousands of words of copy, features numerous writers and contributors, and includes lots of photos, books, movies, and links. It’s gotten a bit out of hand, if I’m being honest.

No, I’m not saying we’re doing too much. I’m fine with the volume as long as you are. Instead, the presentation and design of FP have now become our limiting factors.

Email clients cut off full editions because they’re often too long. There’s no easy way to browse or jump to the sections you find interesting, because there’s only so much you can do within an inbox. We can’t experiment with newer layouts because it forces us to design for the lowest common denominator—something that renders properly on most email clients or services. 

And don’t even get me started on discovering or browsing through past editions. Our design is still stuck in the “list of editions” https://the-ken.com/newsletters/first-principles/ mode that we cobbled together in May 2023, back when FP was a single-writer, single-topic newsletter. Mea culpa!

Then there’s so much I’d like us to do together. Run Quests (remember Parenting https://the-ken.com/newsletter/first-principles/the-parenting-special/? Fitness https://the-ken.com/newsletter/first-principles/first-principles-quest-special-the-meaning-of-fitness/?) with greater frequency. Feature more of your unique voices and ideas each week. Share cool recommendations with one another. Host meetups. Discover new books. Debate AI use.

But I’m—we are—held back because of our current design. It feels like we’re driving a freewheeling, fun-filled bus 🚌 on a road built three years ago to handle a bicycle 🚲 😄.

For any other product at The Ken, this would be a problem for us to solve. Not First Principles though, and you know why. So, let’s rebuild this together.

This Sunday (or Monday), I request that you take 10 minutes of your time to tell me how we should think about FP for, say, the next 3 years. There’s nothing else in today’s edition. No books, no movies, no photos. Just this.

How do we keep what’s good about FP while building around it for the future? Something that makes it easier for all of us, myself included, to explore, discover, read, connect, and participate around topics and themes that interest us most. How do we rebuild the FP experience into a place where we’d love to hang out every weekend, and possibly a few other days of the week, too?

I’ve created 21 questions for this survey, most of them single taps. The three open-ended ones at the start matter the most, so please give those your unhurried Sunday brain. 

At the end there’s an invitation to join the new FP Reader Panel, a group I’m setting up that will get to see half-built pages and early designs before anyone else. 

I’ll run the results in a coming edition, of course. That’s how this whole rebuild is going to work. Because that is the FP way 😊.
