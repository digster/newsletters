Good Morning Ishan,
Walk into any PVR Inox multiplex and you will probably find more posters of Ranveer Singh from Dhurandhar clad in Ajio merchandise than displays for overpriced popcorn. If you fancy sitting through trailers and intervals, you will likely see a stream of ads for Jio Hotstar’s Peacock Hub. The Indian theatre, it would seem, is Reliance’s stage.
And Jio Studios is the conductor.
The production house under Reliance’s media and entertainment division is booming, delivering commercial hits as if off a conveyor belt—Stree 2, Laapataa Ladies, and now Dhurandhar.
Stree 2, released in 2024, was the highest-grossing Hindi film ever, raking in Rs 698 crore, before it was surpassed by the phenomenon that was Dhurandhar, with the two movies in the franchise making about Rs 2,200 crore.
It’s a success story, alright.
In FY26, per Reliance’s financial-results presentation, Jio Studios became the largest production house by revenue, catalogue size, and box-office share.
The report dedicates two pages to the impact of Dhurandhar and notes that it’s likely to be the first Hindi film franchise to cross Rs 3,000 crore in worldwide box-office earnings.
This isn’t just about margins, though. For Reliance, Jio Studios is the piece that completes its media-empire puzzle that encompasses news, TV, streaming, and cinema. Which also explains its success…
