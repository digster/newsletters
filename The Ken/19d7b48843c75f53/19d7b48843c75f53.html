Hi! This is Rohin. I wear many hats at The Ken. 

But at this time tomorrow, I will exclusively be the host and moderator of an event, an off-shoot of our all-things-AI podcast and newsletter, Zero Shot. The agenda for this one is Agentic AI and what it could or might do to the Indian consumer internet space over the next 24 months. A few tickets are still available, but not for long. 

Whether you are a user or an investor in this space, casual or hardcore, there is a good chance that whatever unfolds in this sector will affect you one way or another. Through your habits. Your workflow. Or your job. But that’s just at a personal level. 

“I can see our society becoming even more divided and lonelier.”

“It feels like I am being asked to write a mini Black Mirror Episode.”

“Monday, April 2028. Just another manic Monday. Or not. I wake up to confirm three requests my agent sorted overnight.”

This is a peek into the minds of the community gathering tomorrow morning. If you want to be part of imagining what the future, or the near-present, is going to look like, head over to our event page and join an evolving discussion where your voice helps decide what we explore. 

Once you buy a ticket, you can even vote on the Agentic AI-driven scenarios that we should discuss together. 

Little drops of water make the mighty ocean. Our little personal lives and habits make big companies like Zomato, Phonepe, Makemytrip, and others. As people we are never separate from the aftershocks of what makes or breaks the big companies that influence our lifestyle. 

We have set some ground rules that will definitely make the event interesting for all of us:

  • All scenarios are set within 24 months (mid-2026 to mid-2028)
  • “Grounded speculation”—every scenario starts from real data, real companies, real market structures
  • First-principles thinking—we ask “why not?” instead of “why?”
  • Companies, not sectors. We name names: Zomato, Flipkart, Phonepe, Urban Company, instead of “the food delivery space”.
  • Market leaders only. We stress-test incumbents, not imagine hypothetical startups
  • No credit for innovation. We deliberately do not assume existing companies will adapt brilliantly. If they do, great. But the interesting question is: what if they don’t?
  • India only. Every scenario is grounded in Indian markets, Indian consumers, Indian infrastructure.

We promise you a riveting Sunday morning, as good as a marathon or open-water swimming, but for your brain. No sitting and just listening to discussions. At every point, you will be asked to share your thoughts, opinions, votes, and rankings. 

Come experience a one-of-a-kind event at Bangalore International Centre presented by The Ken!

See you there!
