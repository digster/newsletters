Across 14 cases, the trail after fraud detection leads to frozen accounts, disputed ownership, court battles, and money that does not always make it home
A flagged account can be frozen even when only a small part of its balance is linked to a fraud trail, leaving legit customers to challenge the restraint. Banks can initiate their own fraud reviews, while police and I4C can trigger separate actions, creating different routes for the same suspicious account. Establishing who actually controlled a mule account can be harder than identifying the account itself, particularly when account holders, recruiters, and fraud operators are different people. Even when money is lien-marked, or a court orders its restoration, competing claims and gaps between banks, police and victims can delay the final recovery.
