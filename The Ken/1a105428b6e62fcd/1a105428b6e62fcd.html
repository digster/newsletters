Hello Ishan, I had big plans for today’s email. Because, yesterday—3 October—marked 10 years since we published our first paywalled story. We consider it our “birth”, because on that day in 2016, we gathered the courage to also launch our paywall, business model and subscriber promise.

I said I had big plans because I came down with a bad flu earlier this week after quick trips to Mumbai and Delhi. I’m still recovering and in no state to write sweeping, nostalgia-tinged notes about the decade that was. (Just as well, some of you will be rightly thinking!).

So, in the spirit of Bengaluru’s autumn rain (yes, the playlist is due too!), allow me a rain check for this Sunday. I’ll be back next week with a (promise) non-sappy edition to make up.

It’s been a long weekend in India. So, if you still have some lie-back energy left, spend some of it on the latest episode of our Intermission video podcast. It’s on the company that in so many ways saved the Tata Group.
