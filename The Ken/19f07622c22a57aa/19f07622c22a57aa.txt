This week on the Zero Shot podcast

While we wait for the column, I have a movie recommendation for you this Saturday. 

It’s the 2016 Bollywood film Kapoor and Sons. In one line, it is a family drama. But as with all family dramas, one line cannot capture the layers, dynamics, and conflicts it holds. It’s great, gets better with every rewatch, and makes me wonder if there is a film about my family somewhere, too. 

I watched the movie again this week after speaking with the film’s director, Shakun Batra, for Zero Shot’s latest episode. Shakun is at an interesting juncture right now. He is among the most vocal directors in India regarding AI and is bullish on what the technology offers. He knows the tools like the back of his hand and is currently developing long-form content using AI. 

Shakun was joined by Bangkok-based ad filmmaker Kobayashi, who has an AI-native production company. When we paired the two of them together, our attempt was to explore two approaches to a technological advancement. One had built an entire practice around AI from scratch, and the other had grafted it onto an auteur career built over years. 

We expected some level of divergence. But both arrived at the same place: Production is getting easier. At the same time, the bar for storytelling is rising, too.

This was a different and refreshing episode of Zero Shot. I took away a lot from it, as I am sure you will too.
