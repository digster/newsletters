[Sniffing dogs join the race to catch cancer early in India](https://the-ken.com/story/sniffing-dogs-join-the-race-to-catch-cancer-early-in-india/?utm_source=weekly_email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly_recap_card)
D2C wellness firms are selling urban rich Rs 25,000 full-body scans that promise more than they can deliver. Dognosis is betting on, well, dog noses to attain the holy grail of cancer screening at a third of the cost
Dognosis is using trained dogs and Bayesian statistics to detect cancer early. By calling Breatheeasy, its first health-monitoring service launching next year, a “triaging” tool, the Bengaluru startup sidesteps regulatory approval. While some experts question whether using animals in this manner is scalable, Dognosis claims that just 15 dogs can screen 2,50,000 patients a year. In a country where less than 1% of the population is screened for cancer, Dognosis is banking on undercutting expensive and invasive tests to make inroads.
