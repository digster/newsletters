“We don’t track Nykaa Fashion, just its beauty vertical,” said an executive at the e-commerce major Myntra.

On his screen was an excel sheet detailing the gross merchandise value (GMV) of competitors that Myntra actively keeps an eye on, including Flipkart Fashion, Amazon Fashion, Reliance Retail’s Ajio, and Meesho. Former Myntra CEO Nandita Sinha would also refer to this list during business meetings. 

Nykaa Fashion’s name, though, was conspicuously absent. To find it, you’d have to use the drop-down menu, select “others”, and then tap on Nykaa to see its numbers. This tells you all you need to know about where Nykaa Fashion stands among its peers.

But its fate now seems to be turning.

In FY26, Nykaa Fashion’s GMV jumped 30% year-on-year to cross Rs 4,900 crore. In Q1 FY27, the vertical also became profitable for the first time, its Ebitda margin turning positive at 0.1% from -6.2% a year earlier.

No wonder in early August, Nykaa’s share price touched its highest since January 2022—with the fortunes of its fashion vertical widely considered a factor. 

So how, exactly, did Nykaa become fashion-forward?
