The Iran war has created headaches for everyone, including Finolex Cables. Yet that is not its biggest problem.

For a cable-maker that was the first to join the party, Finolex Cables has ceded significant ground to companies that entered the industry much later. 

Founded in 1958, the company still holds nearly a quarter of the organised wires market, but that’s only part of the story. Overall, it seems to have missed the infrastructure boom that its peers are capitalising on, as real estate sentiment revives and data centers surge. But with the recent increase in the price of raw materials, catching up isn’t going to be easy. 

This shows up in its numbers.
