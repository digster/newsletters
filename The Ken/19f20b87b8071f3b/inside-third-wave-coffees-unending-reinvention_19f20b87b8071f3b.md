Good Morning Ishan,
The Third Wave Coffee Roasters in Indiranagar sports a bright pink sign under the awning, saying Third Rush desserts.
It ushers people in to try the Bengaluru-based specialty coffee chain’s latest offering—Jim Jam tres leches, pineapple cake, and some 200-odd dessert options. Similar signs decorate the Third Wave outlets in Koramangala, Sadashivnagar, and Vijaya Bank Layout, with plans to expand to around 50 cafes by the end of the year.
This is the latest in what has been a series of experiments and reinventions for the chain. First, it wanted to open outlets near Starbucks cafés, then it doubled down on its food menu, including pizzas and paneer tikka wraps. Now, it’s going all in on desserts.
The founder of a rival chain put it simply. “No coffee place can rely on coffee alone, because coffee consumption is not that high. The whole idea is, you have to be able to extract more revenue from customers.”
Third Wave has, in fact, been struggling. In FY24, the company posted a loss of Rs 150 crore on revenue of around Rs 250 crore. Close to 70% of its stores were loss-making. This is after the chain had raised close to Rs 665 crore ($70 million) across 10 funding rounds. Investors started second-guessing its strategy.
