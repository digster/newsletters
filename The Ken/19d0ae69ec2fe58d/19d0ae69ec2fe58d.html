Hello Ishan,

Big changes are taking place in how the internet works, and every facet of your life—personal, professional, and everything in between—will feel the impact.

The shift has a name: agentic AI. It’s coming for every business model built on human attention and interaction. That means food delivery, e-commerce, fintech, SaaS, advertising, and everything else that you use.

This interregnum raises uncomfortable questions:

  • When agents order on your behalf and bypass aggregators such as Swiggy and Zomato, what happens to companies that previously capitalised on your eyeballs?
  • Who will you trust enough to act as your agent—Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, or a startup you’ve never heard of?
  • If AI agents complete UPI transactions invisibly, do Phonepe, Paytm, and Cred become irrelevant? Does it matter which intermediary handles those transactions?
  • What does SIP investing or stock trading look like when an agent acts on your behalf without you ever opening the Zerodha or Groww apps?
  • Will ONDC see a revival in the restaurant space, once restaurants are able to accept and fulfil orders directly from customers’ AI agents?

There aren’t any clean answers, but some sharp operators and founders are thinking about these new conditions, just like you and everyone at The Ken. That’s why we want to seat them together in a room and have an open, unfiltered discussion.

They’ll be at The Ken’s next live event on Sunday, April 12, at Bangalore International Centre. 

The discussion will be moderated by my colleagues and fellow Zero Shot hosts Praveen Gopal Krishnan and Rohin Dharmakumar. 

But there are some differences from our events held in 2025. 

This time, you can expect more than a standard panel of guests. We want as many viewpoints and disagreements as possible. And we’re bringing you, the audience, into the fold: you’ll get to shape the discussion’s direction in real time.

It’s an ambitious format that hasn’t been tried in India before. We promise you’ll leave with strong impressions and stronger thoughts about the future.
