I sometimes use VPNs to watch Netflix shows that aren’t available in India. But until last month, I had never used one for Youtube.

As someone who spends an embarrassing amount of time on the platform, it was surprising that I’d missed a tech controversy. One that was purposely made invisible. Only after a video from British-Indian creator Arun Maini appeared on my feed did it catch my attention.

The controversy centred on AI+ smartphones, launched by deep-tech firm Nxtquantum, founded by former Realme CEO and co-founder Madhav Sheth. The company had marketed the phones as India’s privacy-first “Made in India” solution to rival Chinese brands. But content creators Techwiser and Techbar questioned AI+’s privacy claims by highlighting that the phones came with pre-installed Chinese-linked apps.
