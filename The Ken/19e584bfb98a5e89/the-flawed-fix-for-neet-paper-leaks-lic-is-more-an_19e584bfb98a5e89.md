The government is moving NEET online to fight question paper leaks, but the real problem might not have to do with the mode of exam
The NEET exam this year was cancelled within a week amid concerns of a question paper leak. This has put the focus back on the NTA, which was already in hot water when NEET question papers leaked two years ago. As a quick response, the education ministry announced that NEET will be a computer-based test from now on. But the question remains on how far NTA has followed up on the Radhakrishnan Commission’s suggestions, which were put forth in 2024. As drones shrink delivery times and logistics costs, D2C brands are beginning to see a way around e-commerce giants
Drone delivery in India is moving past the pilot stage, with operators like Skye Air and Airbound now running daily commercial routes. Drone delivery unit-economics tend to be sensitive to demand volumes and are more likely to struggle in quick commerce, while mid-mile use cases have seen meaningful adoption. Among notable buyers of mid-mile drone delivery are D2C brands, for whom drones offer one way to approach marketplace delivery speeds without investing as much in road-bound infrastructure. However, government regulations around drone usage are hindering the scaling of these services, especially as large airspaces are marked as no-fly zones in cities.
