For companies selling household essentials in India, the past few years were like a nightmare they struggled to wake up from.
When Sudhir Sitapati stepped down as chief executive of Godrej Consumer Products two weeks ago, he [said](https://x.com/NeerajCNBC/status/2087150398209687680) India was emerging from a “difficult period for FMCG”. He added that Godrej’s total shareholder return during his five-year run exceeded that of the Nifty FMCG index, but the difference was hardly headline-grabbing.
But for another large FMCG company, the passing of the baton had more questions than just the state of the sector.
Colgate-Palmolive (India) CEO Prabha Narasimhan, who joined the company from Hindustan Unilever (HUL) in 2022, is taking up an Asia-Pacific role at the oral-care giant. Her successor, Manish Anandani, is returning to Colgate at what is, undoubtedly, a tricky time for the company.
“The past five years have been the worst for Colgate in twenty years,” said a former long-time FMCG executive.
