The most aggressive acquisition strategy in Indian healthcare has bruised Manipal’s margins, but some essential metrics could surprise the critics

For the longest time, Ranjan Pai has been concerned about the single-digit profit margins of listed hospitals in India. He thinks that only if hospitals do well, more investors will come. By paying down some or all of the debt from the IPO proceeds, Manipal will improve its margins.
