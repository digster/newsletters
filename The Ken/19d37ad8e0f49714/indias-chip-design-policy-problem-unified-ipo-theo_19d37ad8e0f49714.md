India is readying phase-2 of its flagship local chip-design-support scheme even as the first phase hasn’t hit its target. The most aggressive acquisition strategy in Indian healthcare has bruised Manipal’s margins, but some essential metrics could surprise the critics
For the longest time, Ranjan Pai has been concerned about the single-digit profit margins of listed hospitals in India. He thinks that only if hospitals do well, more investors will come. His massive consolidation drive has made Manipal the #1 hospital by hospital beds, but has also dented its margins. By paying down some or all of the debt from the IPO proceeds, Manipal will improve its margins. The rest of the ducks are in a row for Pai to prove to public and private investors that listed hospitals can do well.
