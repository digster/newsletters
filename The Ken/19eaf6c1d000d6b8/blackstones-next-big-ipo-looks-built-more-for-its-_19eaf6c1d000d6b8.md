Good Morning Ishan,
When the world’s largest property owner is about to list yet another batch of buildings in India, you don’t expect much of a plot twist.
Blackstone has been listing real estate in India almost every year since 2019. In fact, the firm listed the country’s first-ever Reit, which has since become the standard exit playbook for institutional owners of Indian real estate.
Blackstone has run that playbook so many times, and so profitably, that it closed its largest-ever Asia fund of $13 billion in early June. This was twice the size of its predecessor. The money came from private investors abroad, gladly buying into the same India success story that the firm’s earlier listings had turned into proof of concept.
That very same day, Blackstone was out raising money in India too, only this room looked nothing like the last. It comprised public investors gathered for the roadshow of its next real-estate listing. And the pitch they were hearing was a complete departure from Blackstone’s usual script.
Horizon Industrial Parks, the pitch went, is the country’s largest collection of grade-A warehouses, each the size of several football fields, leased to blue-chip companies such as Flipkart and Decathlon. It’s made up of 45 assets, spread across 10 cities, totalling 58 million square feet, all to be assembled under a soon-to-be-listed platform.
