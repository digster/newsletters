Blissclub, the women’s functional apparel brand, has secured a new high-profile backer. 

This month, Vidit Aatrey, Meesho’s co-founder and CEO, invested in his wife’s venture as part of its Series B round, which was led by Singularity AMC and raised Rs 160 crore. 

The Indian consumer-tech ecosystem is a tight-knit circle, small enough that most founders have raised money from each other at some point. Swiggy’s Sriharsha Majety, for instance, has backed Kunal Shah’s Cred while Mamaearth’s Ghazal Alagh has backed half a dozen startups outside her own category. 

Blissclub fits squarely within this network as well. But that’s not the only trick up its sleeve.
