“I’m embarrassed about the first product we launched,” said Utkarsh Gupta, co-founder of Comet, over a Whatsapp call, on his way back from opening the sneaker brand’s second store in Bengaluru.
Early this month, Comet raised Rs 100 crore in a Series B led by Verlinvest, with existing investors Elevation Capital and Nexus Venture Partners doubling down. The round valued the company at around Rs 535 crore—more than triple its Rs 167 crore valuation after its 2024 Series A.
When it comes to global brands, these are small numbers, but Comet is confident it’s time to take them on.
“We don’t see anybody who has reached full scale and can be considered to have product-market fit. In general, all of Comet’s competition today comes from global brands, primarily Puma, Adidas, and Nike,” said Gupta.
