[New-age venture capital would rather manufacture founders than find them](https://the-ken.com/story/new-age-venture-capital-would-rather-manufacture-founders-than-find-them/?utm_source=weekly_recap&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly_recap_card)
From Entrepreneurs First’s co-founder speed dates to The Foundery’s idea vault, a new wave of venture studios believe that plug-and-play founders are safer for their portfolios
The venture studio model is in vogue. Instead of backing founders who arrive with their own ideas, studios build companies in-house to cut early-stage risk. The formats vary widely, from EF’s 8% stake to The Foundery’s 75%. In each case, the aim is to de-risk the portfolio by turning company-building into a systematic, repeatable process. The model rewards speed, which pulls studios towards businesses that can be built and validated fast, such as AI products for the US market. But speed can cost what organic company-building allows time for, like real chemistry between co-founders. Some studios have dropped the outside founder altogether, and hire executives to run the companies they create. Studios pitch themselves as an asset class in their own right, but investor appetite in India is yet to catch up.
