Good Morning Ishan,
LMGTFY or “Let me google that for you”—a snark putting down people for asking lazy questions—is becoming more passe by the day now. It’s now more like LMALLMFY (Let me ask an LLM for you), as Indians have changed their search behaviour from Googling things to prompting LLMs.
ChatGPT, currently at around 800 million monthly active users, and Gemini, at around 750 million, are both growing at nearly 200% year-on-year. And this shift in user behaviour is reflected in how companies digitally advertise.
“SEO is no longer the only thing that brands focus on. Increasingly, AEO (answer engine optimisation) and GEO (generative engine optimisation) matter as well,” said Sanditya Srivastava, the founder of AEO intelligence platform Unveilr AI.
But that means the internet’s decadal economic engine of search > click > website > convert is sputtering. Read Suprit’s [story today](https://the-ken.com/story/ai-just-broke-brands-google-ad-strategy-the-answer-is-the-ad/?utm_source=daily_story&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=daily_newsletter) on how brands like Flipkart and Mamaearth are navigating the brave new world of LLMs and what the financial tradeoffs look like in this new order.
