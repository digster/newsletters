Good Morning Ishan,
I’m a firm believer in the idea that domestic workers should be impossible for most households to afford, but there are companies in India that are doing their best to counter that. Here are their offers:
Urban Company’s Instahelp charges Rs 66 for three one-hour sessions of someone doing your chores.
Snabbit’s rate for three visits is Rs 198.
Pronto’s fee for the first visit is a symbolic Re 1, then Rs 25 for every 30 minutes.
Consumers appear to be lapping it up. Instahelp had a modest 6,000 orders in the first three months of 2025; it ended the year with 1.6 million orders in the October-December quarter.
Read this as the latest service category where investors are subsidising your lifestyle. But we know what that means: money is sunk in, there are big promises about speed and cheap prices, that’s followed by a race to the bottom, and it isn’t entirely clear there will be a payoff.
Suprita analyses the situation in today’s [story](https://the-ken.com/story/how-urban-company-turned-the-tables-on-snabbit/?utm_source=daily_story&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=daily_newsletter).
