Midway through a conversation with a former senior executive of Phonepe Invest, his phone beeps. He’s just gotten a Substack notification from Zerodha’s* co-founder Nithin Kamath, who had put up a post about how profitable retail futures traders really are.

To the executive, the notification made his point. “Whether it’s Lalit Keshre at Groww or Pravin Jadhav at Dhan, they lead from the front,” he told The Ken. “But at Phonepe? You won’t find anyone debating investment strategy and user behaviour.” The broking platform of India’s largest UPI app chases revenue through sheer marketing, not by going deeper into its products, he added.

This is just one of the platform’s many problems. Perhaps that’s why earlier in October, it dropped its erstwhile name, Share.market, and rebranded itself as Phonepe Invest—in a bid to change the narrative.

Not that that will help much. It all comes down to execution. 

The platform handles some Rs 6,000 crore-worth of mutual-fund distribution. It executes 2.3 million SIPs every month and has onboarded over 1.3 million demat accounts, 37% of which came via Phonepe. The payments app, itself, has over 700 million users, of which 300 million are active, and commands 48% of the UPI-payments market. 

That’s surely impressive. But the company has struggled to cross-sell across its platforms. Nine of the 11 current and former Phonepe executives The Ken spoke to, alongside five analysts and industry experts, agreed that Phonepe Invest could do with a sharper focus on core investment products…
