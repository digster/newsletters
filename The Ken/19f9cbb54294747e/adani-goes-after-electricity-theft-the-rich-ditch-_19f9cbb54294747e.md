[Adani pays Rs 3,000 crore to outwit electricity-stealing Indians](https://the-ken.com/story/adani-pays-rs-3000-crore-to-outwit-electricity-stealing-indians/?utm_source=weekly_email+&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly_recap_card)
After Adani Energy’s acquisition of smart-meter company Intellismart, the combined entity now manages a book of 47 million smart meters or 23% of the total sanctioned rollout
India’s Rs 3 lakh crore RDSS scheme has made smart meters one of the country’s biggest infrastructure rollouts, with private AMISPs financing, installing and operating them for state DISCOMs. Adani Energy’s Rs 3,050 crore acquisition of IntelliSmart is less about manufacturing smart meters and more about owning the long-term contracts that run them. The rollout is becoming increasingly difficult to ignore because financially stretched DISCOMs need accurate billing, lower losses and better visibility over electricity consumption. Power theft remains one of the biggest drains on the sector. Smart meters won’t eliminate it overnight, but they make it far harder to steal electricity undetected, turning leakage into a business opportunity.
