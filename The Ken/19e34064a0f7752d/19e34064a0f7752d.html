Can ‘drug visas’ soon outgrow drug manufacturing? This pharma company is betting on it

Factory-less HRV Pharma has turned managing regulatory paperwork into a successful standalone business

India has long manufactured generic drugs for the world but that moat may have reached its limit. The US and Europe now want newer and more complex drug molecules, which aren’t just harder to make but also harder to keep onside of compliance with regulatory bodies like FDA. As regulatory needs have tightened, Indian pharma’s next moat may be shifting upwards, from factories to compliance. One virtual company is betting big on this by building its business around ‘drug visas’. [Read the full story]

Is Swiggy snatching defeat from the jaws of victory?

Once a pioneer, Instamart now takes 15 months to add as many users as Blinkit does in three. Swiggy’s growing losses are no comfort, either

During the latest earnings call, Sriharsha Majety, Swiggy’s founder and CEO, reaffirmed the company’s position that Instamart is not going to buy growth. In FY26, however, Swiggy spent more money on advertisements and promotions, and its losses increased by over 30%. While Swiggy’s food delivery and out-of-home segments have been Ebitda positive, the company is losing ground in the crucial quick commerce segment, despite being the first entrant. Swiggy has traditionally been strong in product design and innovation; however, lately, it seems to be struggling to communicate effectively with users, according to experts. [Read full story here]

From Bengaluru to Mumbai to Pune, metro projects are colliding with India’s monsoons

Cities building metros acquire land, divert drains, and tear up roads. Fixing what the rain does to all of it is somebody else’s problem

Every monsoon, major cities flood along the same corridors where metros are being constructed. Metro corporations are structures to answer to the state and centre, not to city governments, so no one is accountable for the drainage disruption they cause. The insurance and contracts are designed to protect the metro bodies and move the work along, so it’s nearly impossible for affected parties to recover damages. No metro project in the country has ever modelled how the construction would alter the city’s drainage system. [Read the full story]
