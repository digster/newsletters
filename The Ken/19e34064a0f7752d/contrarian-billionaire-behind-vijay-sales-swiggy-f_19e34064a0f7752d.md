[From Bengaluru to Mumbai to Pune, metro projects are colliding with India’s monsoons](https://the-ken.com/story/urban-flooding-along-metro-corridors/?utm_source=weekly_email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly_recap_card)
Cities building metros acquire land, divert drains, and tear up roads. Fixing what the rain does to all of it is somebody else’s problem
Every monsoon, major cities flood along the same corridors where metros are being constructed. Metro corporations are structures to answer to the state and centre, not to city governments, so no one is accountable for the drainage disruption they cause. The insurance and contracts are designed to protect the metro bodies and move the work along, so it’s nearly impossible for affected parties to recover damages. No metro project in the country has ever modelled how the construction would alter the city’s drainage system.
