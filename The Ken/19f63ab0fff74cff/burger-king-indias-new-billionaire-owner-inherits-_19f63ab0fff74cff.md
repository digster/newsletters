Good Morning Ishan,
Aayush Agrawal wasn’t the buyer many expected for Restaurant Brands Asia, the listed company that runs Burger King outlets in India and Indonesia.
The 37-year-old is part of the family behind Ajanta Pharma, the nearly Rs 43,000 crore mid-sized pharmaceutical company.
But he isn’t just parachuting into the restaurant business. Rather, he’s been building brands of his own for more than a decade. These include Chinese Wok, Big Bowl, and The Momo Co. All of them are housed inside Lenexis Foodwork, the quick-service restaurant company that’s now taken over Restaurant Brands Asia from its previous owner, Everstone Capital.
Earlier in July, the Burger King operator informed stock exchanges that the acquisition was complete.
It seems like a good bet, given that Restaurant Brands Asia has built a far better business over the past five years than the one its investors first bought into.
Its restaurant-level profitability has more than doubled, gross margins have climbed to record highs, and the burger chain has delivered five straight quarters of same-store-sales growth (SSSG). This, even as consumers cut discretionary spending and rival chains struggled to bring diners back.
Yet, Restaurant Brands Asia’s stock trades close to its IPO price of Rs 60.
The answer partly lies in Indonesia, where an acquisition by the previous owner remains a weak spot.
….
The other part has to do with Agrawal.
