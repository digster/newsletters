Last week, Google began testing buying and shopping from Walmart-owned Flipkart inside the AI interface in India, according to a Techcrunch report https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/26/google-tests-buying-from-walmart-owned-flipkart-through-gemini-and-ai-mode-in-india/. 

Though Google or anyone would have liked no one to step out of their ecosystem, especially with agentic AI big in the works, companies are realising that if you can’t beat them, you join them. 

And everyone is desperate to be visible inside chatbots. For good reason. 

To give you an example, my mom wanted a particular kind of oil and asked ChatGPT for recommendations. 

It gave her a shortlist of four; she bought one. 

For a 62-year-old, she said, it’s a saviour: “Why scroll through 100 oils on Blinkit when I can just ask?”
