The Global Fintech Fest (GFF) just concluded last week, on 11 September. Everyone, from the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Sebi), the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), and state governments, to the likes of Nandan Nilekani, was gaga about one thing. Tokenisation.
At the 2025 GFF, the RBI launched Unified Markets Interface (UMI), introducing the idea of tokenising financial assets using Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC). At this year’s edition, the major highlight was tokenising physical assets using CBDCs.
It’s seen as a breakthrough because, until now, physical assets couldn’t easily be transacted using money. Tokenising them with proper descriptors brings them onto the same digital footing.
