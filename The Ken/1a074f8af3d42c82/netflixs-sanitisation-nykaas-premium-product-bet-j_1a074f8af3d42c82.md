The Safe City Project is a privacy nightmare, but over 180 police stations are nearly ready to match your face to your voter’s ID, driver’s licence, passport, and more
The Delhi police’s Rs 857-crore Safe City Project was started in the name of women’s safety, but became a mass surveillance system. The system is supposed to track criminals. But it can as easily identify ordinary citizens and vehicles. The police have built a data lake of the personal details and pictures of nearly four million individuals by integrating over two dozen databases held by various government agencies. The surveillance system operates in a legal vacuum and, experts say, runs counter to the Supreme Court’s ruling recognising privacy as a fundamental right.
