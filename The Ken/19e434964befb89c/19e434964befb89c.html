Sometime in 2022, as Honasa Consumer—skincare brand Mamaearth’s parent—prepared to go public, its co-founder Varun Alagh made two unusual bets on logistics.

The first was on Shiprocket, an e-commerce shipping aggregator that promised independent brands they could build their own delivery networks without relying entirely on Amazon or Flipkart. The second was on a far stranger proposition: a small Delhi-based drone startup called Skye Air Mobility.

At the time, more than a third of Honasa’s sales still came through large online marketplaces. 

Alagh’s wager was not really about drones, but about whether brands could eventually escape the infrastructure advantage of marketplaces. Four years later, those two bets have converged.

In 2026, Shiprocket integrated Skye Air into its same-day-delivery network. Together, they now move parcels for brands like Mamaearth, Giva, Lenskart, Bombay Shaving Company, and Frido. In Gurugram alone, Skye Air says it completes more than 6,000 drone deliveries a day, ferrying everything from groceries and medicines to iPhones across the city.

And the who’s who of Indian e-commerce and logistics make up their client list: Flipkart, Zepto, Bigbasket, Tata 1mg, Blue Dart, and DTDC, among others. 

Skye Air is hardly an outlier. Commercial drone deliveries are only getting popular.
