At the turn of the century, ITC chose three businesses that would allow it to prepare for a world where neither the consumer nor the taxman was going to be kind to its main product, cigarettes. Of the three, it became the largest in the private sector in one (agribusiness) and the second biggest in another (FMCG). The third bet, however, has been forgettable at best, no matter how much ITC has tried to change course in recent years. 

After a quarter-century in existence, ITC Infotech’s revenue is less than Rs 5,000 crore. Were it listed, it would be among the smallest firms in its peer group. As my colleague Anand wrote https://the-ken.com/story/itc-infotech-the-hidden-it-gem-that-could-make-itcs-stock-shine/ in 2021.

Despite being set up in the halcyon days of 2000, ITC Infotech has largely missed the software wave that churned out many Indian IT giants over the last two decades. The business was simply not a priority for ITC Ltd, according to an expert on the sector… 

At one point, ITC was sitting on the fence about the future of ITC Infotech, added the expert. The stakes weren’t high—it hadn’t invested much into ITC Infotech, and relatively new entrants to the sector were quickly surpassing the backbencher. Even as engineering-construction-manufacturing behemoth Larsen & Toubro doubled down on IT subsidiary LTI, peers such as the Aditya Birla Group exited the sector.

But in the late 2010s, ITC Infotech, under a new leader, changed...
