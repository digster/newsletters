Accenture and TCS have changed how they pay out salary hikes and bonuses. Others are likely next. The new structure doesn’t hurt today. A smaller fixed salary also means bigger questions around loan eligibility, housing investments, and other emotional milestones. Behind the changes is an industry preparing for leaner times and costlier labour laws.
