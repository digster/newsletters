What looks like a minor tweak to salary structures is eroding the financial certainty that defined India’s tech jobs, with ripple effects on home loans, savings, and long-term financial planning

Accenture and TCS have changed how they pay out salary hikes and bonuses. Others are likely next. The new structure doesn’t hurt today.
