Good Morning Ishan,
For eight years, Sandlogic has operated in the background.
An “invisible AI plumber”, as its work might be described, powering use cases for companies like auto and components maker Aisin-Toyota, frozen-food logistics firm US Cold Storage, non-bank Bajaj Finance, and fertility-clinic chain Indira IVF. It built tools to parse dense RFP documents in hours instead of weeks, score IVF patient leads based on “urgency, financial readiness, and treatment awareness”, and run voice agents for call centres.
Sandlogic’s work has rarely made headlines, but it has replaced manual processes. Until recently, the Bengaluru-based company also made money doing so.
Then it changed course. It chose to give up profitability and pour resources into building something far more ambitious: its own AI chips, models, and software stack. With an overall funding of just $4 million and last-reported revenue of a little over Rs 1 crore ($120,000), Sandlogic’s founders now want to build “the Nvidia of India”.
That claim is… interesting.
