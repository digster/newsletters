In October 2024, when it was sure it had a compelling enough growth story, it went public. And it began trading 70% higher than its IPO price. Roughly a year later, its market value crossed Rs 1 lakh crore.
The company has since lost a quarter of its valuation, but it’s worth more than twice as much as the nearest listed competitor, Premier Energies. And it trades at a far higher price-to-earnings multiple than another prominent rival, Vikram Solar.
The solar-panel maker has since bounced back sharply and is once again worth more than Rs 1 lakh crore. Waaree shares are up 17% this year, while the Nifty Midcap 50 index is flat.
This is not the only stock from the Waaree Group that has run counter to the broader indices. Waaree Renewable Technologies, a subsidiary of Waaree Energies that builds solar farms, is up 11% this year, compared with a 2% fall in the Nifty Microcap 250 index.
There’s yet another Waaree company that has beaten the odds this year. Until last week, Indosolar, a rival Waaree Energies bought in 2022, had lost none of its market value this year. Yesterday, the stock was down 5% after its revenue halved in the December quarter from the same period in 2025, even though profits were up.
