Good Morning Ishan,
L&T Technology Services (LTTS) is betting the house on AI. Not entirely metaphorically.
In 2023, the engineering-services firm took over its parent’s Smart World and Communication’s (SWC) business with the aim of expanding its offerings across telecom, cybersecurity, and smart city projects. In September, it sold the business to Hyderabad-based AMI Paradigm Solutions for a shade over Rs 450 crore, booking a Rs 350 crore loss. And SWC was no peripheral enterprise. At Rs 1,028 crore, it accounted for 10% of the company’s total revenue in FY25 and 7% of profit.
So, why sell?
LTTS is pivoting hard to AI-focused projects, which it calculates offer better returns than the low-margin segments that SWC mainly caters to. It’s a small sample, but the results seem encouraging. Over the last three quarters, the Ebitda margin has grown year-on-year from 17.5% in Q2 FY26 to 19.3% in Q2 FY27, while quarterly revenue has crossed Rs 3,000 crore.
The transition is already well underway. “Large-deal technology crossover ventures (TCV) exceeded $200 million across segments, with AI-led deals accounting for more than 50% of the total,” CEO Amit Chadha reported on the Q2 FY27 earnings call this week.
One of which, for example, entails sorting out the data vault of a leading American refinery client.
