Once every week, Kunal Gupta, the head of Flipkart Minutes, holds a two-hour catch-up meeting with his whole team. And every week, he talks stock. 

“He goes through data on SKUs step by step with category managers and directors,” a category manager says, meaning stock-keeping units. “I have never seen a senior vice-president going through SKUs at that level before.”

That attention to detail is how Gupta has turned Minutes into the hottest new quick-commerce property in the country and, in the process, made it critical to Flipkart’s growth strategy.

Minutes, flush with cash from its Walmart-backed parent, has built a network of about 1,000 fulfillment centers and dark stores across 130 cities and 8,000 pincodes, surpassing Swiggy Instamart in the top 10 metros. It’s catching up on order volume, too. Remarkable for a company that only started in 2024. And rewarding for Flipkart.

“Today 20–25% of overall Flipkart business comes from Minutes if we don’t include electronics and mobiles,” the manager says. “So, Minutes is doing about Rs 1,200 crore a month. That’s the scale we are at.”

It’s only getting started…
