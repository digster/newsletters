As the way people search evolves, companies like Flipkart, Mamaearth, and The Whole Truth must go from getting clicks to being the answer—which is both harder and more expensive
With AI increasingly becoming the go-to platform for queries, Google Ads are no longer making sense for companies in general and D2C brands in particular. The visibility game has now moved from SEO to SEO+AEO+GEO. Not only are keywords getting replaced by personalised queries and prompts, but the medium is also changing from text to voice AI and images. Amid multiple startups shutting down due to poor online visibility plus poor demand, brands are now focusing on building AI visibility. Unlike Google Search that features hundreds of links for every keyword, AI just offers the top three or four options, rendering the remaining brands practically invisible.
