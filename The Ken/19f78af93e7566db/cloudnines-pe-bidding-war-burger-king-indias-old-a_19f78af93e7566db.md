Tools from voice-AI startups like Sanas mute out “human” emotions like laughter. Meanwhile, AI agents are sounding more human everyday
To combat accent-based discrimination, BPOs have adopted accent translators like Sanas, so even an Indian can sound American. The ultimate goal is to improve representatives’ net promoter score, which ensures clients don’t shift calls to better-performing geographies elsewhere. The flipside, though, has been these accent translators risk muting out “human” emotives such as laughter, whispers, and tones of voice. All this is happening as AI agents are getting better and cheaper by the day, threatening to replace humans at call centres.
