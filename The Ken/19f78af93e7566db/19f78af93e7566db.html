Cloudnine spent four years growing, only to start a PE bidding war

Cloudnine is the centre of attraction for private-equity firms, four of whom are in a race to bid for a 25% stake in the company. This is a reversal of fortune for the healthcare chain that shelved its IPO plans four years ago. The turnaround, fuelled by inorganic scaling, still couldn’t push up its operating margins, which are currently below the lower end of the industry standard. BPOs used accent translators to cut costs. Its agents are paying the price

Tools from voice-AI startups like Sanas mute out “human” emotions like laughter. Meanwhile, AI agents are sounding more human everyday

To combat accent-based discrimination, BPOs have adopted accent translators like Sanas, so even an Indian can sound American. The ultimate goal is to improve representatives’ net promoter score, which ensures clients don’t shift calls to better-performing geographies elsewhere. The flipside, though, has been these accent translators risk muting out “human” emotives such as laughter, whispers, and tones of voice. All this is happening as AI agents are getting better and cheaper by the day, threatening to replace humans at call centres. [Read the full story]
