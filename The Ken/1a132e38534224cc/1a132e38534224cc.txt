“If I’m working at Flipkart or Myntra, I would have a lot of open hand to make decisions and do the numbers,” said an Ajio executive, who was previously at Flipkart.

“But here (Ajio), most people are bound to always follow the leadership who run the show,” the executive added, referring to Reliance Retail and its subsidiaries.

Ajio had leadership changes this year. Vineeth Nair, chief executive officer (CEO) for a decade, moved to a broader role within the parent company. Sandeep Karwa came in as CEO of the group’s digital business, including Shein India. Karwa had worked across fashion, appliances, wholesale, and mobiles in his previous e-commerce stint, with more decision-making power. Now he has moved from leading the band to following the conductor. 

... 

For Ajio, Myntra is the platform to beat. The latter posted Rs 500 crore in profitability and a topline of over Rs 6,000 crore in FY25. “His (Karwa’s) mandate is likely to accelerate digital growth and establish a credible path to profitability,” said Aalok Dubey, a fashion and apparel consultant.

Myntra also leads on repeat buying: growing it from 49% a year ago to 52% in Q1 2026, while Ajio remains almost exactly the same as it was in Q1 2025 at 32%. 

“If they have to build back the traffic...”
