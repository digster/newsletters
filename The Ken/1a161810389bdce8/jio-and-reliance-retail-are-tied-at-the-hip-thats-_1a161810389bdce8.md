Good Morning Ishan,
Jio Platforms’ initial public offering (IPO), India’s biggest yet, has gotten off to a flying start. Anchor investors placed record bids on Monday for the over Rs 30,200 crore public issue, while public investors lapped it up more than 3X on day one on 21 October.
Typical of its parent—the Mukesh Ambani-controlled Reliance Industries—the issue of the digital-services firm has “big” written all over it. Not just its size, the marketing blitz and influencer push to project it as something that’s “much more than telecom” is also unprecedented.
The issue also has “family” written all over it. The Reliance juggernaut reportedly nudged its 420,000 workforce to subscribe to the IPO, even releasing their October salaries in advance. Akash Ambani, the managing director of Jio Platforms and his twin sister Isha Ambani, a non-executive director in the company, jointly addressed the IPO launch press conference with their younger brother Anant Ambani, also a non-executive director, in the audience.
But it goes far beyond just united-we-stand optics.
