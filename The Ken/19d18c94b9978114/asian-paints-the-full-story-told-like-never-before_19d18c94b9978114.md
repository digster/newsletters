Intermission is The Ken’s most ambitious project yet. It’s a video podcast where each episode goes deep on one Indian company with a full telling: the founding, fights, pivots, and the moment the company finds itself at today.
Every company covered in this show has its own “intermission”—a pause between what it was and what it needs to become. We find that moment, and we sit in it.
For episode 1, we spent six and a half hours with Asian Paints.
It’s a company that is older than the Republic of India, built in a Bombay garage, and now needs to fend off newer rivals replicating its own playbook. The Asian Paints story is about patience, paranoia, and the strange art of selling colour. Hosts Seetharaman G and Rohin Dharmakumar take you through all of it.
