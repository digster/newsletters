Good Morning Ishan,
Ather Energy is coming for TVS and Bajaj—and the latest accelerator is its upcoming launch of mass-market scooter Konarc on 29 August.
The Hero Motorcorp-backed EV company has already bagged investors’ trust, its shares rallying 18% in August alone. Its strategy is unique too. It’s focusing on micromarkets and targeted regional growth, even as TVS and Bajaj cater to much larger national and international markets.
“With Konarc falling in the Rs 1–1.2 lakh price bracket, we’re aiming to expand our footprint in North India, where our presence remains limited,” Ather’s cofounder and CEO Tarun Mehta told The Ken. The company’s 450 models are already popular in southern markets like Tamil Nadu. Meanwhile, the Rizta was specifically designed to meet the needs of customers in states such as Maharashtra and Gujarat, where people wanted a different form factor with bigger seats and storage, he added.
Ather is even setting up a much larger manufacturing facility to support the production of Konarc, in Aurangabad, Maharashtra. It will be operational by around October and the first phase—to be completed by Q4 FY27—will have a capacity of 42,000 units a month. The company’s total production capacity will also jump from an existing 35,000 to 77,000 by the beginning of FY28.
