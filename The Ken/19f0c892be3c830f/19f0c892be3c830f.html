This Bengaluru startup isn’t building faster chips than Nvidia. It’s building cheaper AI

Nvidia won the race to train AI. Turiyam thinks the race to run it is still wide open, and cheaper to win

Running a single AI query costs a fraction of what it used to. But companies keep finding new ways to use AI, so total spending keeps climbing instead of falling. Turiyam built a chip just for “using” AI, not “training” it. Training teaches a model; inference is what happens every time someone actually uses it. Companies are billed in tokens, but they think in finished work. That mismatch is unpredictable, and it’s only getting worse as cheaper AI tempts every department to find a new use for it. Small-scale AI use is cheap regardless of the chip. Turiyam’s pitch only matters to a business already spending millions a month, and only if it never built five years of habits around Nvidia first.
