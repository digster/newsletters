Nearly 90% of all investment and debt in India’s GPU-and-data-centre ecosystem comes from abroad. Domestic lenders are still evaluating the risk
Yotta Data Services is on a GPU buying spree, and the financing behind its orders is being guaranteed by Nvidia itself. Even without the chipmaker’s backing, Indian companies like AMI are buying chips in bulk. Most of the money in the ecosystem is coming from outside the country. While Indian financiers are willing to shell out credit for land and power infrastructure, most are unsure as to how to lend money against GPUs. When this investment does pay off, it is the early foreign backers who cash in, not Indian banks, who are still waiting on the sidelines to see if the bet works.
