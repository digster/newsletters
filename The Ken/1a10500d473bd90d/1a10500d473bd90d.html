Who’s actually paying for Yotta, rivals’ multi-billion-dollar Nvidia orders?

Nearly 90% of all investment and debt in India’s GPU-and-data-centre ecosystem comes from abroad. Domestic lenders are still evaluating the risk

Yotta Data Services is on a GPU buying spree, and the financing behind its orders is being guaranteed by Nvidia itself. Even without the chipmaker’s backing, Indian companies like AMI are buying chips in bulk. Most of the money in the ecosystem is coming from outside the country. While Indian financiers are willing to shell out credit for land and power infrastructure, most are unsure as to how to lend money against GPUs. When this investment does pay off, it is the early foreign backers who cash in, not Indian banks, who are still waiting on the sidelines to see if the bet works.  [Read full story here]

Wealth managers love job hopping. An upstart wants to fix that

Nexedge wants to swap cash incentives for equity, requiring a long-term commitment, to get experienced relationship managers to stay

Wealth management firms are facing intense competition as many newer firms chase the same pool of capital. This makes relationship managers or private bankers an important currency for wealth management firms. They’re trying to retain them through better compensation or other incentives. Nexedge Capital, founded by former 360 One executive Anirudha Taparia, aims to retain these bankers by offering equity ownership in the company. A $12B R&D fund was meant to turbocharge startup funding. It did the opposite

Of the allotted Rs 3,000 crore, only Rs 500 crore has been disbursed. And of the 162 AIFs who’ve applied, not one has received a letter of approval

The RDI fund has delayed our funding by 12 months, as the disbursal will take another six months. Once the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) approves it, we will then have to re-engage with private limited partners (LP), alleged alternate investment funds (AIF). Be it the making ANRF or the RDI fund, it has slowed down certain other R&D funds towards technology readiness levels 1–3, a source from DST told The Ken. Amid no clarity on approval of 162 AIFs and 38 FROs that applied back in January, some have already closed their funds. Under the Rs 1,00,000 crore RDI fund, which is about to complete 12 months, TDB and Birac have so far given Rs 2,500 crore funding commitments to 30 startups. [Read the full story]

Go Digit is an unintended beneficiary in the proposed shake-up of Indian insurance

As the industry reels from the regulator’s radical suggestions on commissions, the 10-year-old general insurer has a reason to smile

India’s insurance regulator, in September, proposed changes to insurance distribution, including cutting commissions and tightening expense caps. Big names like PB Fintech, Policybazaar’s parent, and Turtlemint saw their stocks plummet. But for Go Digit, whose business leans heavily on motor insurance, the changes are a shot in the arm. After paying between 36–44% in commissions, there could be relief and gains ahead. [Read the full story]
