It’s raining gold and diamonds on India’s stock market. 

In 2026 alone, there have been eight mainboard IPOs by jewellery companies, six of them in the last three months. Four companies have also had SME IPOs. In fact, one of them opened its subscription today. 

Surprisingly, B2B players make up a high percentage of this mix—Nityas Gems & Jewellery, Deepa Jewellers, Priority Jewels, and Shankesh Jewellers went public between August and October. 

They’re manufacturers who buy gold, diamonds, and gemstones as raw materials, make jewellery either in-house or through third-party karigars (craftsmen), then sell it to retail chains. Deepa, Priority, and Shankesh Jewellers all supply Kalyan Jewellers. Priority also makes jewellery for Caratlane and Senco Gold. Malabar Gold also gets its jewellery from Deepa and Priority. 

Here’s the conundrum: manufacturers have impressive revenue and profit growth, but don’t actually hold it.

... 

All four companies plan to use the IPO proceeds either to pay off debt or to buy more inventory, roughly earmarking three-fourths of the total money raised. Of the four, Deepa is using most of its Rs 250 crore issue (86%) for its gold inventory.
