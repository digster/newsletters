Every day, somewhere between 10,000 and 25,000 photographs of crops and pests are uploaded to servers at IIT Ropar. They are taken by people at the Centre of Excellence for AI in Agriculture, Annam AI calls its “data warriors”, who are given phones and paid for every picture that meets the centre’s quality standards. 

... 

Anyone, from governments to startups, is meant to be able to build on top of it. When asked if others can plug in, Annam says “yes”. “What we are doing is we are building solutions which will be directly implemented into Bharat Vistaar,” he adds. But the data foundation has not formally launched, and unrestricted API access is a long-term vision. 

Annam is not the only one with this ambition. 

Agriculture has become the sector where everyone, from the Centre to state governments, wants to build the next UPI. Maharashtra’s AI farm-advisory platform, launched in May 2025. Bihar has Bihar Krishi and Odisha has Krushak. Additionally, the government has already named its candidate for the data layer (Agristack) and for the AI advisory layer on top of it (Bharat Vistaar).  

So, where does Annam fit in all this?
