I’m going to start with two hot takes:
Hot take 1 comes from me:
Kunal Shah moves to Meta, struggles to make a dent with Whatsapp, partly because of Meta’s culture and chaos, and partly because he cannot easily transplant this team from Cred to Menlo Park, so he’s forced to work with what he has rather than whom he trusts and wants. Cred becomes a middling fintech that goes public. But in the process, when Kunal Shah’s tenure at Meta ends, he becomes a really capable and interesting AI founder from India who builds a completely new company in the Valley—a transition that no Indian founder has been able to make thus far.
Hot take 2 is delivered by Rohin, who joins me for this week’s episode of Two by Two:
“I think the real import of this story is a wake-up call for India. If some of the most bullish and capable founders in India, like Kunal, who’ve built businesses, raised a billion dollars into companies… is in some senses checking out and going to where the opportunity is… I think it’s a warning sign for India.”

Over the last 72 hours, the internet turned into a firestorm after Meta announced that it was investing roughly $900 million into Cred and that Kunal Shah would leave the company to run Whatsapp globally. There were theories everywhere, with people drawing all kinds of conclusions like 7D chess moves, and how this was a proud validation for India. By the time we started to figure out what exactly was going on, the internet had already produced a dozen explanations.
[...]
We decided that we wouldn’t come in with a strong, clear explanation of why and how things happened the way they did, and what’ll happen next. Instead, we decided to approach the entire episode as a series of questions. We decided to remove all the press-release language and ask, “What is the simplest explanation that has the fewest number of assumptions?”
And to do this, both Rohin and I did our own reporting—we spoke to investors, operators, founders, and former employees of Cred, before recording this [episode](https://the-ken.com/podcasts/two-by-two/the-meta-cred-kunal-shah-deal-stripped-to-its-fewest-assumptions/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=podcast).
