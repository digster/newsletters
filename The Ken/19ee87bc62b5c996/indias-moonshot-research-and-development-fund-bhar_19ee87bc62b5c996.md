[Heads, founders win. Tails, platforms win. AI fund Activate wins either way](https://the-ken.com/story/heads-founders-win-tails-platforms-win-ai-fund-activate-wins-either-way/?utm_source=weekly_email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly_recap_card)
The country’s latest AI fund, founded by a former IndiaAI Mission advisor, is courting the next crop of Indian AI founders, while also backing Elevenlabs, the platform many of them are likely to build on
Activate, the new Indian AI fund, likes to invest in founders before they even have a company, finding them through events, referrals, and conversations. The fund has also invested in Elevenlabs, the voice-AI firm whose technology most Indian voice-AI startups already build on. This creates an unusual position: if the startups Activate backs succeed, the fund wins; if Elevenlabs expands into their categories instead, the fund still wins. Founders and analysts say building on top of a platform that can absorb your product at any time is the central risk of Indian AI—and one that Activate’s structure does not resolve. The vicious loop poses real-world health risks
AI platforms and search engines present health-related information that can be wrong or misleading. That’s partly because they rely for sourcing on big-name hospitals that publish copious volumes of medical content on their websites. The websites in turn use AI for research and writing content and often publish without verification by a domain expert. This potentially puts patients at risk of harm.
