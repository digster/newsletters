Good Morning Ishan,
“Institutional investors don’t go by numbers on X,” said Pranav Pai, founding partner of the early-stage venture-capital firm 3one4 Capital.
That hasn’t stopped founders from sharing them anyway. This includes Rohan Nayak, co-founder of Bengaluru-based audio-series company Pocket FM, who posted his company’s numbers on 10 September. Strikingly, Pocket FM seems to have doubled its annualised revenue to over $500 million in a year. The reason, wrote Nayak, is the company’s decision to produce all its content using AI.
This is the latest explanation for the company’s growth.
It raised $103 million in early 2024 as part of its series D fundraising round, as an audio platform. By mid 2025, it was looking to fundraise again and positioned itself as being in the “business of IP”, a former employee recalled. But when that fell through, it ventured into the market again, now branding itself as an AI-led business.
“AI is the flavour of the town,” said an industry executive. “If you talk about it, it’s easier to fundraise and get media mentions.” In a market where a new format is in vogue every few months, Pocket FM has made it a habit to chase each one, albeit dropping most of them just as quickly…
