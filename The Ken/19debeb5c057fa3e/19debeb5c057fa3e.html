Get a loan, buy more stock: Groww and Angel One’s latest pitch to investors

Sebi’s F&O clampdown is pushing brokers into margin lending. It’s not exactly low-risk for investors

As Sebi’s curbs have started impacting brokerages’ mainstay business of F&O trading, they’ve found their next bet. The likes of Groww and Angel One are increasingly wearing the hat of a lender, expanding their margin trading facility (MTF) books. MTF allows an investor to buy a stock at 2–5X leverage, increasing their chances of making higher gains as well as losses. What does Zoho offer as India’s new official email provider: security or Indianness?

As the world migrates to open-source software, India is going the opposite way—to a closed, proprietary stack it claims is safer

Meity has shifted about 1.7 million email accounts from NIC’s servers to Zoho, as part of a Rs 180 crore deal. NIC has been struggling with issues of breaches, outages, and outdated software for a while. To push for Zoho adoption, various ministries are now discouraging their employees from using open source software. Meity needs to ensure it has interoperability built into its system to not be trapped in a walled garden. [Read the full story]

Jio Studios strikes it rich by being exactly what its rivals gave up on

From the two-part blockbuster Dhurandhar to prestige flicks like Laapataa Ladies, Reliance’s film business is a throwback to the good old studio model just as Bollywood’s biggest producers are cashing out

Jio Studios is on a generational run, with consecutive blockbusters over the past two years, boasting hits like Dhurandhar and Stree 2. Its success comes down to the right mix of experimental bets and calculated risks, helmed by Jyoti Deshpande. Emboldened by the safety-net of Reliance’s marketing, distribution, and financial muscle, Jio Studios has an ecosystem its competitors lack. But in a personality-driven film industry, Jio Studios is yet to develop something other studios have—a distinct identity. [Read full story here]

District isn’t even 2% of Eternal’s business. But it’s enough to rattle Bookmyshow

In under two years, Eternal’s going-out business has scaled to nearly half of Bookmyshow’s revenue and over 60% of its users by redefining outing

Zomato’s District posted a revenue of more than Rs 970 crore in FY26—that’s more than 4X the top line it registered when Eternal acquired Paytm Insider in August 2024. Unlike Bookmyshow, which capitalises on selling movie tickets and high-profile concerts, District’s moat is everything outdoors. That’s also made rivals take notice. Swiggy launched Scenes in 2024 and now Flipkart is planning to sell movie and event tickets. But District is still loss-making. For it to realise its goal of $3 billion in net order value, the company needs to capitalise its customer-acquisition tactics. [Read the full story]
