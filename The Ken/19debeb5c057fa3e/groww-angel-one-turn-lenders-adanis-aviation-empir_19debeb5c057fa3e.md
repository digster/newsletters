Sebi’s F&O clampdown is pushing brokers into margin lending. It’s not exactly low-risk for investors
As Sebi’s curbs have started impacting brokerages’ mainstay business of F&O trading, they’ve found their next bet. The likes of Groww and Angel One are increasingly wearing the hat of a lender, expanding their margin trading facility (MTF) books. MTF allows an investor to buy a stock at 2–5X leverage, increasing their chances of making higher gains as well as losses. What looks like a diversification move today could be where the risks may lie tomorrow. For it to realise its goal of $3 billion in net order value, the company needs to capitalise its customer-acquisition tactics.
