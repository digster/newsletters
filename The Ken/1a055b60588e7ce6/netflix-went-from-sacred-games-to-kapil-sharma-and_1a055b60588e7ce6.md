Good Morning Ishan,
Netflix and Reliance’s media conglomerate Jiostar have been getting quite cozy.
They’ve just finalised a deal whereby Netflix India and its OTT rival Jiohotstar will share streaming rights for a season of reality-TV show Roadies. “It’s a sweet deal, since Roadies runs for several months, has an established legacy, and audience connect,” said a person familiar with the ongoing exchanges. Others in the know confirmed the news.
Even sweeter is the licensing cost: Rs 18 crore. Mere chump change for Netflix, which is used to paying way more, like Rs 85 crore for Dhurandhar and Rs 175 crore for Kalki 2898 AD.
This is hardly a one-off. In August, Netflix began co-streaming MTV Hustle 5: Apna Homeground with Jiohotstar, signed for Rs 13 crore. It has also onboarded marquee names like The Kapil Sharma Show and CID. It’s all part of a concerted effort by the platform to dabble in a little of everything, as announced by Monika Shergill, vice president of content at Netflix India, in February. Its purpose is simple: to offer stories that speak to every generation.
But this wasn’t always the case. In fact, some would say it’s a visible dilution of Netflix India’s earlier strategy…
