Good Morning Ishan,
Zomato’s District had big plans for 2026. From producing Colombian singer Shakira’s debut India tour to selling tickets for high-profile concerts of international artists, including rappers Kanye West and Flipperachi.
After all, it’s a great way to mint money and break into a market that has, for long, had just one gatekeeper: Bookmyshow. The Reliance Industries-backed company organised British band Coldplay’s 2025 shows in Mumbai and Ahmedabad. The Ahmedabad show alone is said to have generated an “economic impact” of over Rs 640 crore.
Some of District’s events, however, got cancelled or were pushed to another date, thanks to an uncertain geopolitical situation. For a business centred on arranging big, splashy events, this could have been a setback.
But District is unfazed.
“Events are just a part of our business,” Akshant Goyal, Eternal’s (Zomato) chief financial officer, told investors in the latest earnings call held on 28 April. “I don’t think a few delayed concerts will impact our outlook.”
The confidence comes from District’s performance.
In FY26, the first full financial year since its August 2024 launch, District’s revenue of over Rs 970 crore made up barely 1.7% of Eternal’s top line. But in the same period, its weekly active users have grown to over 60% of Bookmyshow’s, cutting the latter’s lead of over two decades.
