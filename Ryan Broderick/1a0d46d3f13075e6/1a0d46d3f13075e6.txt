Ryan and Panic World producer Grant experienced the internet IRL firsthand recently when they attended something gloriously called the “_Spongebob_ And Also _Final Fantasy_ & _Kingdom Hearts_ Mpreg Rave” in Brooklyn. Lots to unpack there, I know. I personally did not attend because I am a 45-year-old woman who prefers to spend my evenings like a normal person, playing a gacha game and watching 23-minute-long YouTube videos silently documenting the experience of taking Japan’s Sunflower ferry from Osaka to Beppu.

The _Spongebob_ And Also _Final Fantasy_ & _Kingdom Hearts_ Mpreg Rave had a bit of everything — VTubers, hotdogs, pup masks, you name it.
