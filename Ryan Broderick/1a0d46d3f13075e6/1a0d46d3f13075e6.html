Ryan and Panic World producer Grant experienced the internet IRL firsthand recently when they attended something gloriously called the “Spongebob And Also Final Fantasy & Kingdom Hearts Mpreg Rave” in Brooklyn. Lots to unpack there, I know. I personally did not attend because I am a 45-year-old woman who prefers to spend my evenings like a normal person, playing a gacha game and watching 23-minute-long YouTube videos silently documenting the experience of taking Japan’s Sunflower ferry from Osaka to Beppu.

The Spongebob And Also Final Fantasy & Kingdom Hearts Mpreg Rave had a bit of everything — VTubers, hotdogs, pup masks, you name it. Check out how the night went below, and you’ll find out why I was delighted to get a message from Grant asking me to approve a “Grant_Mpreg_invoice.pdf” expense.
