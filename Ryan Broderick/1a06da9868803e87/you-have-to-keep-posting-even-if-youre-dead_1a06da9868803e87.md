The Algorithm Doesn’t Want Us Remembering Dolly Parton | Dolly Parton’s death on August 25th was a universal cultural event, but not in a way that the internet could easily process. There have definitely been some impressive engagement numbers. She was on our top lists on Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok, and her songs shot to the top of the [iTunes](https://elink835.www.garbageday.email/ss/c/u001.Qr6Ovh5taOidqUa5P-dRpQYE8RIusqUvBbezVOx50XpvEHLH1Ht5Mi5hRLDJSdfJMj-4Y0lMA9U9pKzPhTHRN3Vkemy4M3JvIPdHfmCaI_PCb43Vq6JO4YGaQ2hv3K6Uiq9r77WsdDzx0jPiKQFpJV_MEpXKQW9oaTB4T5T5tkVByLQY5y9Cv06p8ZDQ3vlTSxU2WUqFg2EyCIRBHJrUrgbeWAc60xQW5OKC0HgX-Fjjq5sFmf-uDM8fqX-bcUkFh6nwvxhiz58I3ZKjPAwksQ/4tr/iyqSloEpSheCGU09p_Lygw/h3/h001.5yFK7_SHLt-5c7c9K5HC8Bcu6oigyecZu-vZAB5w3tw) and [Spotify](https://elink835.www.garbageday.email/ss/c/u001._-qCIiYyp6dD21H99lwa0gAD6a2--nxb-Io8C0lwMdCTx8sAOF3oaGs3BIjXu7VnZMotPx4Q18oLeul_ErsRnxArxxixhGfTTQLbAiwUNRsfxTyc8ltbMRp-YDEXkT3euFRqIz61qc0tNWILIeKN_AZOoZXS6JPEUsxX0Y7mkjDLsEGy1Qpv_dVOPqcsd_psBaj9vgQ4DCEa6T48tce4i56d3zRhZnJbZILZpsRbUcC4vdWOBHI5oxkJwlBcejvdEw0H7ZLjo9dtO-8i13hR72i9aHPp045BQ9cWmkC39zw/4tr/iyqSloEpSheCGU09p_Lygw/h4/h001.6hJXvJvKT4xiwtasw7yij59dGS1S_f30xnEHIqKVpmU) charts. But things go a lot faster online. This isn’t just a matter of Parton being an older artist, or even a dead one — the same day “9 to 5” dropped off the Spotify daily charts, “Billie Jean” was 15th.
