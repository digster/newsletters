Klein and his wife launched the h3h3productions channel in 2011. The name is a play on their initials (Hila’s maiden name is Hacmon and Ethan’s middle name is Edward). The channel started as a mix of “comedy” skits and reaction videos, aimed at a very specific kind of guy. Which was evident from Klein’s collaborators at the time, figures like Post Malone, George Kusunoki Miller, better known as either Filthy Frank or Joji, Jonathan “JonTron” Jafari, Oliver Tree, Pewdiepie, Ian “iDubbbz” Jomha, and Justin Roiland. |
Klein’s big breakthrough was in 2016, with the release of his video “[VAPE NATION](https://elink835.www.garbageday.email/ss/c/u001.Qr6Ovh5taOidqUa5P-dRpayqJJO4EEn_ZHoWp5l0deHLMpEGvxiBaKd7G9_sKfIIbrAZkw0Yrldc8G9TN2yAFP_7WtIB5QJvzfFmi3aSBkuZMNghN3BIhbbiF-4RUeyFQh99rrbXuJY5aJn77E7Ac97Wzxf_qzyLLNeCM5cRIOlK_olXbXU5Lez0Uj5b6hItPtbipV6QaYwSkXdl3PihtkCoGPyECnJgUtp9KvZ640wg74dP2aTB8X7uf2l1CFnhRqoSgj9MLo8zRgPG0UYUOQ/4v7/27_YlIY-Sq2viPzNRoSVxw/h8/h001.mKs_yrwpAuqcEHkN1SW9XXMekDRLMJr2O_XCfFqCFO4),” which currently has 30 million views. With that attention, Klein quickly established himself as a major figure on YouTube. He launched his podcast and became very interested in the meta discussion of the platform. Scrolling through his earliest podcast episodes, you can see how Klein blended together YouTube drama, cringe comedy, and a fascination with the lolcow of the week. This is also when he connected with the collaborators that would go on to define the second phase of his career, personalties like Trisha Paytas and Hasan Piker, both of which he had podcasts with at one point. |
There is probably a world where Klein successfully evolved into something closer to an edgier Smosh and took a step back into a proper managerial role like most of his peers. Maybe even chilled out enough to be another elder statesman of the platform like Hank Green or Casey Neistat. But that didn’t happen and, strangely enough, even his fans agree on why. In 2018, the comedian Bill Burr [went on](https://elink835.www.garbageday.email/ss/c/u001.Qr6Ovh5taOidqUa5P-dRpayqJJO4EEn_ZHoWp5l0deGtOeTAnxpO61g5hpQhaKb_33I12c4pYcNkgev3FkzCtes8RZ_UBZ1YiqZPArl8GPh5oMGhCwFlKfCCD0XeNpDjSw_g25ZGfsjKW7FaVhh1RprGODgAIsKSQ3T6i_QIHpyPvFAvpkhsF4hvQQthJQASLYJ36qbHYjsOWmuaiTKGtQm0rHp2tcHDvzOacFfzjJ5OLU4MYzPIgbTmApMCkVN5SEyETvUbBB16zs5mXA4KuQ/4v7/27_YlIY-Sq2viPzNRoSVxw/h9/h001.4sHLbtfWHHreavHP3fCSFAmHJq-EZaazpdlGj5H7HHg) Klein’s podcast, which was streamed live, and ran circles around Klein to the point where it basically permanently altered his career. |
Klein continued to make content and stream during and after the pandemic. But with the Burr episode, it became sort of impossible to pretend that Klein was a proper comedian or even an entertainer. And it’s arguable that a lot of his early fans started to move on. Klein leaned further into his livestream chat show format and embraced his new parasocial role as a hangout streamer. His podcasts with Paytas and Piker helped him replace his old audience with a new, distinctly more female one. And up until October 7th, 2023, it felt like Klein might just host low-rent gossip podcasts forever. October 7th is, once again, a nearly-universally agreed upon moment for Klein. Following Hamas’ attack, Klein, who is Jewish, and Hila, who is Israeli and served in the Israel Defense Forces, took the show to a far darker place. |
Klein has spent the last three years becoming increasingly genocidal and outspoken about his hatred of Palestinians. He finally ended his friendship with Piker after a [livestreamed debate ](https://elink835.www.garbageday.email/ss/c/u001.Qr6Ovh5taOidqUa5P-dRpayqJJO4EEn_ZHoWp5l0deFcvRRk2gRMPW9rL1DiYX5wFKrq7AExbOBqcVrkDO7WYTcqr5w5Yxfn29c9s_iROmYvc7KfRBUI9sUem2cuxwIyUyKnmIHuWSqs5idKzAHNP2oMUf3ZwmUCJW2wjPmTCiqHxNps-g0hDr7iyAWC86W1IK9YEHqTy0-RYJWgr9WDXuffhyHf2wTa47OO3urjeSm-O6EmEUBWWsoKr_765tOyKCHf2TP5g2ZXwts7BR_RBg/4v7/27_YlIY-Sq2viPzNRoSVxw/h10/h001.lT-ovaa1SKAKzGkZuDkKpBU-yRwzBGDR8uj_DQ2cAoA)last year. He [sued](https://elink835.www.garbageday.email/ss/c/u001.Qr6Ovh5taOidqUa5P-dRpayqJJO4EEn_ZHoWp5l0deF6EWAwDp_ryvzsR6lUO4Vt4lZk7MtdH0UsHFX3eeJ5GU8AGy0u4CpOAYg9Q3B0i3eFUHaUQqH3L8h732ByB4ZNumME1e_3byPdxFSRkFvJQO6uk6SAn7om_1hSZZy75APCE3OZoKJf43TgH4oGMGxbt38o-1ooFGwvzZ9MIksD2yKSDcd8v7UZb3Gfv2rJdZWlj-coW08IWuG1xoSQft5od63jv7H4z0D6Fk8rSIql4A/4v7/27_YlIY-Sq2viPzNRoSVxw/h11/h001.OTHAvLMkW7ZxrxHH4hMToGKIV8K2jf6MkdMW84KG_xU) three streamers, Denims, Frogan, and Kaceytron, over their reaction streams to a callout video about Piker. Klein has also ended his friendship with Jomha, who then [released](https://elink835.www.garbageday.email/ss/c/u001.Qr6Ovh5taOidqUa5P-dRpayqJJO4EEn_ZHoWp5l0deHFJPrFfVhJmAHvIjrn2EGp19ghWlp__U6anejEM8uKJKnz3Ler4qtqHrYkumQsA2aOPyY2U0YyK7nUZ7SbPXQ8WALkV--_12N4XnWsPsXD8ABecIHI68JsJAvDgGn94-EvzUN5YBvVA2K3JHo5Zh3mggirbyxRjTEhHeTAjyXi9e3yKuH7RZ2J_s_5sDoQtPWv42ugletgdiPUJteMC2ilWtK5bZbDIg3PDRGaO53P1Q/4v7/27_YlIY-Sq2viPzNRoSVxw/h12/h001.AWyh-ub95D-PWX0q76bT6CO8unh607rZio7Y1uweQgM) a pretty brutal response video, which covers a lot more ground than I will in this piece today. And, interestingly enough, Jomha points out that the redditors currently in the h3h3 subreddit might not even be genuine fans of his and are, increasingly, just posting ragebait in there to antagonize him. Which I think is the most interesting aspect of all of this. And also makes it nearly impossible to gauge how his fans actually feel about the affair. Assuming he has real fans still that feel any kind of way about it at all. |
Because Klein has cycled through so many different audiences over the years, the only people that follow him are intensely parasocial “fans” that basically treat him like a lolcow. They post things in his Reddit to trigger him into having meltdowns on stream and they obsess over his wife and children and his employees and an affair that blows up the show is basically the only thing Klein could have done to make them truly angry. Because it means their friendship simulator has to end. And it also means that the rabid audience he cobbled together after decades of attacking and gossiping about other creators is now pointed straight back at him. His fans have already decided they know who Klein was having an affair with and are poring over every bit of footage they have of her. |
Obviously, we should take everything in the h3 subreddit with a grain of salt, especially right now, but I did find one interaction in there this morning interesting. One user said that Klein should apologize to fans for making them “a part of his parasocial fanbase.” And several other users question if that was legit or not. Another user chimed in, though, [writing](https://elink835.www.garbageday.email/ss/c/u001.Qr6Ovh5taOidqUa5P-dRpeVbr9GbYMAAWdyI82INr51t-xG7O-DTEAC6VFlqawygmLZEzGhgWQDgGtzPb0CmcgYpygwx4ktCHbKN9FsSepboW9RIenfhRAaA1isDgj72EWS0oyMNLU_M7-tY1hpUsUkzT88-AY1QU0JAnyKhQfnp9IK4n8pvvogzswDGFaUOpUanaR67mf9uf5ebX8m9Gq7VgzbTtzkbnBY5zEXjcI4EfYWHTtVzXACKfuChj1R1yXJA-cpYfWE_b_ko7IErPaABLUblFxwPgo7rHWWcOrg/4v7/27_YlIY-Sq2viPzNRoSVxw/h13/h001.TV2RtTiAsBbg03t1yI6zmpieH7UAEqMa2UGxr0RxSiw), “Ethan fostered that parasocial relationship with viewers through his (apparently selective) openness and entire show format. I think with fewer parasocial fans it wouldn't be such a perfect denouncement of Ethan due to the connection people feel to Hila. Kind of ironic.” |

The big bet in Silicon Valley is that Americans don’t actually want prestige at all actually. Instagram moving to TVs confirms this for me.
