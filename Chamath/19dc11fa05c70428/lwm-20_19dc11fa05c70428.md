LWM 2.0The way people search for, interact with, and build on information is changing quickly. We believe that creates an opportunity to make the Learn With Me library more useful.Over the last few years, my team and I have built a growing research library across research in our Deep Dives, highlights in our What I Read This Week newsletter, Social Capital annual letters, and now videos on our new YouTube channel. The goal has always been the same: explain important topics from the ground up, show why they matter now, and share the insights we use to understand the world and make better decisions. That is why I called it Learn With Me. We’re going to keep doing that, and we’re going to keep raising the quality of the work. But I also think the next step is bigger than publishing more research. The way people search for, interact with, and build on information is changing quickly. We believe that creates an opportunity to make the Learn With Me library more useful. I also want to share the agentic tools and workflows I am actively using daily to think through decisions. For example: Portfolio construction builder: currently using to stress-test portfolio allocations Nash equilibrium bot: helping me negotiate across all my ventures Org chart builder: thinking through building my company in the age of AI
That is the direction we are exploring now: creating a more interactive AI-enabled research and learning experience. Before we overbuild, we want your feedback on a few questions below: By the way, this is not an upsell. I want to keep compounding the value and usefulness we deliver to you over time. This would be included for paid members (we’ll have to figure out tokens!). If you have strong opinions, feature ideas, or examples of how you’d want something like this to fit into your workflow, leave a comment below. Thank you |
