Yes I will be doing a Conversation with him.  From Wikipedia ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liaquat_Ahamed ) :

> Ahamed is the author of Lords of Finance: The Bankers Who Broke the World ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lords_of_Finance )  (2009). The book was awarded the 2010 Pulitzer Prize for History ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulitzer_Prize_for_History ) , the 2010 Spear's Book Award ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spear%27s_Book_Award )  (Financial History Book of the Year), the 2010 Arthur Ross Book Award ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Ross_Book_Award )  Gold Medal, the 2009 Financial Times and Goldman Sachs Business Book of the Year Award ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Times_and_Goldman_Sachs_Business_Book_of_the_Year_Award ) . For 2009 it was recognized as one of Time magazine's ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) )  "Best Books of the Year", New York Times ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Times )  "Best Books of the Year" and Amazon.com's ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon.com )  "Best Books of the Year". It was shortlisted for the Samuel Johnson Prize ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Johnson_Prize ) ...The book narrates the events preceding the Black Tuesday ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Tuesday )  stock market crash of 1929 and the disastrous response of the world's major central banks ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_bank ) .

He has a new and excellent book out, namely 1873: The Rothschilds, the First Great Depression, and the Making of the Modern World ( https://www.amazon.com/1873-Rothschilds-Depression-Making-Modern/dp/1594204179/ref=sr_1_1?crid=1CUKC8SKOF0OE&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.uls54ask_QakfKKfKukyUe5pL8cfx6mdJLk02kd2TeDc_jIPt3Fg1oYOJSgZblM7kNjoRa3Z-nL8-7RM9FSKjEMzCP6YPxLMLP-I68c42zA8z75OXSsunUHYM3il2FdJ5B_wqrteY0IOx_5p1U0up1tXoAyex3Ak5wlU-8URssgaCi7If31codkOZT4GqO3TDpM-qhfCkeNE8T7nf2JLNprsGMdvTgnez6aNy2Kv34w.qVYTwVC6Ov9UK5FONr3OwjI-uunXx8KEF-UoR3sakw4&dib_tag=se&keywords=1873&qid=1783283148&sprefix=1873%2Caps%2C247&sr=8-1 ) .

Liaquat Ahamed also has extensive experience in the private sector, and dealing with the World Bank and IMF.  He has produced a movie and done much more as well.
