Roughly 37,000–40,000 Americans [die in auto accidents every year](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=1a235442fe3f7bd4bf48658cc2f77937031f48455f02bf0fa9d83299a75c922f&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93416&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=e954abf61a2f697e7b7eee87bb2a3c3b&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly9jcmFzaHN0YXRzLm5odHNhLmRvdC5nb3YvQXBpL1B1YmxpYy9WaWV3UHVibGljYXRpb24vODEzODAw&email_id=0950eba2789cc08559ed5c61b45856d8). We now have large‑scale, real‑world evidence—from [Waymo](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=a9298ce57b213fb3d65a30b1e0a62213f0c4b4fc87658d8eb9cabe831aa7716d&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93416&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=d81cc32dcf58ee127d5c58bedeccb48f&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cudGFuZGZvbmxpbmUuY29tL2RvaS9mdWxsLzEwLjEwODAvMTUzODk1ODguMjAyNS4yNDk5ODg3I3N1cHBsZW1lbnRhbC1tYXRlcmlhbC1zZWN0aW9u&email_id=0950eba2789cc08559ed5c61b45856d8) and a[ joint analysis with Swiss Re](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=54b4393fb9f2ea8bcb510f2f470857c5a9c8425a9b4801c45a598b18dbbebe3c&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93416&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=ca8de326d1d0692f3cd2bdd953b03b72&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly93YXltby5jb20vcmVzZWFyY2gvZG8tYXV0b25vbW91cy12ZWhpY2xlcy1vdXRwZXJmb3JtLWxhdGVzdC1nZW5lcmF0aW9uLWh1bWFuLWRyaXZlbi12ZWhpY2xlcy0yNS1taWxsaW9uLW1pbGVzLw=&email_id=0950eba2789cc08559ed5c61b45856d8)—that driverless operations can be substantially safer than matched human driving within their current operating domains. The [latest data](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=36b0a6a0d57551b34f0c5532243ec4260e3420e8caa10eca46ef5c0ba4f391ad&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93416&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=32e494dafc51ef9df5daea7d5ed05337&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly93YXltby5jb20vc2FmZXR5L2ltcGFjdC8&email_id=0950eba2789cc08559ed5c61b45856d8) show that over 220 million miles driven, Waymo vehicles--in Los Angeles, San Francisco, Phoenix, Austin and Atlanta--have 94% fewer serious injuries, 82% fewer air bag deployments, and 93% fewer pedestrian injuries. The evidence is not fully independent, but it is unusually transparent, large‑scale evidence.
So with thousands of lives annually in the balance who is against autonomous vehicles (AVs)? Trial lawyers. Remarkably the trial lawyers saw the writing on the wall very early and the have been lobbying against AVs for nearly a decade! The American Association for Justice, the trial lawyers' lobby, has been [a prominent](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=4f0c5f4ae47884a5b07791026e3dfb6d7e1fd734335a4417d862ee148a25256f&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93416&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=e808cda99db73960fee830a87213a354&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ292dGVjaC5jb20vdHJhbnNwb3J0YXRpb24vTGF3bWFrZXJzLVRyaWFsLUxhd3llcnMtQ2xhc2gtT3Zlci1TZWxmLURyaXZpbmctQ2FyLVJ1bGVzLmh0bWw&email_id=0950eba2789cc08559ed5c61b45856d8) [opponent to AV legislation](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=02666c87f9ea2306c9f22b740c1efaf9e8ec520e10a7e12980a7108eb3efc834&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93416&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=9571270755a78513d7b4ced431cf2c81&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly9lbm90cmFucy5vcmcvYXJ0aWNsZS9jb250ZXh0dWFsaXppbmctY3VycmVudC1jb25ncmVzc2lvbmFsLWVmZm9ydHMtZm9yLWF1dG9ub21vdXMtdmVoaWNsZS1yZWd1bGF0aW9ucy8&email_id=0950eba2789cc08559ed5c61b45856d8) (see also reports [here](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=2b97ce9210a627199ce455becc28564b23e98744a26f758239bb886a7a12daf6&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93416&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=541b7442aa294ddfa61aa457028ca06d&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly9sZGEuc2VuYXRlLmdvdi9zeXN0ZW0vcHVibGljLw=&email_id=0950eba2789cc08559ed5c61b45856d8)). (They have been joined by Democrats worried about labor and demanding that heavy trucks be excluded).
The trial lawyers earn a huge amount litigating ordinary auto accidents--Annual U.S. auto insurance payouts (liability + PIP/MedPay) are on the order of $180–220B and trial lawyers are very eager to retain the right to sue car manufacturers for product liability. In my view, product liability isn't useful as a safety device in this field. Instead, the solution is simple. Every car should be required to be insured, regardless of driver. Indeed, Waymo vehicles are already insured at $5 million liability coverage per vehicle, far higher levels than most human drivers are covered.
The [UK's Automated and Electric Vehicles Act 2018](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=79a907ed62266c2a4e36a4f7641095cf8cbe9458a15f8aaaaff261463705e734&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93416&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=a401097c5b551431722f956301377f36&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubGVnaXNsYXRpb24uZ292LnVrL3VrcGdhLzIwMTgvMTgvY29udGVudHM&email_id=0950eba2789cc08559ed5c61b45856d8) does basically this--a single motor insurer covers the vehicle whether the human or the automated system is driving; the victim is compensated directly by the insurer, no need to establish product defect; the insurer then subrogates against the manufacturer if the software was at fault. Victims get paid fast, manufacturers face the cost of their defects through recoveries and premiums, and the high-transaction costs (i.e. lawyer fees!) and messy manufacturer-versus-victim litigation is replaced by insurer-versus-manufacturer bargaining between repeat players who settle efficiently.
The great thing about this system is that insurance almost certainly deters better than tort: fleets generate data that makes experience rating precise, so insurers become continuous safety regulators, whereas litigation delivers a noisy, lagged, lottery like signal depending on [safety-irrelevant factors of the jury and the locale](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=990f12c42c6f9445a7457531069d106a6cb92e47da87c63eab27e619419ce493&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93416&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=e445ae2b6f30baf1d2e86de6df301fe4&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuam91cm5hbHMudWNoaWNhZ28uZWR1L2RvaS9hYnMvMTAuMTA4Ni8zNDQ1NjA&email_id=0950eba2789cc08559ed5c61b45856d8).
We have the best data on Waymo, Tesla data is murkier but note how well this works with the insurance system. Let the insurers decide how much to charge Tesla robotaxis and FSD drivers--they will internalize the externality far better than tort lawyers. In short, insurance works great for accident victims but not for trial lawyers. Indeed, if the trial lawyers have their way accident victims will continue to be buried in an invisible graveyard.
