First, every Bob Hall paper is interesting! Only a handful of economists can say the same. Every paper has an idea, and some sort of novel contribution. His lesser-known papers can be as thought-provoking as the more famous ones.
Here is [Bob on scholar.google.com](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=d8aa1fa78fab370c6212ef5ba91d06e305bd4fa20816754cf669d3e226d402c1&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93976&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=bb89dafcbd41cdcc70e0f83949cd57bf&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly9zY2hvbGFyLmdvb2dsZS5jb20vc2Nob2xhcj9obD1lbiZhc19zZHQ9MCUyQzQ3JnE9cm9iZXJ0K2UuK2hhbGwmYnRuRz0&email_id=b3ea398b00e9c036db3a16001c43e274). You will notice topping the list are important papers on economic growth, even though Bob might be best known for his work on the consumption function.
One of the first Bob Hall papers I studied was [his supply and demand analysis with David Lilien](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=255bb6bb35116aeb6ef60c714473cfb27324f0018b8b2c26091027c6312e3125&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93976&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=520ae6b7eca28a5c4f8ddad2fc223596&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubmJlci5vcmcvcGFwZXJzL3cwMzA2&email_id=b3ea398b00e9c036db3a16001c43e274). It is one of the best papers for seeing how Bob approaches every issue by thinking like an economist.
Bob also thought very creatively about how claims about the Solow residual [could be translated](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=d2a28db4d6b7ae35cce2f4d57770d9a3050cb85f80dcd46317cc679522106a7e&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93976&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=489a823150edfe675105e2d4cdc51a2a&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuam91cm5hbHMudWNoaWNhZ28uZWR1L2RvaS9hYnMvMTAuMTA4Ni8yNjE1NzA&email_id=b3ea398b00e9c036db3a16001c43e274) into isomorphic claims about the gap between price and marginal cost.
Second, Bob is the greatest commentator on other people's work of all time. He is nice and polite, but if he is assigned to your paper beware! He will find its flaws.
Third, Bob was part of the production function for Andy Hall, whose work [has been covered on this blog much as of late](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=c63398a04050971ac42025f1d51f2bf0a0d05c608b3cf14c4d490567444885b9&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93976&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=ff4baadf903e24123426707f51dd9bf6&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly9tYXJnaW5hbHJldm9sdXRpb24uY29tLz9zPWFuZHkraGFsbA=&email_id=b3ea398b00e9c036db3a16001c43e274), mostly on AI and economics/social science.
Fourth, Bob has in the past emailed me to correct a typographical error on this blog. It is the old saying that if you want to get something done, give it to a busy person.
An award much deserved...
