Post       : Emergent Ventures India, 16th cohort

Posted     : April 18, 2026 at 12:11 am
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Education

Roumak Das ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/roumak-das ) , a grade 11 student from West Bengal, and Samik Goyal ( https://samikgoyal.com ) , a 12th grader from Patiala, received their grants to travel to the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence 2025 in Beijing, where Roumak won a gold medal and Samik a silver medal. Roumak’s grant also supports his college applications, and Samik’s grant supports SPOI ( https://spoi.org.in ) , dedicated to teaching informatics to school students.

Ishaan Gangwani ( https://ishaangangwani.com/ ) , 17, received his grant to develop InkVell ( https://www.inkvell.ai/ ) , an AI-native LaTeX editor, and to support his travel to the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence 2025 in Beijing.

Ronald Abraham ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/ronabraham83 )  received a career development grant for Veeraa ( https://www.veeraa.org/ ) , to build a crowdfunding and growth platform for India’s community leaders.

Tristan Wagner ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/tristanwagner23/ )  received his grant to explore low-cost autoinjectors for treating anaphylaxis and snakebite envenoming in India.

Michael Grasa ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelgrasa )  received his grant to test a transparent, falsifiability-first approach to decoding the Indus Valley script, releasing versioned overlays and open datasets for replication or refutation.

Jasraj Budigam ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/jasraj-budigam ) , 16, received his grant to develop CapNav-Lite, an adaptive AI navigation system that personalizes power-wheelchair control to each user within minutes on everyday hardware.

Mannat Kaur ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/mannat-kaur-023b1b214 ) , 17, freshman at Stanford University, received her grant to continue developing research on wastewater recycling and its integration into the built environment and low-carbon housing.

Vineela Upadhyayula ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/vineela-upadhyayula-644b59352 ) , Hari Krishna Upadhyayula ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/uharikrishna ) , and Phani Madhav Upadhyayula ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/phanimadhavu )  received their grant for NeuraEase ( https://www.neuraease.com/ ) , to build a wearable-driven AI detection and management of acute dysregulation events in neurodivergence and neurological disorders, including autistic meltdowns.

Arnav Kumar ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/arnav-kumar-4b3b18212 )  and Gavneesh ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/gavneesh-6868a0259 ) , cofounders of Vyobha Aerospace, received their grant to build regional eVTOL aircraft with fractional ownership at the cost of a car.

Aditya Raj Chopra ( https://x.com/chopra__aditya ) , a high school senior, received a general career development grant.

Ansh Mishra ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/shree108ansh ) , 17, received his grant to build reliable and accessible bionic prosthetic hands.

Vasu Dubey ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/vasu-dubey-b191461b4 ) , 22, received his grant to build a machine-learning-based medical device for speech restoration in laryngectomy patients.

Snehadeep Kumar ( https://www.snehadeepkumar.in ) , 21, received his grant for Nebula Space Organisation ( https://www.nebulaspaceorg.com ) , to build ultra-low-cost Earth-imaging CubeSats and a global imagery platform that makes space data accessible to everyone.

Uttam Singh ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/somvanshi/ )  and Ayush Das ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/d-ayush/ )  received their grant for Nakshatra Maps ( https://www.nakshatramaps.com/ ) , to help people navigate indoor and outdoor public spaces with dynamic hyperlocal interactive maps, AR navigation, and smart emergency evacuation.

Mankaran Singh ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/mankaran32 )  received his grant to build frictionless human-robot interaction for machines operating in human-centric environments.

Sommaiya Angrish ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/sommaiya-angrish ) , 21, an alt Hindi-pop musician, received his grant to work on his third album, rooted in his personal healing journey.

Achyut Tiwari ( https://www.achyuttiwari.com/ ) , 24, received his grant for GeoLiquefy ( https://www.geoliquefy.com/ ) , an AI system forecasting earthquake-related soil liquefaction from geotechnical data for engineers, insurers, and risk assessors.

Devayan Das ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/devayan-das-29b703252/ ) , 19, a biotech undergraduate, received his grant to develop dissolvable tissue culture nutrient blocks that simplify lab workflows and turn lab prep into a plug-and-play process.

Ayush Kale ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/ayush-kale-809b88223 ) , a materials engineer, received his grant for EarthSprint Solutions ( https://earthsprintsolutions.com/ ) , to transform agricultural waste into low-carbon, high-performance cement blocks.

Mohd Fahad Eqbal ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/fahadeqbal/ ) , 24, received his grant for Chakraswap ( https://www.chakraswap.in ) , to scale an affordable battery swap network for e-rickshaw drivers.

Satyamedh Hulyalkar ( https://satyamedh.me )  received his grant to develop a LoRa-based self-healing mesh network for agricultural and monitoring use cases.

Shivam Parashar ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/shivam-parashar-iitj/ )  received his grant for GreenScore ( https://www.greenscore.in/ ) , to build an industrial effluent monitoring system combining machine learning and IoT to keep Indian rivers clean.

Anand Unni ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/anand-s-unni )  received his grant for Nayaneethi Policy Collective ( https://www.nayaneethi.com/ ) , to develop a public policy curriculum and a community of public policy thinkers and analysts in Kerala, and strengthen the demand side of public policy.
