Post       : Emergent Ventures India, 19th cohort

Author     : Tyler Cowen

Asit Singh ( http://linkedin.com/in/asit-singh-2183433a1 ) , founder of Syngi, received his grant to develop sub-angstrom optical MEMS sensors for next-generation semiconductor lithography.

Rishabh ( http://linkedin.com/in/rishabhverma2 )  received his grant to build a platform for real-time updates from Delhi courts, making judicial activity easier to track and understand.

Hari ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/hariharan-ayappane/ )  and Naveen ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/naveenvenkat17/ )  received their grant for Archgen AI ( https://www.archgen.tech/ ) , an agentic ecosystem connecting legacy EDA tools with AI workflows for semiconductor chip design.

Shubh ( http://shubhxho.com ) , 18, received his grant to scale robotics using world models.

Sarthak Ahuja ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/sarthakahuja1/ )  received his grant for Hypertensight, a smartphone-based system for detecting hypertensive retinopathy, now deployed in over 18 Indian villages. His project was also published ( https://www.internationaljournalssrg.org/IJCSE/paper-details?Id=572 )  in the International Journal of Computer Science and Engineering in 2024.

Michaela Talwar ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/michaelatalwar ) , received her grant for AltShows.com ( https://altshows.com ) , an artist-run ticketing and discovery platform for India’s alternative arts ecosystem.

Avyukt Jindal, 16, received his grant to develop a nonlinear operator framework and pursue related work in condensed matter and other fields.

Anirudh Anilkumar ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/dr-anirudh-anilkumar-587b55141 ) , 29, and AV Surya Vardhan ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/surya-vardhan-av/ ) , 26, received their grant for Fe-rry ( https://fe-rry.com/ ) , developing IoT-enabled modular point-of-care diagnostics for blood testing anywhere.

Bhanvi Kumar ( http://www.linkedin.com/in/bhanvi-kumar ) , a fourth-year Biotechnology and Biochemical Engineering undergraduate at IIT Kharagpur, received her grant to study cellular reprogramming and cancer cell-state transitions.

Samanvya Hooda ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/samanvyahooda/ )  received his grant for nuclear strategy research in the Indo-Pacific using Mandarin sources and quantitative methods.

Pradyumn Chaudhary ( https://x.com/daddy__broccoli )  received his grant to build a low-cost, customizable augmented-reality prototype for practical use.

Jay Handa ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/jay-handa-861696308 ) , 16, received his grant to build SMAC (Swing Match AI for Coaches), an AI-powered golf swing analysis platform for coaches.

Sajeeva Ratnam Nepala ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/sajeeva-ratnam-nepala/ ) , 28, founder of Helen Bikes ( https://www.helenbikes.com ) , received his grant to develop a series-hybrid pedal system for the first fully electric hubless bicycle.

Hitendra Singh ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/hitendra-singh3690 ) , cofounder of Spantrik ( https://spantrik.com/ ) , received his grant to develop reusable rockets, including the Leapfrogger demonstrator; he has built and flown over 100 high-powered rockets.

Avi Mohan Kumar Shuklaa ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/avi-mohan-kr-shuklaa-235605282/ ) , 17, received his grant for a solar-powered AI elephant deterrent using seismic and radar sensor fusion, already field-deployed in Jharkhand.

Surya Uthkarsha ( http://suryauth.info ) , 14, received his grant for Flake AI, infrastructure that converts websites into editable designs and reflects changes in the underlying codebase.

Kritarth Agrawal ( https://linkedin.com/in/kritarthagrawal )  received his grant for Sakshya, an AI-powered document-intelligence platform for India’s compliance and regulatory ecosystem.

Tejas Pugalia, 13, received his grant to advance his research on the Ramanujan-tau function and prepare for the International Mathematical Olympiad.

Arnav ( https://arnavdportfolio.vercel.app/ ) , 17, from Navi Mumbai, received his grant to build a $200 diagnostic pen intended as an alternative to a $3,000 Parkinson’s scan.

Arjan Chaudhary ( https://arjanchaudharyy.lol ) , 15, received his grant for GGameChamps ( http://x.com/ggamechamps ) , which structures competitive-gameplay data for analytics and AI use.

Satadru Chakrabarty, Ph.D., received his grant to develop an affordable multiplexed point-of-care diagnostic using de novo antibodies for liver-function testing in rural India.

Varun Bajpai ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/varun-bajpai-768117374 )  received his grant for the Biological Data Network (BDN-04), a bio-integrated communication architecture exploring human muscle tissue for data transmission.

Semil Vaghasiya ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/semil-vaghasiya-5187701b1 ) , 24, received his grant to develop sensor-free, 3D-printed hoof pads that use wear-deformation to predict equine injuries.

Kanukant Om Gupta ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/kanukantgupta )  and Mansi Gupta ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/mansi-gupta-4271a817 )  received their grant for Clearquik ( https://www.clearquik.com ) , to make customs clearance in India simpler, more transparent, and legitimate.

Parth Anand ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/parth-anand-545b8a2a7 ) , 15, received his grant for research on temporal hallucinations in large language models across Western and non-Western domains. His paper was accepted for an oral presentation by the Society for Study for Artificial Intelligence and Simulation Behaviour XTAI symposium.

Shadman Sakib ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/shadman-sakib-4bb41a2b8 )  received his grant for youth development, communication, practical career guidance, and accessible learning initiatives in Bangladesh; his content reaches over 160,000 subscribers.

Sk Reezaal Arafat ( https://sk-reezaal-arafat.netlify.app ) , 16, received his grant for Project SATARK, a decentralized agricultural fire-interception grid combining AI, IoT sensors, and a local response network in rural West Bengal.

Manan Gupta ( http://manangupta.com )  received his grant for Artemis ( http://www.theartemis.app ) , a proactive AI layer that lets users control their digital life through a phone call.

Srijal Dutta ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/srijal-dutta-537b65435/ )  received his grant for ZSMC Co. ( https://thezsmc.co ) , an independent research initiative working across emerging technologies and engineering.
