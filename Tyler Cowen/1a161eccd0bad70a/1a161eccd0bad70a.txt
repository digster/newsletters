Post       : Do mass shootings in universities lead to permanent effects?
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/10/do-mass-shootings-in-universities-lead-to-permanent-effects.html
Posted     : October 22, 2026 at 12:42 am
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Data Source, Education, Law

> Using a stacked difference-in-differences design covering shootings between 1990 and 2025, I compare cohorts at affected universities before and after the shooting with cohorts at matched control universities. I find no detectable effects on time to degree, but undergraduates enrolled at the time of the shooting are 4.2% less likely to pursue further studies. Those who do continue shift toward health: the share of exposed cohorts who later enroll in a health degree rises by 1.3 percentage points (8.8%), and this effect persists among cohorts who enroll after the shooting. Effects on students present during the shooting are consistent with a response to trauma; for later cohorts, I find evidence of sorting, as master’s students with prior healthcare experience become more likely to enroll in affected universities. These results suggest that mass shootings leave institutional and cultural legacies that outlast the students who witnessed them.

That is from the job market paper of María Medellín-Esguerra ( https://mariamedellin.com/ ) , who is currently on the job market from Brown University.
