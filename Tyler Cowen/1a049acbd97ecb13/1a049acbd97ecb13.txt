Post       : Tysons Corner (Alaska) fact of the day

Posted     : August 28, 2026 at 2:37 pm
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Uncategorized

> The centerpiece is Tysons Corner Center, today the eighth largest mall in the country, and one of the largest ( https://wtop.com/business-finance/2020/07/dc-area-now-leads-for-suburban-office-space-led-by-tysons/ )  sub-city-sized markets for office space. It is half-owned ( https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/economy/tysons-corner-center-just-a-piece-of-oil-backed-alaska-wealth-funds-portfolio/2011/09/21/gIQARE3wtK_story.html )  by the Alaska Permanent Fund, which is the state-owned corporation that invests oil revenue on behalf of Alaska’s citizens, and half-owned ( https://investing.macerich.com/news-releases/news-release-details/macerich-completes-710-million-refinancing-tysons-corner-center )  by Macerich, a giant REIT, which is in turn owned by hundreds of large institutional investors and many individuals.

This very interesting essay ( https://www.thenewatlantis.com/publications/you-probably-own-this-7-eleven )  by Joseph Lawler argues that liquid real estate markets, and distanced ownership, militate against beauty.
