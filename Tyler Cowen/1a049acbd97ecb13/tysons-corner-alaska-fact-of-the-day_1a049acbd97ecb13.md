The centerpiece is Tysons Corner Center, today the eighth largest mall in the country, and
[one of the largest]sub-city-sized markets for office space. It is[half-owned]by the Alaska Permanent Fund, which is the state-owned corporation that invests oil revenue on behalf of Alaska’s citizens, and[half-owned]by Macerich, a giant REIT, which is in turn owned by hundreds of large institutional investors and many individuals.
