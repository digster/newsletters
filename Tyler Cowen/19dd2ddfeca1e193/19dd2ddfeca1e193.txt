You do not hear this point made too often, yet it is true:

> Very interesting new study finding that *even in large European cities*, in most places it is easier to access opportunities by car than by public transport https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.01019 ( https://t.co/YzQbrtfR0i )  The exceptions are Paris, Zurich and the innermost parts of Milan and Barcelona

That is from Giulio Mattioli ( https://x.com/aboutJoy/status/2048754764364571082 ) , citing the paper by Bruno Campenelli, et.al.  In turn via Joy Buchanan ( https://x.com/aboutJoy ) .
