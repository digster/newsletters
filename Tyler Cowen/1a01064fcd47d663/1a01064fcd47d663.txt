Yes I will be doing a Conversation with him.  Most of all he is obsessed with design, here is Wikipedia ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Mars ) :

> Roman Mars is an American radio producer. He is the host and producer of 99% Invisible ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/99%25_Invisible ) , a KALW ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KALW )  radio show and podcast ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Podcast ) , and a founder of the podcast collective Radiotopia ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiotopia ) , which he describes as efforts "to broaden the radio landscape [and] make shows that aren't bound by conventions" of public radio in the United States. In 2020, he co-authored the New York Times best seller The 99% Invisible City ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_99%25_Invisible_City?action=edit&redlink=1 )  with Kurt Kohlstedt...Mars, with Elizabeth Joh, also hosts the podcast What Roman Mars Can Learn About Con Law ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/What_Roman_Mars_Can_Learn_About_Con_Law ) .

His TED talk on city flags ( https://www.ted.com/talks/roman_mars_why_city_flags_may_be_the_worst_designed_thing_you_ve_never_noticed )  is a good introduction to his thought.
