A key U.S. government report on mental health, substance use and addiction showed continued improvements in several metrics, especially among young people.
There was some uncertainty about the future of the National Survey on Drug Use and Health last year, when the entire 17-member team responsible for it was
[laid off]by the Trump administration. But the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration released the latest report Monday, with data that lets researchers look at trends from 2021 to 2025.Fewer adolescents aged 12 to 17 reported using tobacco, alcohol, marijuana and binge drinking in the previous month, the survey found.
And in the past year, fewer in that age group reported:
— illicit drug and marijuana use
— starting drinking alcohol, vaping or marijuana
— substance, alcohol or drug use disorders
Adolescents also saw declining trends for major depressive episodes and fewer reported serious thoughts of suicide, making a suicide plan and attempting suicide.
