Post       : Interviewing Yiyang Zhuge (Zhong Shu)
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/08/interviewing-yiyang-zhuge-zhong-shu.html
Posted     : August 29, 2026 at 12:07 pm
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Media, Philosophy, Uncategorized

Many people are writing to me, upset that I am interviewing Ziyang Zhuge ( https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/08/what-should-i-ask-yiyang-zhuge-%e8%af%b8%e8%91%9b%e4%b8%80%e6%9d%a8-also-known-as-zhong-shu-%e4%bb%b2%e6%a0%91.html ) , a well-known Chinese internet intellectual, who recently was accused of plagiarism ( https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1018944 ) .  I am keen to proceed with the interview, and am happy to explain why.  A few points:

1. The relevant facts here still are being disputed, and furthermore it all happened in Chinese.  If I cannot personally adjudicate the matter, I remain inclined to proceed.  I am also well aware that standards in China, including for plagiarism, are very different than in the United States.  If someone tells me "A person in China did [fill in the blank]" my initial reaction is still "Are they interesting to talk to?"

2. I have had on numerous CEOs and business leaders.  Your opinions of each will vary, but surely some of them are guilty of something.  I do not regret any of them as guests, except insofar as they were boring.  You also can debate whether a former CIA head, in this case John Brennan ( https://conversationswithtyler.com/episodes/john-o-brennan/ ) , ever did anything wrong.  He was fascinating ( https://www.reddit.com/r/UFOs/comments/1aviidl/former_cia_director_john_brennan_20132017_is/ ) .

3. I once had on Sam Bankman-Fried ( https://conversationswithtyler.com/episodes/sam-bankman-fried/ ) , now a convicted felon.  It was an excellent episode, and I will point out my lines of questioning were more perspicacious than many people since have realized.

4. I once had on a convicted murderer, Shaka Senghor ( https://conversationswithtyler.com/episodes/shaka-senghor/ ) .  It was an excellent episode, and if I recall only a single person wrote me to complain.  I would gladly have more criminals on, and they do not all have to be repentant in the manner that Shaka is.  Would not a pickpocket guest be intriguing?  For Althusser however it is too late.

5. Without knowing the full story, I suspect the case of Yiyang Zhuge (neither a felon nor a murderer) is most analogous to having on the CEO of DeepSeek, Liang Wenfang.  At this point it is pretty well known that DeepSeek (and other Chinese AI models) distilled a lot from Anthropic's Claude.  That is a violation of terms of service, and in economic terms it is an event and a depredation of pretty serious magnitude.  I still would be delighted to have Liang Wenfang on the show, and I would do so without obsessing over the issue of distillation.  How about it Liang Wenfang?

Xi Jinping is welcome too.  Chairman Mao, is pork belly with red sauce over- or underrated?  The world awaits your response.
