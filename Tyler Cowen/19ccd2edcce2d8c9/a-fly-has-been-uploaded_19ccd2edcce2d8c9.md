In 2024, the[ entire neuronal diagram](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=5d1b62e65aa1afde7def86c91605cd879e2eba30ab96d5a2aa0cc56f5ae23ad7&blog_id=42693868&post_id=92582&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=d0d1149f516ce1f891806a4c55744f24&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubmF0dXJlLmNvbS9hcnRpY2xlcy9kNDE1ODYtMDI0LTAzMDI5LTY&email_id=18bf1c8e6617e12a8c204e2db921dc3c) of the fruit-fly brain--some 140,000 neurons and 50 million connections--was mapped. Later research showed that [the map could be used to predict behavior](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=dade1df602f427ceeebd4994558a058096b0f3fdfb33f90ee939c0ab756477c9&blog_id=42693868&post_id=92582&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=4690c19f25b042e70e3a12d867ecafa5&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubmF0dXJlLmNvbS9hcnRpY2xlcy9zNDE1ODYtMDI0LTA3NzYzLTk&email_id=18bf1c8e6617e12a8c204e2db921dc3c). Now, [Eon Systems](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=ae8287dd73df90a59caa33997da7e26edd9e36e98aa7d5f7c01a9a821e2d3bd0&blog_id=42693868&post_id=92582&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=b2341d1f4d6076b6b5d5ca4887647dd5&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly9lb24uc3lzdGVtcy8&email_id=18bf1c8e6617e12a8c204e2db921dc3c) a firm with some of the scientists involved in the fruit-fly research and with the goal of uploading a human brain has announced that they [uploaded the fruit fly brain to a digital environment](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=75e5ddff700618e04244ad70bfa7ea72868d44373850bacfb7dce19ce5008e84&blog_id=42693868&post_id=92582&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=2274a163213310289ea39b5a17e4aa6d&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly94LmNvbS9hbGV4d2cvc3RhdHVzLzIwMzAyMTczMDE5MjkxMzIzMjM/cz0yMA=&email_id=18bf1c8e6617e12a8c204e2db921dc3c).
The digital fly appears to behave in the digital environment in reasonably fly like ways--this is not a simulation, the fly's "sensors" are being activated by the digital environment and the neurons are responding. Some more details [here](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=c294ffec556b64fa0fb19b3d9024c2e8e47d04eca4715749491a920d678634d8&blog_id=42693868&post_id=92582&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=f6d238d80b653f5f01d01b5ea0a141b4&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly90aGVpbm5lcm1vc3Rsb29wLnN1YnN0YWNrLmNvbS9wL3RoZS1maXJzdC1tdWx0aS1iZWhhdmlvci1icmFpbi11cGxvYWQ&email_id=18bf1c8e6617e12a8c204e2db921dc3c).
