Post       : A Fly Has Been Uploaded
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/03/a-fly-has-been-uploaded.html
Posted     : March 8, 2026 at 7:18 am
Author     : Alex Tabarrok
Tags       : age of em, upload
Categories : Philosophy, Science

In 2024, the entire neuronal diagram ( https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-024-03029-6 )  of the fruit-fly brain--some 140,000 neurons and 50 million connections--was mapped. Later research showed that the map could be used to predict behavior ( https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07763-9 ) . Now, Eon Systems ( https://eon.systems/ )  a firm with some of the scientists involved in the fruit-fly research and with the goal of uploading a human brain has announced that they uploaded the fruit fly brain to a digital environment ( https://x.com/alexwg/status/2030217301929132323?s=20 ) .

The digital fly appears to behave in the digital environment in reasonably fly like ways--this is not a simulation, the fly's "sensors" are being activated by the digital environment and the neurons are responding. Some more details here ( https://theinnermostloop.substack.com/p/the-first-multi-behavior-brain-upload ) .

N.b. this work is not yet published.

Addendum 1: Of course Robin Hanson ( https://www.amazon.com/Age-Em-Work-Robots-Earth/dp/0198754620/?tag=margrinalrevol-20 )  is an advisor to Eon Systems.

Addendum 2: In other news, human brain cells on a chip ( https://www.newscientist.com/article/2517389-human-brain-cells-on-a-chip-learned-to-play-doom-in-a-week/ )  learned to play Doom. No word on whether they were conscious or not.
