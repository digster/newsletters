...on March 12 we’ll be launching [Liberalism.org](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=84874ab78f5e63906e5e077778aa146244e5a25413c5c385c46ea8ce8a9cb3f7&blog_id=42693868&post_id=92592&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=48afc771ce56618743fe1a1efe5bbcf6&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly9saWJlcmFsaXNtLm9yZy8&email_id=dad2f65651a663a9384f98c703c2e4ce), a new project from IHS [Institute for Humane Studies]. We’re aiming to build something akin to a modern-day coffee house of the liberal tradition—a digital gathering place where today’s most innovative liberal thinkers can weigh tradeoffs, think across differences, and apply liberal values to the challenges of today and the future.
The idea is to create a space that is serious but accessible—a home for exploring political, economic, intellectual, and civic freedom as a coherent and evolving tradition. We’re hoping it will serve as both an outlet for the ideas and a public-facing resource for those who care about the future of liberalism in its broad, classical sense.
