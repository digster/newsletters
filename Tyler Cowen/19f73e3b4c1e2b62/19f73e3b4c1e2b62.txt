Yes I will be doing a Conversation with him.  From Wikipedia ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Garicano ) :

> Luis Garicano Gabilondo (pronounced [ˈlwis ɣaɾiˈkano] ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Spanish ) ; born 1967) is a Spanish economist and politician who was a Member of the European Parliament (MEP) from 2019 to 2022. He was also vice president of Renew Europe ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renew_Europe )  and vice president of the European political party Alliance of Liberals and Democrats for Europe (ALDE Party ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_of_Liberals_and_Democrats_for_Europe_Party ) ). Before entering politics, he was a professor of strategy and economics at IE Business School ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IE_Business_School )  in Madrid and at the London School of Economics ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics )  (LSE). After leaving the European Parliament he has returned to academia as a visiting professor at Columbia Business School ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_Business_School )  and at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago_Booth_School_of_Business ) . In 2023, returned to LSE ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics )  as full professor at the School of Public Policy.

He is one of the leading European economic liberals, here is his home page ( https://sites.google.com/site/luisgaricano/ ) .  Here is his Google scholar page ( https://scholar.google.com/scholar?hl=en&as_sdt=0%2C47&q=luis+garicano&oq=luis+gar ) .  Here is Luis on Twitter ( https://x.com/lugaricano ) .  he also has a very good book coming out called Messy Jobs ( https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H42PP3BC?lv=shuf&channelId=500&plpRedirect=mhFallback ) , co-authored with Jim Li and Yanhui Wu.  So what should I ask him?
