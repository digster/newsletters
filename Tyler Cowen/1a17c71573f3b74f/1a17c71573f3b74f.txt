Emily Bazelon was the moderator, there were five of us in the symposium ( https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/26/magazine/ai-companies-law-regulations.html?smid=nytcore-ios-share ) .  Excerpt from me:

> Bores: Do you think the federal government pre-empting the states is consistent with democracy?
>
> Cowen: Absolutely. We have federal laws that pre-empt state lawmaking in many other areas. Courts have upheld it many, many times.
>
> Bores: OK, but is it pro-democracy, in giving people a say?
>
> Cowen: It is pro-American Constitution.
>

Most of the panel disagreed with most of my points.
