Post       : Why I do not use LLMs more
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/10/why-i-do-not-use-llms-more.html
Posted     : October 15, 2026 at 4:20 am
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Books, Music, Web/Tech

They are so very, very good at matching me to the books I wish to read and the music I wish to listen to.  In this odd sense they are not very sticky.  The LLMs are amazing, but the world is awesome too!  Thus the LLMs keep on sending me away.  There is quality travel as well.

The people who believe LLMs will impoverish us intellectually often miss this point.  And it might also provide a cap on market demand.  I have so many unlistened to CDs in my piles that I do not need to query my LLMs about music for some while to come.
