Post       : Estimating the Economic Value of Zoning Reform
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/08/estimating-the-economic-value-of-zoning-reform.html
Posted     : August 4, 2026 at 2:51 pm
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Data Source, Economics, Law, Uncategorized

> We estimate the economic value of zoning reform in São Paulo, which altered maximum permitted construction along transportation corridors. Developers increased filings for multifamily construction in blocks affected by the reform, leading to more housing supply and lower housing prices in neighborhoods that allow more densification. Our equilibrium model of housing markets estimates an aggregate 1.6 percent increase in housing stock and a 0.4 percent reduction in prices, resulting in large housing wealth transfers from current to future homeowners. The reform produced welfare gains of 0.65 percent of city GDP, mostly due to developer profits and consumer gains from the newly built environment.

That is from the AEA policy journal ( https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/pol.20230542 ) , by Santosh Anagol, Fernando Ferreira, and Jonah Rexer.
