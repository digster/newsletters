Yes, I will be doing a Conversation with her.  Here is Wikipedia ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katja_Hoyer ) :

> Hoyer was born in Wilhelm-Pieck-Stadt Guben ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm-Pieck-Stadt_Guben ) , Bezirk Cottbus ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bezirk_Cottbus ) , German Democratic Republic ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Democratic_Republic )  (GDR), where her mother was a teacher and her father an officer of the National People's Army ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_People%27s_Army ) . She received a Master's degree ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master%27s_degree )  from the University of Jena ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena )  and moved to the United Kingdom in about 2010.
>
> Hoyer is a visiting research fellow at King's College London ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King%27s_College_London )  and has published two books about the history of Germany ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Germany ) . She is also a journalist for The Spectator ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Spectator ) , The Washington Post ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post ) , Times Literary Supplement ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times_Literary_Supplement ) , UnHerd ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UnHerd ) , and Die Welt ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Welt ) .
>
> Her first book, Blood and Iron, about the German Empire ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire )  from 1871 to 1918, was well reviewed, even though some reviewers suggested that she had played down the negative aspects of the period and of Otto von Bismarck ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_von_Bismarck ) 's legacy. Her second book, Beyond the Wall ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyond_the_Wall_(2023_book) ) , about the history of the GDR from 1949 to 1990, was well reviewed in the United Kingdom, but less well received in Germany.

She also has a new, forthcoming book on the history of the city of Weimar, namely Weimar: Life on the Edge of Catastrophe ( https://www.amazon.com/dp/1541605799/?bestFormat=true&k=katja%20hoyer%20weimar&ref_=nb_sb_ss_w_scx-ent-bk-v2_k2_1_11_de&crid=3C07C0UHDLLOM&sprefix=katja%20hoyer ) .  So what should I ask her?
