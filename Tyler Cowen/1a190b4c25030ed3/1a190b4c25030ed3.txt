Post       : The pass-through of retail crime
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/10/the-pass-through-of-retail-crime.html
Posted     : October 31, 2026 at 2:44 am
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Data Source, Economics, Law, Uncategorized

> This paper shows retailers increase prices in response to organized retail crime. We match store-level crime data to scanner data from the universe of transactions for cannabis retailers in Washington state. Using quasi-experimental variation from robberies and burglaries, we find a 1.7–1.8 percent price increase at victimized stores and nearby competitors. This rise is not driven by short-to-medium-term demand changes but is consistent with crime-related cost shocks. Effects are larger for independent stores and less concentrated markets. We estimate crime imposes a 1 percent "hidden" unit tax on affected stores, implying $30.7 million in additional social costs, primarily borne by consumers.

That is from a newly published paper ( https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/pol.20240889 ) , AEJ: Economic Policy, by Carl Hase and Johannes Kasinger.
