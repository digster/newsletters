Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Books, History, Law

> The most controversial of the forced removals occurred in the second half of the 1960s, with the expulsion of 65,000 coloureds from District Six, a vibrant inner-city ward of Cape Town, where whites, many of the slumlords, owned 56% of the property.  Against their will, District Six residents were moved out to the sandy townships of the Cape Flats.  In Johannesburg, the inner-city suburb of Sophiatown, where blacks could own freehold property, was another notorious site of forced removals.  Often long-established community institutions such as churches and schools had to be abandoned.

That is from the very good book by Hermann Giliomee The Afrikaners: A Concise History ( https://www.amazon.com/Afrikaners-Concise-History-Hermann-Giliomee-ebook/dp/B08NV5NTHF/ref=sr_1_1?crid=110INZ49KPFY9&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.aWODkhrqNJvKmTnwhd_BVMFK3M7mN9x4Jua9KZfxwvVwSJHDh1xayW88GdIILnXzNOXPaHO0Xu2FrdI2J4jj5xpZHabr3TqzlFtekc0s857ziwFkK73s8I-vQGTBPJz6M1kmR6qHb2225TadfVQtWHcVqQXCGrwCMfCrXvlaBLBw-H-6peVsjd7B3Gjr2XL3EhOo4xbL3Hehwt-m8vpYvbk55SEVM-RtWlKMazVZI4M.5Bx6yBHCFS8KjQVxXbnQIHnj8OysMJWABEpds9Hd7nA&dib_tag=se&keywords=The+Afrikaners%3A+A+Concise+History&qid=1773519561&sprefix=%2Caps%2C1217&sr=8-1 ) .
