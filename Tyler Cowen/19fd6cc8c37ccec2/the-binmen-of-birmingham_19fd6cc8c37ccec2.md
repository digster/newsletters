One thing I discussed in the podcast which I haven't blogged on is the amazing Birmingham dustbin dispute.
In 2010 an employment tribunal ruled that Birmingham City Council had discriminated against thousands of female workers — cooks, cleaners, care assistants, caretakers — who were denied bonuses paid to the mostly male binmen, gardeners, and gravediggers. Why were the binmen given bonuses? Well, refuse collection is filthy, heavy, outdoor work and not many people want to be gravediggers. Thus, these jobs command a premium for exactly the reason [Adam Smith gave in 1776](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=923feec7350cce74dfd12c775c72e11c4ac80b6a4303ebd3d1782b78aafde7d0&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93548&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=e05c291282ff0e42b258a67d05659dd7&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZWNvbmxpYi5vcmcvbGlicmFyeS9TbWl0aC9zbVdOLmh0bWw/Y2hhcHRlcl9udW09MTMjYm9vay1yZWFkZXI&email_id=b786251bce4b6f4f53fb44250975c2cb) — [compensating differentials](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=bdb50e3fe57b054683ed11009314fe10abf33a92923701b570f94e8e6bf4712c&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93548&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=d1523e6a085c4d2ef178a0181d8b808c&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly9tYXJnaW5hbHJldm9sdXRpb24uY29tL21hcmdpbmFscmV2b2x1dGlvbi8yMDE1LzA0L2NvbXBlbnNhdGluZy12YXJpYXRpb25zLmh0bWw&email_id=b786251bce4b6f4f53fb44250975c2cb). Or was it sexism? Well, note first that there is nothing stopping women from becoming "binpersons" and indeed there are female binpersons and they earn the same bonuses as their male counterparts (just as with the [Next case](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=567f8e83832bada8f61cbb863ef4845aac18fc02c42b0e7f700c08026aadb021&blog_id=42693868&post_id=93548&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=52a9925da31cc97b852684e5cf7ee1c3&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly9tYXJnaW5hbHJldm9sdXRpb24uY29tL21hcmdpbmFscmV2b2x1dGlvbi8yMDI0LzA5L2VxdWFsaXR5LWFjdC0yMDEwLmh0bWw&email_id=b786251bce4b6f4f53fb44250975c2cb)). Moreover, we can test the sexism versus compensating differentials theory. Let's see what happened.
Here's the problem, which contributed to Birmingham going bankrupt in 2023. The council employs roughly 400 binmen and something like 6,000 women in comparable graded roles. Every extra pound paid to a binman therefore implied about fifteen pounds owed to the cooks and cleaners. The council simply didn't have the money to pay everyone binman wages so they cut the binmen's wages. But the market wage for collecting rubbish is what it is, so when Birmingham finally deleted the premium in January 2025, the binmen went on strike — and they are still on strike, nearly eighteen months later. The rubbish piled up, 17,000 tonnes of it, and the city declared a major incident.
Here's the most amazing part. The council hired an outside contractor to take over its rubbish collection and it now pays roughly triple its pre-strike outsourcing bill--more than it was paying its own employees. So much for sexism. Apparently the premium wasn't a favor to men; it was the price of the job. If you want your rubbish picked up and your graves dug, you must pay the market wage. This was pure regulatory arbitrage, of course. Because the new binmen were contractors they legally had a different employer than the Council's female caregivers and the Act's comparator rules stop at the employer's nexus.
