Yes I will be doing a Conversation with her.  She has a new and very interesting book coming out, namely The Time Tax: How the Government Wastes Our Time―and How to Fix It ( https://www.amazon.com/Time-Tax-Government-Wastes-Time_and/dp/0063442558/ref=sr_1_1?crid=26IUEBFINPWAD&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.uGgyyxuWDYCnWag8zsxby03x3dahrvRSsvd5tqmrOqNxcDGa2hQy8noqXU7_PgvaMU8wbSBzC-UwwbL_9QfEjw.hxtZSoVcZu6kWBSguc1PKR6R0e26GJXBGcriar141qE&dib_tag=se&keywords=annie%2Blowrey&qid=1787333783&sprefix=annie%2Blowrey%2Caps%2C159&sr=8-1 ) .  More generally, from Wikipedia ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Lowrey ) :

> Annie M. Lowrey...is an American journalist who writes on politics and economic policy for The Atlantic ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Atlantic ) . Previously, Lowrey covered economic policy for The New York Times ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times )  and prior to that was the Moneybox columnist for Slate ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slate_(magazine) ) . She was also a staff writer ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staff_writer )  for the Washington Independent ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Independent )  and was on the editorial staffs of Foreign Policy ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Policy )  and The New Yorker ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Yorker ) . She is a proponent of universal basic income ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_basic_income ) .

So what should I ask her?
