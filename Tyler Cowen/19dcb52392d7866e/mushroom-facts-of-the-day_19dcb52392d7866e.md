You would be surprised to learn that almost
[69% of the US mushroom production]occurs in the borough of Kennett Square, Pennsylvania. It is a small town of about 6000 people, but mushroom-growing facilities around town produce almost[451 million pounds of mushrooms annually](2024). 451 million pounds of mushrooms would occupy about 45 American football fields or 35 soccer fields. The dollar value of mushroom production in the US is roughly $ 1 billion per year.China is the undisputed leader in mushroom production. China accounts for 93% of the world’s global mushroom production.
