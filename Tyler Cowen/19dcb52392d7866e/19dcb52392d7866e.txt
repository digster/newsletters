Author     : Tyler Cowen

> You would be surprised to learn that almost 69% of the US mushroom production ( https://pennsylvaniaindependent.com/politics/pennsylvania-is-home-to-the-worlds-mushroom-capital/ )  occurs in the borough of Kennett Square, Pennsylvania. It is a small town of about 6000 people, but mushroom-growing facilities around town produce almost 451 million pounds of mushrooms annually ( https://esmis.nal.usda.gov/sites/default/release-files/r781wg03d/9593wm52z/9306vq05c/mush0824.pdf )  (2024). 451 million pounds of mushrooms would occupy about 45 American football fields or 35 soccer fields. The dollar value of mushroom production in the US is roughly $ 1 billion per year.
>
> China is the undisputed leader in mushroom production. China accounts for 93% of the world’s global mushroom production.

That is from Rhishi Pethe, here is the full story ( https://sftw.substack.com/p/the-case-of-missing-american-mushrooms ) , via Anecdotal ( https://x.com/anecdotal ) .  Much of the piece is about why mushroom production is switching to Canada.
