Post       : *Finding Emily*
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/08/finding-emily.html
Posted     : August 30, 2026 at 1:52 am
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Uncategorized

I very much enjoyed this British movie ( https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OGuJf70S0s ) , which reminded me of the older-style romantic comedies, namely the ones that actually favor love and romance.  It also has themes of class/accent, US/UK, is anti-media, anti-cancel culture, anti-podcast (Laura Lewis steals the show), anti-academia, and is sceptical of certain branches of feminism.  But it is mostly entertaining.  Not an important film, but the sort of movie that makes going to the movies fun in the first place.  Let's hope for more like this one.
