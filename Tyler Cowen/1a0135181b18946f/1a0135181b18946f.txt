Post       : Morocco facts of the day, Morocco is not hopeless
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/08/morocco-facts-of-the-day-morocco-is-not-hopeless.html
Posted     : August 18, 2026 at 1:18 am
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Current Affairs

> For decades, South Africa was the great success story of African industrialization. Last year, Morocco took the lead, emerging as the African Development Bank’s top-ranked industrial economy in Africa. In the Bank’s analysis, published in the latest edition ( https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/am2026-morocco-named-africas-industrialization-leader-new-african-development-bank-index-93601 )  of its industrialization index, Morocco’s growth has been “driven by sustained industrial upgrading, export diversification, and strong industrial policy.” Since the mid-2000s, it has leveraged its location and dominant phosphate reserves to get free trade agreements with the US, the EU, and fifty other countries: the Kingdom would like Morocco to be to France what Eastern Europe is to Germany, or what Mexico is to the US: an export-base “workbench.”
>
> Having positioned itself to profit from the shift to clean energy supply, Morocco is nimbly pivoting ( https://static1.squarespace.com/static/64ca7e081e376c26a5319f0b/t/6509dcd23997ca57fb0bcc2f/1695145170321/GP01+Morocco+vf.pdf )  its auto manufacturing sector to build EVs, working with incumbents like Renault and Stellantis, and is host to Africa’s first battery gigafactory. That single 70 gigawatt-hour battery factory in Casablanca is expected ( https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2025-09-09/china-is-winning-its-power-play-for-the-global-south?embedded-checkout=true&srnd=undefined )  to supply a third of Europe’s current market. Last year, the government approved construction for a new green hydrogen ( https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/sustainable-finance-reporting/morocco-approves-green-hydrogen-projects-worth-325-bln-2025-03-06/ )  industrial park, aiming to produce ammonia, steel, and industrial fuel worth a total of $32.5 billion. It has also been strategic in focusing its industrial strategy on aerospace manufacturing, with Airbus ( https://northafricapost.com/91241-airbus-deepens-industrial-presence-in-morocco-with-new-investments.html )  becoming a strategic partner, along with investments from Boeing, Thales, and Safran. Its aerospace exports more than tripled in the decade to 2024, to reach almost $3 billion.
