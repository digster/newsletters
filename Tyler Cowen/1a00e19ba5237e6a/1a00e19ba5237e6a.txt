Post       : What I&#8217;ve been reading
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/08/what-ive-been-reading-294.html
Posted     : August 17, 2026 at 12:58 am
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Books, Uncategorized

1. Gerald Martin, Mario Vargas Llosa: A Life ( https://www.amazon.com/Mario-Vargas-Llosa-Gerald-Martin/dp/1639737723/ref=sr_1_1?crid=2P6MQ6J2FBQTF&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.KYi8qateS8KCUkIxETEglphkoBivgkdNjNohPieEmiPPWTxHDow7rE0suogMTVMd3_II-oCI9ubvaAwA_NqlMObm3tUJ-1-H2jajHuxqv4ad10fohJntB6ESR7oyupiafdGjRLAy4TF6fFInajGI32jxkazTjfnYDvf4VvQzX4myX_MyKIguXaqsqz1McZBvw5JocRWKfOTUf3T1nnccapDMx9Bk9S-sZFnWXonUWMWQ1Ua8yoG-EJItlSOFFZng0SdBl6mC2s38279cMsOEJu2aWxhKXyd_SEIcJetgDtA.XNb52m1UlPkJAa-IKLEmE7x0Yy5wemcmiJ1hVzy9D2s&dib_tag=se&keywords=mario+vargas+llosa&qid=1786883349&sprefix=mario+vargas+ll%2Caps%2C1178&sr=8-1 ) .  A good book if you wish to read about his strange sex life, starting early in his years.  But if you are more interested in the history of ideas, Peruvian politics, or the development of the Latin American novel it is far less compelling.  So for me a disappointment.

2. Sean Mathews, The New Byzantines: The Rise of Greece and Return of the Near East ( https://www.amazon.com/New-Byzantines-Rise-Greece-Return/dp/1805264184/ref=sr_1_1?crid=X8ZJN76MK8QO&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.er0k3ZlyC48nyXpF-3mjDfgqEeLAqTWAhmiYJTkeGd-KPZP1mloKYv_y-iaAtD_DJHjzqdf8JX1TUfVhLlwdiYdALSnODDfOJAbPG3js3fHkyUVj2llNeoLjViihwobkrgzU9wFh18VQfuvexddG6vVcGZyvWd1tzDw5l0s9Tqg0nHabAyv-PE03Of2ds3ieI5bfCAegQp_ZwAmrhFjBq0Usv9sBTs6ThEpfg4YjALg.4WBF7mUDtcTazcmxKmM-efd1OYg32Oeffn4VA4aXyW0&dib_tag=se&keywords=The+New+Byzantines%3A+The+Rise+of+Greece+and+Return+of+the+Near+East&nsdOptOutParam=true&qid=1786883462&sprefix=mari%2Caps%2C1604&sr=8-1 ) .  A consistently interesting look at Greece, the Ottoman roots in the area, the radical changes brought by the early 20th century, and Greece's relations with Israel, among other topics.  The book has the virtue of "trying to explain how things actually are," sadly not the case for all works.

3. Simon Morrison, The Love & Wars of Lina Prokofiev ( https://www.amazon.com/Lina-Serge-Love-Wars-Prokofiev/dp/0547391315/ref=sr_1_1?crid=2C5OPSXJFGXP4&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.pv0SMAD5zR_B58e-3XcFsY3SzNhSaPhwgVjTlT4zY_M.aqC3t17ZHscouF6sWrUmFKsr1w2aQGFMYCsjWjtWLRE&dib_tag=se&keywords=simon+morrison+love+%26+war+prokofiev&qid=1786884071&sprefix=simon+morrison+love+%26+war+prokofi%2Caps%2C1080&sr=8-1 ) .  More suspense and interest for me than any biography of Prokofiev I have read.  I had not known that she grew up in Brooklyn.  I am happy to recommend Morrison's books more generally.

4. Emily Wilson, Crossing the Wine-Dark Sea: Journeys through Ancient Literature ( https://www.amazon.com/Crossing-Wine-Dark-Sea-Journeys-Literature-ebook/dp/B0GTWS27SS/ref=sr_1_3?crid=J6RBCOU8N0R5&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.nlWA8zfW0ONtRngjmndejGLJgEkgbsSxP5Vzov5pSSMsYdytAaxxDjelxgEhIYo5z62eppYkz1oeMTJ-sIvtX-7mwubYI9BLmBM4kcCkbv3VQfVfAH5v0Qp33H3J0M16heHjqy2b4aBjsFo5mjwI2lV_ElyxRx8opfpZhOc-ldpvi_oj4vHoP5Z5dAKdPM4jxFpGt6MjU3cWNzLE1vy6ul4YB0Y9QUmMsRJ0fzQqS2Q.MzoyotAhsWGNgitalVjXRfHn9fCopqhepAs3qNxN16c&dib_tag=se&keywords=Cross+the+Wine-Dark+Sea&qid=1786884305&sprefix=simon+morris%2Caps%2C1736&sr=8-3 ) .  Wilson has become a controversial figure for a variety of not-so-merited reasons, but the simpler truth is this.  She is a superior intellectual presence and mind, so you should be reading what she writes, no matter what your stances.  My two favorite essays in this volume were the one on Logue's War Music, and the closing piece on translations of The Odyssey.

5. Munir Hachemi, The Mulai ( https://www.amazon.com/Mulai-Munir-Hachemi/dp/155245519X/ref=sr_1_1?crid=1O0QYL9I2NUJC&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.Xr_bhu43mOBpBpXxnyH4eA.s9__tyADxNE7SMyIdqtOne60hdwgtQiQ5NiaYVYbjUg&dib_tag=se&keywords=the+mulai+munir+hachemi&qid=1786885284&sprefix=the+mulai%2Caps%2C1280&sr=8-1 ) .  A somewhat loopy but intriguing science fiction story, broadly in the direction of Ursula LeGuin.  I am not sure what to think, but definitely will keep on reading.
