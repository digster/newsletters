For a while now, we have been hearing claims that social media radicalize the American electorate, and that social media damage teen mental health.
Well, we just held a major set of elections that produced resoundingly [moderate results](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=4ac58731e65bd01810fe04ae0f5083f4103f2a0a3e5362c13e645e0b9738b0e6&blog_id=42693868&post_id=94136&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=cf99c352de902a8196a54def71faffd8&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly94LmNvbS9yc2FudGk5Ny9zdGF0dXMvMjExNzgwNTQ5NjY2OTI4MjgwNz9zPTQ2JnQ9ZlN5cEZ4dDg0YkJISWQtZG85cjVBZw=&email_id=01c518663eae66a091e5c7037c50aba7), repudiations of both Trump and the extreme Democratic left. You may or may not like those results, but they are pretty clearly moderate.
And [the recent trends in teen mental health are positive](https://marginalrevolution.com?action=user_content_redirect&uuid=6c500edbcb70e32eb8c1b06e4123274e2c3ebbd4182754b8a31a47dc88eae81c&blog_id=42693868&post_id=94136&user_id=262258391&subs_id=225460721&signature=877642ebd9d0bc9fd8291a45f3b7b2fb&email_name=new-post&user_email=ishan.mail@gmail.com&encoded_url=aHR0cHM6Ly9ncmltb2lyZW1hbm9yLnN1YnN0YWNrLmNvbS9wL3RpbWUtdHJlbmQtZGF0YS1ub3ctdHVybmluZy1hZ2FpbnN0P3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9Y2hhdGdwdC5jb20&email_id=01c518663eae66a091e5c7037c50aba7). All the while social media usage continues unabated, and furthermore short video continues to rise.
Neither the political nor the mental health trend is guaranteed to continue, but this is a sign that the standard anti-social media polemics are radically incomplete and currently failing in their predictions.
