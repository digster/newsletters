> Instead of Amazon, I spent the past week browsing a new breed of websites known as “dopamine sites,” a trend that emerged in South Korea. These websites — like Dopamine Shop ( https://dopamineshop.co/ )  and FoodNeverComes ( https://foodnevercomes.com/#menu )  — recreate the entire ritual of online shopping: You search for products, compare reviews, add items to your cart, enter a shipping address, place an order, and even track your delivery.
>
> Then … nothing happens. No money changes hands. No package arrives.
