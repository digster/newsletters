The subtitle is The Politics of Feasible Liberalism ( https://www.amazon.com/Equality-Permission-Politics-Feasible-Liberalism/dp/0226852393/ref=sr_1_1?crid=32YAABNB8OCUX&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.z_MnooU2ghBQjmURR6btJrd90lewJzubT0GwJBAWb8U.jY_g1r-KD1pvBC_5Lo5RVxZvaGK_tFudyBoscacqauk&dib_tag=se&keywords=equality+of+permission&qid=1779538143&sprefix=equality+of+permission%2Caps%2C178&sr=8-1 ) , and the author is Deirdre Nansen McCloskey.  The author wishes to argue for liberalism as opposed to statism, a very good book.  And unlike many authors, Deirdre also tells you what she thinks of everyone else's views.  Due out in November.
