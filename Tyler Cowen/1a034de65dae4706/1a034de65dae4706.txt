Post       : Common sense in charge
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/08/common-sense-in-charge.html
Posted     : August 24, 2026 at 1:39 pm
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Data Source, Education, Uncategorized

> ...children are increasingly seen as interfering with the freedom of parents; views on whether mothers of young children should work have become markedly more progressive and account for a substantially larger share of the decline among the tertiary-educated; and fewer people believe that women or men need children to lead a fulfilled life. This last attitude changed most over the decade and is the largest contributor to the fall in intended fertility, above all among less-educated women.

That is from a new NBER working paper ( https://www.nber.org/papers/w35651#fromrss )  by  Raquel Fernández,  ( https://www.nber.org/people/raquel_fernandez ) Inés Berniell, and  ( https://www.nber.org/people/ines_berniell ) Milagros Onofri. ( https://www.nber.org/people/milagros_onofri )
