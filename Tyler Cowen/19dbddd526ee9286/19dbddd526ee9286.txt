Post       : What I&#8217;ve been reading
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/04/what-ive-been-reading-286.html
Posted     : April 24, 2026 at 12:58 am
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Books

1. Mason Currey, Making Art and Making a Living: Adventures in Funding a Creative Life ( https://www.amazon.com/Making-Art-Living-Adventures-Creative/dp/1250824524/ref=sr_1_1?crid=305F9Z7JKYD2X&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.Cmu1YC7-eccalorcDpyzlg.uewPW8nPy_vuIxfgWBS49N4rnm2KHy44xFNVGDfhUOk&dib_tag=se&keywords=Making+Art+and+Making+a+Living%3A+Adventures+in+Funding+a+Creative+Life&nsdOptOutParam=true&qid=1776960006&sprefix=making+art+and+making+a+living+adventures+in+funding+a+creative+life%2Caps%2C228&sr=8-1 ) .  The best overall book I know on the different methods top artists have used to keep themselves going financially.  It is perhaps more anecdotal and less theoretical than I would prefer, still a nice work.

2. Mangol Bayat, Mysticism and Dissent: Socioreligious Dissent in Qajar Iran ( https://www.amazon.com/Mysticism-Dissent-Socioreligious-Thought-Quajar/dp/0815622600/ref=sr_1_1?crid=244FRDF1UNNH9&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.gaM82FReAN62Futr0oet8Eh6QetGa-WDV6EmmjIG4j4.Ew1yXy4v-KTxbXJ9ZAVP3iQ_UuBETG-BmxSE3bTMdUI&dib_tag=se&keywords=Mysticism+and+Dissent%3A+Socioreligious+Dissent+in+Qajar+Iran&nsdOptOutParam=true&qid=1776959953&s=books&sprefix=mysticism+and+dissent+socioreligious+dissent+in+qajar+iran%2Cstripbooks%2C515&sr=1-1 ) .  A very good, clear, and useful book on different dissident religiouis developments in Iran, leading up to the Bahai faith.  Recommended, one of the best books I have found for grappling with the history of current Iran.

3. Lena Dunham, Famesick: A Memoir ( https://www.amazon.com/Famesick-Memoir-Lena-Dunham/dp/0593129326/ref=sr_1_1?crid=2Y289ZODKVWEI&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.mfcGkbcUmKPm1_IJiPKI_-QTvQm7uJrP94VkxKNUmxjxlVp-4pZcy_CGigkEHoaQ.2gY1GrLQ1el_PEXjQqXv9nqvYB-n2VmcpWAJl56Xtq4&dib_tag=se&keywords=Famesick%3A+A+Memoir&qid=1776959889&s=books&sprefix=famesick+a+memoir%2Cstripbooks%2C751&sr=1-1 ) .  Not exactly my thing, so I did not finish it.  But it is pretty good, so if you are tempted give it a try.

4. Iain Pears, Parallel Lives: A Love Story from a Lost Continent ( https://www.amazon.com/Parallel-Lives-Love-Story-Continent/dp/1324073772/ref=sr_1_1?crid=TMNCXR3BTEZA&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.h9k3cr_ihpYRuZo-IFdY1r8op_uQL0cW7iNej06Eu0kTjg6eVIYjE8zvy4OleHnuM6SxzBQfm84kNVAtAaleAv1eFCL0-W0fVVojt4dWqiRVzEsQUE__Ldmz1ybGsD_gDjS5NCN_vBYmsb5IlEyLYmRNhn405YurAL1S2nCQ_SqdHbNpgfawpAGWD0jaRM1SfrDDpgFEA2RFNWxq_Eg093tVoqRjtRN2IgwtzUQDG-0.OtVGGJ2PK7zd_TYisUgEh3uiU3t2tgF1Lxhwbfw0Rys&dib_tag=se&keywords=Parallel+Lives%3A+A+Love+Story+from+a+Lost+Continent&nsdOptOutParam=true&qid=1776959844&s=books&sprefix=parallel+lives+a+love+story+from+a+lost+continent%2Cstripbooks%2C359&sr=1-1 ) .  A delightful story/indirect memoir, telling the tale of the lives and marriage of Frances Haskell, the British art historian, and Larissa Salmina Haskell, a Russian woman who survived the siege of Leningrad as a girl.  Pears had the full cooperation of Larissa, at an age where she doesn't give a damn any more.  This story truly comes to life, and that is helped by Pears's background as a writer of very good fiction.

5. Lázár ( https://www.amazon.com/L%C3%A1z%C3%A1r-Novel-Nelio-Biedermann/dp/1668200554/ref=sr_1_1?crid=3QHZ80KZMARV7&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.obeBVqH27htgwc3nhQ2CqiQHPJ0ujZis4Lpnc1zSiabZALzpKSUgMqPM2YlTSJu42ehCS9RvQv2-4Y5WTBQsph8JvOPELX-qpOeZTbPwkyI.otxmDdV0an2cHWvL3K0RHqylSl_u_WVRQybPeT16XxQ&dib_tag=se&keywords=Nelio+Biedermann&qid=1776959798&s=books&sprefix=nelio+biedermann%2Cstripbooks%2C160&sr=1-1 ) , by Nelio Biedermann.  An excellent novel of ideas, in the style of earlier Continental literature, by a 23-year-old Swiss phenom.  It is very good in German, I have not sampled the translation.
