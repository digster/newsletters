Post       : Short Videos, Big Self-Control Problems
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/09/short-videos-big-self-control-problems.html
Posted     : September 5, 2026 at 2:11 pm
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Data Source, Web/Tech

> I study how short-form design amplifies self-control problems in digital media. Short units repeatedly renew temptation that lasts longer than each unit, turning local temptation into sustained overconsumption. Using microdata from a U.S. short-drama platform, I exploit a nonlinear top-up menu to infer viewing plans and show that paying users watch 82.1% more than intended. Structural estimates imply an average temptation horizon of 11.2 minutes, short relative to the full drama but long relative to one-minute episodes. Counterfactuals show that larger decision units, default limits, and breaks improve long-run welfare. A short-video calibration highlights the broader welfare relevance.

That is from Renjie Bao of Princeton University ( https://renjie-bao.github.io/uploads/Just_One_More_Clip.pdf ) .  I believe a Princeton job market candidate?  Via Quan Le ( https://x.com/qlquanle/status/2094406208027283761 ) .
