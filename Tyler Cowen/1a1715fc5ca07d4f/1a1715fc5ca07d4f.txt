Post       : Emergent Ventures India, 20th cohort
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/10/emergent-ventures-india-20th-cohort.html
Posted     : October 25, 2026 at 12:42 am
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Education

Zahan Turel ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/zahanturel ) , a backend engineer and eighth-generation Zoroastrian priest from Surat, received a grant to digitize his community’s manuscripts and liturgical records before they are lost.

Aditya Vijay ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/aditya-vijayk ) , 17, is building Forge ( https://www.forgeresidency.com/ ) , supporting overlooked, self-taught young founders with shared housing, capital, and access.

Dev Goyal ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/dev-d-goyal-a2747a419/?isSelfProfile=false )  received a grant for SCRIPT, exploring whether stylus-based drawing kinematics can support non-diagnostic clinical triage for ADHD, autism, and anxiety.

Adwait Tiwari ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/adwait-tiwari-b9a0813a8/ ) , 16, a self-taught founder from Indore, is building BharatBahi, a WhatsApp-native AI assistant helping Indian shopkeepers.

Mayukh Goswami ( https://www.mayukhgoswami.com/about )  and Meher Manda ( https://mehermanda.com/ )  received their grant for a graphic novel examining democratic crises around the world through the lens of a fictional Indian town.

Ajith Chekka ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/ajith-sai )  is building Oru'el ( http://www.oru-el.com ) , a physics-enabled intelligence layer to improve the reliability and efficiency of data centers.

Gaurav Kumar ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/gaurav-r-b14192ba ) , a software engineer and social entrepreneur, is building OncoVault, which turns fragmented cancer records into AI-generated clinical summaries to support physicians’ treatment decisions.

Kush Agrawal ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/kush-agrawal-211a131ba/ ) , an engineering student at IIT Bombay, received a grant for Tensor Recon ( https://tensorrecon.com ) , developing execution-aware financial reconciliation infrastructure for high-volume cryptocurrency trading firms.

Mohd. Ashzad ( https://www.skshadan.com/ ) , 22, a security researcher and engineer, is building Build Jam ( https://www.buildjam.co/ ) , helping young South Asian builders discover creative skills through real-world projects in AI, design, filmmaking, and photography.

Vaibhav Sai ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/vaibhav-sai-sai ) , 25, received a grant to develop AI for intent-based biomolecular design, starting with a nucleotide-focused model and capture molecules for biosensors.

Rithvik Vibhu ( https://blek.space )  and Rithwik Jayasimha ( https://rithwik.me )  received their grant for research on passive optical fiber network vulnerabilities, including wiretapping and attacks on ISP infrastructure, and presentations at Black Hat USA 2026 and DEF CON 34.

Neha Sarda ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/neha-sohan-sarda/ )  is building Zyogen, sovereign AI software converting injury images into patient-specific printable graft designs and bioink formulations, aiming to make regenerative medicine 30–40% more affordable.

Hari Shankar Ramalingam ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/harshan05/ ) , cofounder and CEO of SpaceTug ( https://spacetug.tech ) , received a grant to develop and validate autonomous spacecraft technologies for in-orbit satellite servicing, inspection, and life extension, ahead of orbital demonstrations.

Laiba Noor ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/laiba-noor-a91484248/ )  received a travel grant to attend a research program at Özyeğin University and develop a speech-analysis tool for detecting presymptomatic Alzheimer’s in low-resource settings.

Abhishek Raj ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/karma-gray-5b12423a7/ ) , 16, a crime and mystery writer and dual national record holder, received a grant to advance his fiction writing and criminology research.

Kunal Kanase ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/thekunalkanase ) , an IIT Bombay pre-doctoral fellow, received a grant for GullyWell ( https://www.youtube.com/@GullyWell ) , studying Dharavi  residents’ wellbeing where formal mental-health care rarely reaches and developing low-cost tools informed by that research.

Divyaratna Joshi ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/divyaratna-joshi-3829b7172/ ) , a Chemical Engineering undergraduate at IIT Madras, is developing low-cost S-PEEK membranes to replace expensive fluorinated membranes and make fuel cells and electrolyzers affordable and manufacturable at scale.

Tanjot Singh ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/tanjotsingh27 ) , 16, received a grant for PraaNavya ( https://tanjotbuilds.top/ )  (Erynth), a climate startup developing bio-based nanofiber air filtration technology for sustainable clean air.

Tribhi Kathuria ( https://sites.google.com/umich.edu/tribhi-kathuria/home ) , a Lecturer in Robotics at the University of Michigan, received a grant to connect aspiring Indian engineers with robotics and AI research and mentorship from experts in the United States.

Swamynathan Jerra ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/swamynathanjerra ) , founder of Hyderabad’s AcquaHT Labs, received a grant to upgrade and seek patents for machines extracting drinking water from air at 30% humidity, rather than the usual 50%, for dry areas with unsafe groundwater.

Niharika Srivastava ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/niharika-srivastava-49030a17a ) , a criminal justice and technology lawyer, received a grant to develop a rule-based system identifying Indian undertrial prisoners held beyond statutory custody limits, enabling targeted legal intervention.

Anirudh Varma ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/anirudh-varma-76754212b ) , a New Delhi pianist, composer, and producer, leads the Anirudh Varma Collective ( https://open.spotify.com/artist/3bD7WaIwFeJMNv8smNGODB )  (AVC), received a travel grant for reimagining Indian classical music in a contemporary format for its debut UK tour.

Himaneesh ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/himaneesh-sompalle-73000237a )  received a grant to build AI software that simplifies professional digital audio workstation workflows.

Sahasrabda Sai Pradhan ( https://in.linkedin.com/in/sahasrabda-sai-pradhan-859994211 )  received a grant to develop a skin-regenerative biomaterial that can be fabricated on-site for urgent graft substitution when suitable tissue is unavailable from the patient’s donor site.

Gaurav Hadavale ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/gaurav-hadavale-5b3a5b291/ ) , 21, a final-year undergraduate at the Sardar Patel Institute of Technology, received a travel grant to present his mechanistic interpretability and trustworthy medical AI research at the MI4MedFM workshop at MICCAI 2026.

M R Sharan ( https://www.mrsharan.com/ ) , an economist, received a grant to establish a data and intelligence platform focused on India’s local bodies.

Nitish Goel ( https://www.instagram.com/nitish.goel_tech_god/?__d=1%252F ) , 14, from Panchkula, is building Thalamus ( https://thalamus.aphantic.skinticals.com/ )  to reduce AI costs and improve efficiency, and Agentoverflow ( https://agentoverflow.aphantic.skinticals.com/ ) , a shared repository of agents’ solutions that reduces duplicated work and supplies model-training data.

Sudharsanam ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/sudharsanam-s-121baa252/ )  received a grant for Hardlight ( http://hardlightspace.com/ ) , developing autonomous space robots to assemble and maintain assets in low Earth orbit, including space stations, data centers, and factories.

Naveen Gulati ( https://givara.energy/ ) , Noida, received a grant for instrumented rooftop trials of GIVARA ONE, a compact dual-rotor vertical-axis turbine for low, gusty winds. Givara Energy ( https://givara.energy/ )  owns, installs, and maintains the machines; customers pay only for delivered electricity.

Daevan Mangalmurti ( https://www.linkedin.com/in/daevan-mangalmurti/ )  and Bilal Moin ( https://www.bilalmoin.com/ )  received their grant for Kindly Adjust ( https://www.kindlyadjust.com/welcome ) , a podcast and Substack discussing small fixes for India’s big problems.
