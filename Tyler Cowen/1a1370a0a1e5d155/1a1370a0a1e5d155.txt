He and I were in the same Harvard class together, and spent a good deal of time talking economics together.  We also were (briefly) bridge partners in a few games.  I was a big fan of his book on Fischer Black ( https://www.amazon.com/Fischer-Black-Revolutionary-Idea-Finance/dp/0471457329/ref=sr_1_1?crid=2F3EDG4J3LNBA&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.lOT0IGTSiyCeUKxHIKAqAbtcZR9r-ZPNAwQvnuBl_bTGjHj071QN20LucGBJIEps.2W8JoGzFB_jv1vXJRveQXsOZsJh6zRu0q6pNdJyBKxQ&dib_tag=se&keywords=perry+mehrling+fischer+black&qid=1791923524&sprefix=perry+mehrling+fischer+black%2Caps%2C141&sr=8-1 ) .  I am very sorry to see him gone ( https://x.com/int_mon_econ/status/2110094844475449514 ) .
