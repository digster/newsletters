A New Jersey Girl Scout troop has taken cookie sales to new heights, setting up shop right outside a popular cannabis dispensary.
A South Jersey-based troop recently teamed up with
[Daylite Dispensary]in Mount Laurel to sell their beloved cookies at the cannabis shop this cookie season.“You use cannabis, you get the munchies,” Daylite Dispensary owner Steve Cassidy told NJ.com “There’s a connection between snacks and cannabis and the fact that we don’t have to pretend that doesn’t exist anymore is really awesome.”
Daylite became Mount Laurel’s first dispensary when it opened in 2023. Cassidy said the idea was proposed back in 2024, but it was turned down by
[Girl Scouts of Central & Southern New Jersey], the Girl Scout council that oversees troops in the region.When the idea reemerged ahead of this year’s cookie season, the troop was allowed to sell cookies at Daylite on a trial basis, according to Cassidy.
