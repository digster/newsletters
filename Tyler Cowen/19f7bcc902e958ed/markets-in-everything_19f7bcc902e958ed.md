[Peter Brook]‘s five-hour-plus film adaptation of “The[Mahabharata]” is set for a[BFI]Blu-ray release on Aug. 10, presented in high definition from a new 4K restoration of the 1989 production.The release centres on the triptych version of the film, running 332 minutes, which sits between Brook’s three-hour cinema cut and the six-hour television version he shot concurrently. Based on the ancient Hindu epic, the film was originally produced by Brook with screenwriters Jean-Claude Carrière and Marie-Hélène Estienne, and features an international cast drawn from 16 nationalities.
