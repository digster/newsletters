Yes I will be doing a Conversation with him, live at the Roots of Progress event next week.  From Wikipedia ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moxie_Marlinspike ) :

> Moxie Marlinspike is an American entrepreneur ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepreneur ) , cryptographer ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer ) , and computer security ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security )  researcher. Marlinspike is the creator of Signal ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_(messaging_app) ) , co-founder of the Signal Technology Foundation ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_Technology_Foundation ) , and served as the first CEO of Signal Messenger LLC ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_Messenger_LLC ) . He is also a co-author of the Signal Protocol ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_Protocol )  encryption used by Signal, WhatsApp ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WhatsApp ) , Google Messages ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Messages ) , Facebook Messenger ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook_Messenger )  and Skype ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skype ) .

There is much more at the link, for instance he is also an anarchist of some kind or another.  So what should I ask him?
