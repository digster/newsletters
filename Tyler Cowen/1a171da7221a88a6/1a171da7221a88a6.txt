Post       : Facts about breastfeeding
URL        : https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/10/facts-about-breastfeeding.html
Posted     : October 25, 2026 at 2:57 am
Author     : Tyler Cowen
Categories : Data Source, Economics, History, Uncategorized

> We produce a numerical estimate of the value of unpaid breastfeeding relative to national income spanning five centuries and integrating historical and bioarchaeological data. The relative economic contribution of maternal nursing was greater in the distant past than in recent history, rivaling regionally significant industries such as pottery, glassmaking, and even the historical public sector. It was likely the largest source of human capital investment in early modern England, supporting the physical and cognitive development of the workforce. Finally, we show that breastfeeding rates probably fell as incomes rose, challenging the Malthusian assumption that higher income correlates with lower mortality.

That is from a recent paper ( https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2600844123 )  by Louis Henderson and Jane Humphries.  Via the excellent Samir Varma ( https://x.com/samirvarma ) .
