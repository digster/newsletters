Yes I will be doing a Conversation with her.  From Wikipedia ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gita_Gopinath ) :

> Gita Gopinath...is an Indian-American economist ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist )  who is currently serving as the Gregory and Ania Coffey professor of Economics at Harvard University ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University )  and previously served as the first deputy managing director of the International Monetary Fund ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Monetary_Fund )  (IMF), from 21 January 2022 to 31 August 2025. Before that she also served as chief economist ( https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Economist_of_the_International_Monetary_Fund )  of the IMF between 2019 and 2022.

Here is Gita on scholar.google.com ( https://scholar.google.com/scholar?hl=en&as_sdt=0%2C47&q=gita+gopinath&btnG= ) , she is an expert in international finance and exchange rates, and also international capital flows, among other topics.  Here is Gita on Twitter ( https://x.com/GitaGopinath?lang=en ) .  So what should I ask her?
