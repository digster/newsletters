The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints has grown 66% this century, fueled in part by a record-breaking number of convert baptisms in 2025.
The church had 10,752,986 members at the
[end of 1999]. The church had 17,887,212 at the end of 2025, according to[an annual statistical report]released Saturday during the church’s[196th Annual General Conference].
Furthermore the growth is coming in every part of the world (as a qualifier I am not sure what the outflow is).
