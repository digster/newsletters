> The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints has grown 66% this century, fueled in part by a record-breaking number of convert baptisms in 2025.
>
> The church had 10,752,986 members at the end of 1999 ( https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2000/04/statistical-report-1999?lang=eng ) . The church had 17,887,212 at the end of 2025, according to an annual statistical report ( https://www.deseret.com/faith/2026/04/04/latter-day-saint-membership-passed-1788-million-in-2025-as-churchs-growth-accelerates/ )  released Saturday during the church’s 196th Annual General Conference ( https://www.deseret.com/faith/2026/04/04/april-2026-general-conference-talks-photos/ ) .
>

Furthermore the growth is coming in every part of the world (as a qualifier I am not sure what the outflow is).  Here is the full article ( https://www.deseret.com/faith/2026/04/05/latter-day-saint-growth-tops-66-since-2000-after-2025-shattered-old-record-for-convert-baptisms/ ) , via Tyler Ransom ( https://tyleransom.github.io/ ) .
