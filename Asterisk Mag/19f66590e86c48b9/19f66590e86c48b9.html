Asterisk in print, now shipping worldwide

Plus: Subscribe at $50/year before prices change on August 1

Jul 15

We have two pieces of news about print subscriptions:

  • You can now subscribe to print from anywhere in the world. If you live outside of the United States, buy a subscription to receive our print issues delivered to your door.

  • Our prices are going up, starting on August 1. A print subscription will be $80/year if you live in the United States and $100/year for everywhere else in the world. Shipping is included, no matter where you live.

This is our first price change since launching Asterisk nearly four years ago, and it brings us closer to what it costs to produce a quarterly print magazine, as well as everything we want to do beyond – such as publishing Dagger, our blog for timelier takes, and bringing more of our scenes and subcultures in conversation with each other. Print subscribers also receive early access to our events in the Bay Area and beyond.

Until the end of this month, you can still subscribe at the current price – $50/year in the United States, or $70 anywhere in the world – for the first year.

Subscribe now

If you’re currently a print subscriber, you won’t see a price change until your next annual renewal.

Finally, while you’re browsing our online store: we’ve now made our entire back catalog available for purchase. Collect the complete set, or pick up an extra copy for wherever you’re going this summer.
