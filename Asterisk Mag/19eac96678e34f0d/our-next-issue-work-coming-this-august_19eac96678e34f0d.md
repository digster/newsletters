Our next issue, Work: coming this AugustWhat do people do all day? For most of us, the answer involves some kind of job (for now). What we do to fill our time, put food on the table, and keep civilization running are as interesting to toddlers reading Richard Scarry as they are to adults reading Studs Terkel — especially now that it feels like work itself is in for the biggest set of changes since the Industrial Revolution. Issue 15 takes a long look at work: its history, its future, and all the ways it shapes our identity. Featuring pieces about: The socialist siblings who built a machine to liberate cotton farmers – and lost to John Deere What a multi-agent AI “village” can tell us about how work gets organized The unacknowledged, and unacknowledgeable, work of being a pastor’s wife The arc of India’s IT industry What we already know a world without work might look like
…and much more. Subscribe to our print magazine by June 30, and it’ll be delivered to your door in August. Finally: if you’d like to browse the archives for earlier editions of Asterisk, you can now Here’s to working smarter, - Clara, Angela, and Nadia |
