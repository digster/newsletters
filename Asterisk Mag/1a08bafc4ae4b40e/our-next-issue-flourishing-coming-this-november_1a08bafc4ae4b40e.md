Our next issue, Flourishing: coming this NovemberEveryone wants to build a better future, but ask them what it actually means for things to go well, and you’ll get a mishmash of competing visions. Should we homestead on a farm? Revert to a retrofuturistic, chrome-filled utopia? Issue 16: Flourishing is our attempt to take seriously the question of what it means, and what it will take, to thrive — as individuals, institutions, and a civilization that may soon be sharing the planet with minds that outclass us at nearly everything.
