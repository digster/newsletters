Everyone wants to build a better future, but ask them what it actually means for things to go well, and you’ll get a mishmash of competing visions. Should we homestead on a farm? Revert to a retrofuturistic, chrome-filled utopia?
Issue 16: Flourishing is our attempt to take seriously the question of what it means, and what it will take, to thrive — as individuals, institutions, and a civilization that may soon be sharing the planet with minds that outclass us at nearly everything.
Featuring:
Ken Liu and Ada Palmer on imagining AI futures
Kelsey Piper and Nicole Ruiz on education and childcare
Carmen Lau on meditators who voluntarily turn off their consciousness
Asif Ghazanfar on how to turn a philanderer into a father
Étienne Fortier-Dubois in defense of modern architecture
…and much more.
