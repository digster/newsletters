Book I’m rereading
------------------

​Buffalo for the Broken Heart: Restoring Life to a Black Hills Ranch ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/mvu9xermxzs5hq6xxkpsmhrxl7okrs3hlr9/reh8hohml3mkz8u2h6/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYW1hem9uLmNvbS9CdWZmYWxvLUJyb2tlbi1IZWFydC1SZXN0b3JpbmctQmxhY2svZHAvMDM3NTc2MTM5WC8_dGFnPW9mZnNpdG9mdGltZmUtMjA= ) by Dan O’Brien ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/mvu9xermxzs5hq6xxkpsmhrxl7okrs3hlr9/08hwh9h2692n50slh5/aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvRGFuX08nQnJpZW5fKGF1dGhvcik= ). From the description: “For twenty years, Dan O’Brien struggled to make ends meet on his cattle ranch in South Dakota. But when a neighbor invited him to lend a hand at the annual buffalo roundup, O’Brien was inspired to convert his own ranch, the Broken Heart, to buffalo. Starting with thirteen calves, ‘short-necked, golden balls of wool,’ O’Brien embarked on a journey that returned buffalo to his land for the first time in more than a century and a half. Buffalo for the Broken Heart is at once a tender account of the buffaloes’ first seasons on the ranch and an engaging lesson in wildlife ecology. … O’Brien combines a novelist’s eye for detail with a naturalist’s understanding to create an enriching, entertaining narrative.”
