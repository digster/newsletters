“How A.I. Helped One Man (and His Brother) Build a $1.8 Billion
Company (
https://click.fourhourmail.com/38u8kd42kehkh2g2qwdcrh4pd2qd8a7hegz/6qheh8hlrexv2rfo/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubnl0aW1lcy5jb20vMjAyNi8wNC8wMi90ZWNobm9sb2d5L2FpLWJpbGxpb24tZG9sbGFyLWNvbXBhbnktbWVkdmkuaHRtbA==
).” (The New York Times).
​
​From his house in Los Angeles, Mr. Gallagher, 41, used A.I. to
write the code for the software that powers his company, produce
the website copy, generate the images and videos for ads and
handle customer service. He created A.I. systems to analyze his
business’s performance. And he outsourced the other stuff he
couldn’t do himself.
​
His start-up, Medvi, a telehealth provider of GLP-1 weight-loss
drugs, got 300 customers in its first month. In its second month,
it gained 1,000 more. In 2025, Medvi’s first full year in
business, the company generated $401 million in sales.
​
Mr. Gallagher then hired his only employee, his younger brother,
Elliot. This year, they are on track to do $1.8 billion in sales.
