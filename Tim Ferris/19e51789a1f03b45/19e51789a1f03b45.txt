What I’m drinking in the mornings
---------------------------------

​Brodo Bone Broth ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/lmuzwpd0wosmhn9d79xu6h8w24363fgh0o4/48hvhehmen8n4esx/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYnJvZG8uY29tL3Byb2R1Y3QvYnJvZG8tc3RhcnRlci1ib3gv ). Broth is one of the easiest ways to get quality protein and nutrition first thing in the morning. For the past two months, I’ve been having 1–2 Brodo packets per day, often simply poured into a mug and microwaved for 60 seconds.
​
Brodo was founded by Chef Marco Canora, who was one of my primary teachers in The 4-Hour Chef ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/lmuzwpd0wosmhn9d79xu6h8w24363fgh0o4/wnh2hghqp363e2s7/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYW1hem9uLmNvbS80LUhvdXItQ2hlZi1Db29raW5nLUxlYXJuaW5nLUFueXRoaW5nL2RwLzEzMjg1MTkxNjMvP3RhZz1vZmZzaXRvZnRpbWZlLTIw ). ​Marco is the real deal. He won a James Beard Award for Best Chef: New York City, which is like winning an Oscar in the food world, and he has plenty of other awards and accolades to boot.
​
Brodo quickly became the most famous broth in NYC. The challenge for Marco has always been creating versions of his broths that (A) were shelf-stable, (B) met his high standards, and (C) didn’t require refrigeration… which he finally nailed.
​
My favorite flavors so far are Tuscan Sun and Tom Yum, which are included in their sampler box ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/lmuzwpd0wosmhn9d79xu6h8w24363fgh0o4/48hvhehmen8n4esx/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYnJvZG8uY29tL3Byb2R1Y3QvYnJvZG8tc3RhcnRlci1ib3gv ). If you wanna get fancy, you can use a milk frother to buzz extra-virgin olive oil (I like Kosterina ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/lmuzwpd0wosmhn9d79xu6h8w24363fgh0o4/reh8hohmrezeggb2/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cua29zdGVyaW5hLmNvbS9jb2xsZWN0aW9ucy9vbGl2ZS1vaWw_c3JzbHRpZD1BZm1CT29wQlprTExBMXNhV2ItU1ZJeFhQVFhaNVNESkdJbWRqYm1KeDN2dFNIQUd5Z0E5ZHZpZQ== )) into Tuscan Sun, and you can squeeze some lime juice into the Tom Yum for extra pop.
