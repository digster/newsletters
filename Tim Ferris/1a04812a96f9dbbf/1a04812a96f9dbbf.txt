Book I’m enjoying
-----------------

​Seeing Silence: The Beauty of the World’s Most Quiet Places ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/o8u50wr80xfqh60x5lxavhqp8d4llfoh75d/owhkhqhwq4ed4vhvhr/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYW1hem9uLmNvbS9kcC8wODQ3ODcwODYzP3RhZz1vZmZzaXRvZnRpbWZlLTIw ) by Pete McBride ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/o8u50wr80xfqh60x5lxavhqp8d4llfoh75d/z2hghnhelozxo2hph0/aHR0cHM6Ly9wZXRlbWNicmlkZS5jb20v ). The sublime is easy to find in this book, whether you simply flip through the gorgeous photos or do a deep dive into the prose. Pete McBride, a National Geographic photographer and filmmaker, shot the images across all seven continents, from Everest to Antarctica. The foreword is by Bill McKibben ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/o8u50wr80xfqh60x5lxavhqp8d4llfoh75d/p8heh9h4q9eo9nbqh3/aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvQmlsbF9NY0tpYmJlbg== ), a renowned environmentalist and journalist. The prologue is by Erik Weihenmayer ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/o8u50wr80xfqh60x5lxavhqp8d4llfoh75d/x0hph6heqnm0nqi5hl/aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvRXJpa19XZWloZW5tYXllcg== ), the first blind person to summit Everest.

Movie I’m surviving
-------------------

​Society of the Snow ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/o8u50wr80xfqh60x5lxavhqp8d4llfoh75d/6qheh8hl9p4ep3fohk/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubmV0ZmxpeC5jb20vdGl0bGUvODEyNjgzMTY= ), a film from Juan Antonio Bayona ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/o8u50wr80xfqh60x5lxavhqp8d4llfoh75d/kkhmh6hnx8pv8pclh7/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuaW5zdGFncmFtLmNvbS9GaWxtQmF5b25hLw== ) about one of the most remarkable stories of the twentieth century. Society of the Snow was selected to represent Spain at the 2024 Oscars, where it was nominated for two Academy Awards. It won 12 Goya Awards (including Best Film and Best Director), was nominated for a BAFTA and a Golden Globe, and premiered as the closing film at the Venice Film Festival.​
​
​Sometimes, I like to visit the brutal, so that I can better appreciate the everyday beauty I—and we—take for granted.
​
This film, spectacularly cast and executed, fits that bill. Not for the squeamish. If this movie doesn’t make your hands sweat, you should go climbing with Alex Honnold ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/o8u50wr80xfqh60x5lxavhqp8d4llfoh75d/58hvh7hg65w25ef6h4/aHR0cHM6Ly90aW0uYmxvZy8yMDE2LzA1LzE3L2FsZXgtaG9ubm9sZC8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1jb252ZXJ0a2l0JnV0bV9tZWRpdW09Y29udmVydGtpdCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249NWJm ).
​
The official description: “Following a plane crash in the remote heart of the Andes, survivors join forces and become each other’s best hope as they navigate their way back home.” See the trailer here ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/o8u50wr80xfqh60x5lxavhqp8d4llfoh75d/25h2hoh3m7xw2rf3h4/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cueW91dHViZS5jb20vd2F0Y2g_dj1wRGFrNHFMeUY0UQ== ).
