Book I’m reading
----------------

​Hard Times: An Illustrated Oral History of the Great Depression ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/o8u50wr80xfqh60455nhvhqp04325toh75d/l2hehmhlxn6d9nc6h0/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYW1hem9uLmNvbS9kcC9CMDA5MThKUTBDLz90YWc9b2Zmc2l0b2Z0aW1mZS0yMA== ) by Pulitzer Prize-winner Studs Terkel ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/o8u50wr80xfqh60455nhvhqp04325toh75d/m2h7h5h34qmlp9smhq/aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvU3R1ZHNfVGVya2Vs ). I quite unexpectedly came across this book, and it totally captivated me. I couldn’t put it down, even though I had no initial interest in a book on the Great Depression. The stories are incredible.
​
Studs Terkel interviewed hundreds of people who lived through the Great Depression and let them tell the story in their own words. The result is a collection of firsthand accounts—some devastating, some surprisingly funny—of how ordinary people got through it. This edition includes period photographs alongside the interviews.
​
Special thanks to Ed Cooke ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/o8u50wr80xfqh60455nhvhqp04325toh75d/dpheh0hepo82gwimh4/aHR0cHM6Ly90aW0uYmxvZy8yMDE0LzEyLzMwL2VkLWNvb2tlLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWNvbnZlcnRraXQmdXRtX21lZGl1bT1jb252ZXJ0a2l0JnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj01YmY= ), Grand Master of Memory and cofounder of the learning app MindMax ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/o8u50wr80xfqh60455nhvhqp04325toh75d/e0hph7h7rlqdg2h8h2/aHR0cHM6Ly9taW5kbWF4Lml0Lw== ).
