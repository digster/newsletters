Kitchen gear that I’m loving and using
--------------------------------------

​Ninja Air Fryer Pro ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/92u58xp28qfnhxkl44gt9hzzow2wwiwho3n/dpheh0hexd2d70umh4/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYW1hem9uLmNvbS9kcC9CMERXNEtHOFZNP2x2PXNodWYmdGFnPW9mZnNpdG9mdGltZmUtMjAmY2hhbm5lbElkPTEmcGxwUmVkaXJlY3Q9bWhGYWxsYmFjaw== ). This thing is amazing. I was skeptical (“Does ‘air frying’ even make sense?”), but now I’m a true believer. For fast cooking and even faster cleanup, it’s become a go-to in the kitchen. My favorite thing I’ve cooked so far is Maui Nui Reserve aged rib chops ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/92u58xp28qfnhxkl44gt9hzzow2wwiwho3n/e0hph7h7zmdmn7b8h2/aHR0cHM6Ly9tYXVpbnVpdmVuaXNvbi5jb20vcHJvZHVjdHMvcmVzZXJ2ZS1hZ2VkLXJpYi1jaG9w ). It’s literally what’s for dinner tonight. Here’s the recipe, if you can call it that: Get the chops to room temp, pat dry, rub on a little olive oil, add Montreal steak rub, then cook for 9 minutes at max temp (400°F). F’ing DELICIOUS, and cleaning is a breeze.

Tools I’m using to vet doctors
------------------------------

​Open Payments ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/92u58xp28qfnhxkl44gt9hzzow2wwiwho3n/7qh7h8h9edxd4wuzh6/aHR0cHM6Ly9vcGVucGF5bWVudHNkYXRhLmNtcy5nb3Yv ) (official US gov website) shows reported payments from drug and device companies to U.S. doctors. Doctor Payments Data ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/92u58xp28qfnhxkl44gt9hzzow2wwiwho3n/owhkhqhwex0x8ofvhr/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZG9jdG9ycGF5bWVudHNkYXRhLm9yZy8= ) makes those records easier to explore, so you might want to start there. Financial ties don’t automatically mean wrongdoing, but they’re worth understanding. Incentives affect all of us, and that includes medical professionals. As Charlie Munger famously said, “Show me the incentive, and I’ll show you the outcome.” I recently used these tools to choose between two surgeons. The doc who wanted to do the minimum effective dose of surgery had $0 in payments, and the doc who wanted to do more extensive procedures and implants had ~$1M in payments, including from the manufacturer of the implants. Again, this doesn’t mean bad behavior, but I was more comfortable with the completely free agent.
​
If a great medical professional—one who is known as a thought leader—has few or no conflicts, it’s probably not accidental. Turning down cushy consulting gigs, paid speaking, and related perks to preserve your objectivity isn’t easy. I’ve thought about possibly creating something like a MacArthur Grant ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/92u58xp28qfnhxkl44gt9hzzow2wwiwho3n/z2hghnhez5m5d0cph0/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubWFjZm91bmQub3JnL2Fib3V0Lw== ) to reward those people. What do you think?
