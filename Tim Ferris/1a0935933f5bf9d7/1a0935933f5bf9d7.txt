Nut butter my testes and I are enjoying

​Artisana Organics Raw Walnut Butter with Cashews ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/gku6dv5rd2s5hl2nqd0frh853r6l6cmh8ed/6qheh8hl995l45cohk/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYW1hem9uLmNvbS9kcC9CMDA0Sk5YRE5LLz90YWc9b2Zmc2l0b2Z0aW1mZS0yMA== ) (Amazon ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/gku6dv5rd2s5hl2nqd0frh853r6l6cmh8ed/6qheh8hl995l45cohk/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYW1hem9uLmNvbS9kcC9CMDA0Sk5YRE5LLz90YWc9b2Zmc2l0b2Z0aW1mZS0yMA== ), Artisana ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/gku6dv5rd2s5hl2nqd0frh853r6l6cmh8ed/kkhmh6hnxx5npeilh7/aHR0cHM6Ly9hcnRpc2FuYW1hcmtldC5jb20vcHJvZHVjdHMvb3JnYW5pYy13YWxudXQtYnV0dGVyLXdpdGgtY2FzaGV3cw== )). This contains just two ingredients: organic raw walnuts and organic raw cashews. Artisana also makes my favorite almond butter, and both are delicious.

Gents, walnuts are good for your swimmers.

The strongest walnut-specific study ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/gku6dv5rd2s5hl2nqd0frh853r6l6cmh8ed/58hvh7hg66ngwou6h4/aHR0cHM6Ly9wdWJtZWQubmNiaS5ubG0ubmloLmdvdi8yMjg5NTg1Ni8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1jaGF0Z3B0LmNvbQ== ) was a 2012 randomized controlled trial in 117 healthy men ages 21–35 eating a Western-style diet. They were randomized to either continue their diet or add 75 g/day of walnuts (~2.6 oz) for 12 weeks. This amount isn’t trivial, but it isn’t impossible, either. 75 grams is roughly 0.63 cups of chopped walnuts, which amounts to roughly 2.5 handfuls per day.
​
Compared with controls, the walnut group improved:

Sperm vitality: significant, P = 0.003
Sperm motility: significant, P = 0.009
Normal morphology: significant, P ≈ 0.03–0.04
Sperm concentration: no significant improvement

Nuts seem to be generally supportive of fertility. A 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/gku6dv5rd2s5hl2nqd0frh853r6l6cmh8ed/25h2hoh3mm83xei3h4/aHR0cHM6Ly9wdWJtZWQubmNiaS5ubG0ubmloLmdvdi8zNzk3NzMyOC8= ) also showed that consuming at least 60 g/day of nuts improved sperm motility, vitality, and morphology, although it did not significantly improve sperm concentration.

Science I’m following
---------------------

“Brain-Stimulating Contact Lenses Match Prozac in Depression Study ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/gku6dv5rd2s5hl2nqd0frh853r6l6cmh8ed/qvh8h7hdkk4d36blhk/aHR0cHM6Ly9uZXVyb3NjaWVuY2VuZXdzLmNvbS9icmFpbi1zdGltdWxhdGluZy1jb250YWN0LWxlbnNlcy1kZXByZXNzaW9uLTMwNjkwLw== )” (Neuroscience News).
​
​Materials scientists have developed a ‘wearable, drug-free’ contact lens capable of treating depression by delivering mild electrical signals through the retina. The study demonstrates that these specialized lenses are as effective as fluoxetine (the active ingredient in Prozac) at reducing behavioral, neural, and physiological signs of depression in mice. This technology leverages the eye’s anatomical connection to the brain to stimulate mood-regulating circuits non-invasively.​
​
Treating the brain through the eye opens a fascinating new frontier, and it is one I am excited to follow. Here’s theoriginal paper ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/gku6dv5rd2s5hl2nqd0frh853r6l6cmh8ed/g3hnh5hmqqlm2etrh9/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuc2NpZW5jZWRpcmVjdC5jb20vc2NpZW5jZS9hcnRpY2xlL3BpaS9TMjY2NjM4NjQyNjAwMjA5Mg== ) in Cell Reports Physical Science if you want to go deeper. Sincere thanks to Dr. JF for putting this on my radar.
