​Momofuku Soy Sauce (

brewed soy sauce, meaning it’s produced using the traditional
microbial fermentation process rather than chemical hydrolysis. I
bought this at Whole Foods.
​
Momofuku is the brainchild of legendary chef David Chang (
https://click.fourhourmail.com/75u0qzo2qkh8h6l9vr2f3hwrv452dbnhz7r/9qhzhnhdwkol9lu9/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuaW5zdGFncmFtLmNvbS9kYXZpZGNoYW5nLw==
), who Esquire called one of the most influential people of the
twenty-first century. His team’s soy sauce is built on 10 years
of flavor research from the Momofuku Goods team, and it’s steeped
with kombu (
https://click.fourhourmail.com/75u0qzo2qkh8h6l9vr2f3hwrv452dbnhz7r/3ohphkh3m6enzdhr/aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvS29tYnU=
) for 48 hours, which gives it a richer, rounder savory profile
than anything you’ll find at the grocery store. It’s
restaurant-grade and an easy way to elevate simple things like
steamed vegetables or give an Asian twist to main dishes.
