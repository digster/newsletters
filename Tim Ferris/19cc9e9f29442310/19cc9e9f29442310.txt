What I’m enjoying
-----------------

​Lost Pines Light Roast Yaupon Tea — loose leaf, 2 oz. (
https://click.fourhourmail.com/27u53lgo36foh89gkl6t4hg38eodwsgh7w8/reh8hohm4ggmr6i2/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYW1hem9uLmNvbS9Mb3N0LVBpbmVzLVlhdXBvbi1UZWEtY2FmZmVpbmF0ZWQvZHAvQjA3NTM5NTdCSC8_dGFnPW9mZnNpdG9mdGltZmUtMjA=
) Yaupon holly is one of only two caffeinated plants native to
North America. Though technically not a true tea (it’s not
camellia sinensis), it was widely consumed by Indigenous peoples
long before European contact. It has been enjoying a revival,
especially in Texas. Yaupon’s caffeine content is similar to
green tea, and it also contains theobromine, which some of you
may recognize from cacao / dark chocolate. Just as with yerba
mate, this combination gives me a smoother, less anxious energy
curve than coffee. It’s very neutral and not bitter at all.
Despite its unfortunate Latin name, Ilex vomitoria, I’m enjoying
experimenting with it.
