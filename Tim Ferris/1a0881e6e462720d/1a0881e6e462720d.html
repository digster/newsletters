​🎧 New podcast episode:
​Tales of Overcoming the Odds, and How to Turn Losses Into Wins — Tim McGraw, Terry Crews, Dax Shepard, and More​

This is a special episode about overcoming mistakes and failure. What do you do when your work is rejected, when the big bet fails, or when the path ahead suddenly disappears? As you’ll hear in this episode, setbacks can often present opportunities if you know how to look for them.

In this episode:

BJ Miller, a triple amputee, on his years-long quest to ride a motorcycle.

Terry Crews on missing a season-ending shot—and the mindset shift that stayed with him for life.

Tim McGraw on his debut album flopping and betting his career on next steps.

Jessica Lahey on being told her first book was “unpublishable” and turning brutal feedback into a New York Times bestseller.

Dax Shepard on the collapse of an identity and career he thought he wanted—and the unexpected much better life it made possible.

Cathy Lanier on becoming a teenage single mother, passing her GED by a single point, and beginning an unlikely climb.

Janna Levin on the liberating realization that life is not a smooth path interrupted by obstacles—life is the obstacles.

Boyd Varty on using the mindset of a lion tracker to move through uncertainty and the paralysis of not knowing what comes next.
​
Please enjoy!
