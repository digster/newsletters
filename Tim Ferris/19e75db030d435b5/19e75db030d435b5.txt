What I’m reading
----------------

"Gell-Mann amnesia effect ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/k0u3wp9vw7b6h50epe2alhop0885nf8h08x/dpheh0heg8gr2ohm/aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvTWljaGFlbF9DcmljaHRvbiMlMjJHZWxsLU1hbm5fYW1uZXNpYV9lZmZlY3QlMjI= )" This was coined by the incredible author, Michael Crichton ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/k0u3wp9vw7b6h50epe2alhop0885nf8h08x/e0hph7h7gqg5deh8/aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvTWljaGFlbF9DcmljaHRvbg== ), who created Jurassic Park ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/k0u3wp9vw7b6h50epe2alhop0885nf8h08x/7qh7h8h97w73xptz/aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvSnVyYXNzaWNfUGFyaw== ), the original Westworld ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/k0u3wp9vw7b6h50epe2alhop0885nf8h08x/owhkhqhwvgv507hv/aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvV2VzdHdvcmxkXyhUVl9zZXJpZXMp ), the ER ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/k0u3wp9vw7b6h50epe2alhop0885nf8h08x/z2hghnhevpv0mdtp/aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvRVJfKFRWX3Nlcmllcyk= ) TV series, and countless others. From his Wikipedia page:
​
​[Crichton] explained that he had chosen the name ironically, because he had once discussed the effect with physicist Murray Gell-Mann, "and by dropping a famous name I imply greater importance to myself, and to the effect, than it would otherwise have."​
​
​Briefly stated, the Gell-Mann Amnesia effect works as follows. You open the newspaper to an article on some subject you know well. In Murray's case, physics. In mine, show business. You read the article and see the journalist has absolutely no understanding of either the facts or the issues. Often, the article is so wrong it actually presents the story backward-reversing cause and effect. I call these the "wet streets cause rain" stories. Paper's full of them. In any case, you read with exasperation or amusement the multiple errors in a story-and then turn the page to national or international affairs, and read with renewed interest as if the rest of the newspaper was somehow more accurate about far-off Palestine than it was about the story you just read. You turn the page, and forget what you know.
