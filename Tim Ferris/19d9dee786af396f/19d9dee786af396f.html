Book I’m reading

​About This Life: Journeys on the Threshold of Memory by Barry Lopez. I have fallen in love with Barry Lopez’s writing. If you’re new to his work, I would suggest reading Of Wolves and Men or Arctic Dreams first, as they are nonfiction at its best. About This Life is a mostly autobiographical collection of essays. In descending order, my favorites are: “Learning to See,” “Orchids on the Volcanoes,” and “Apologia.”

Study I’m supporting

“Investigating Rapid-Acting TMS for Adolescent Depression.” Do you know a teenager (ages 13–20) experiencing symptoms of depression? If so, this may be worth a look. Dr. Sean O’Sullivan and his team at UT Austin are running a clinical trial using accelerated and personalized transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) to treat depression. It is drug-free, and the treatment is only five days long.
​
This is the very approach I’ve used personally, and I had my most recent TMS treatment for GAD/OCD last week. The results are some of the most impressive I’ve ever seen.
​
If you or someone you know might benefit, you can reach the study team directly: texassaintstudy@gmail.com or (512) 495-5995. Find their ClinicalTrials.gov listing here (NCT06523439). Participants who attend all visits will receive a total of $525. The FDA cleared TMS for adolescent depression in 2024.
​
​Disclaimer: To be an eligible participant for this study, you must have had fewer than 3 months of antidepressant medication treatment in the past year and fewer than 10 psychotherapy sessions in the past year.
