Study I’m supporting
--------------------

“Investigating Rapid-Acting TMS for Adolescent Depression (
https://click.fourhourmail.com/8ku6kplgkosoh2nz25mckhkodeognb3hp9k/58hvh7hg20mgmzu6/aHR0cHM6Ly9zaXRlcy51dGV4YXMuZWR1L29zdWxsaXZhbmxhYi8=
).” Do you know a teenager (ages 13–20) experiencing symptoms of
depression? If so, this may be worth a look. Dr. Sean O’Sullivan
and his team at UT Austin are running a clinical trial using
accelerated and personalized transcranial magnetic stimulation
(TMS) to treat depression. It is drug-free, and the treatment is
only five days long.
​
This is the very approach I’ve used personally, and I had my most
recent TMS treatment for GAD/OCD last week. The results are some
of the most impressive I’ve ever seen.
​
If you or someone you know might benefit, you can reach the study
team directly: texassaintstudy@gmail.com (
https://click.fourhourmail.com/8ku6kplgkosoh2nz25mckhkodeognb3hp9k/25h2hoh3wg232oh3/bWFpbHRvOnRleGFzc2FpbnRzdHVkeUBnbWFpbC5jb20=
) or (512) 495-5995. Find their ClinicalTrials.gov listing here
(NCT06523439) (
https://click.fourhourmail.com/8ku6kplgkosoh2nz25mckhkodeognb3hp9k/qvh8h7hdpvrdr8il/aHR0cHM6Ly9jbGluaWNhbHRyaWFscy5nb3Yvc3R1ZHkvTkNUMDY1MjM0Mzk=
). Participants who attend all visits will receive a total of
$525. The FDA cleared TMS for adolescent depression in 2024.
​
​Disclaimer: To be an eligible participant for this study, you
must have had fewer than 3 months of antidepressant medication
treatment in the past year and fewer than 10 psychotherapy
sessions in the past year.
