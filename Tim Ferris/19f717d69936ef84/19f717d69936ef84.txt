What I’m rewatching
-------------------

​Life Is Beautiful ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/68ug9o7692b8h5mq9g4tohpz6327oh9h6rd/kkhmh6hn073mmpflh7/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYW1hem9uLmNvbS9ncC92aWRlby9kZXRhaWwvYW16bjEuZHYuZ3RpLmRmNmFlMzIzLWQ0MzYtNGUwNS04ZWU1LTg5ZjlmMDU2YjZmZQ== ). My lady hadn’t seen this, so I insisted that we watch it together. It’s an unforgettable masterpiece.
​​
​This 1997 classic was co-written and directed by Roberto Benigni ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/68ug9o7692b8h5mq9g4tohpz6327oh9h6rd/58hvh7hgdez77eh6h4/aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvUm9iZXJ0b19CZW5pZ25p ), who also plays the lead role. Life Is Beautiful follows Guido, a Jewish-Italian father who shields his young son from the horrors of a concentration camp by convincing him it’s all an elaborate game. That’s how the movie is typically described, but that’s truthfully only the second half of the movie, and there is much more to it.
​
Perhaps better than any film I’ve ever seen, it uses hilarity to explore one of the darkest real-world chapters in recent human history. It’s a rare film that is chock-full of belly laughs and gut punches.
​
Thank me later when you find yourself saying “Buongiorno, Principessa!”
