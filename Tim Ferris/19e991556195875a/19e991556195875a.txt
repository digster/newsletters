How I’m finally getting in more Zone 2 cardio
---------------------------------------------

​M3i Studio Indoor Bike | Keiser ( https://click.fourhourmail.com/lmuzwpd0wosmhnm6o2nc6h8wm9r56ugh0o4/wnh2hghqp7zwxlu7hx/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cua2Vpc2VyLmNvbS9zaG9wL2NhcmRpby1lcXVpcG1lbnQvbTNpLWluZG9vci1iaWtlP3Nyc2x0aWQ9QWZtQk9vcU5tanEzR3NkMVNTNndVYWNkNjZTMmRnY1VUM2RkOFdCbVRocEdFWE1xcl9oRjdHWFk= ). I’ve tried a lot of stationary bikes, including very fancy indoor trainers, for getting in Zone 2 cardio and VO2 max training. I hate nearly all of them. This M3i is *not* fancy, but it does allow me to elevate the handlebars enough so that (1) I’m not bumping my thighs into my stomach like a one-hour Heimlich maneuver, and (2) it doesn’t make my low back incredibly grumpy. The controls are simple, it’s durable, I don’t use an app with it, and, so far, it’s the only indoor bike that I’ve used consistently. And more good news: it’s found in a lot of commercial gyms.
