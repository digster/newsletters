From today, Works in Progress is now in stock at Barnes & Nobles nationwide across America.
We’re in 570 stores in 49 states*, including the flagship stores at Union Square & Fifth Avenue in NYC; Georgetown, DC; State Street, Chicago; The Grove, Los Angeles; and the Barnes & Noble branches at San Francisco Airport.
We’re also now in Hudson News outlets across most major US airports, and a handful of independent newsagents in New York, Paris, Berlin, Los Angeles, and London including [magCulture](https://substack.com/redirect/97f0d630-c995-4e68-95f9-8d8857d27c73?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o) in Clerkenwell (from tomorrow) and [Shreeji News on Chiltern St](https://substack.com/redirect/e2ca20d9-2709-4960-87ff-358e71bd6113?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o).
If you Substack Note, Tweet, LinkedIn, or Instagram a photo of your copy of Works in Progress outside any of these stores, share the post with us and we’ll send you a free tote bag as a thank you. If you’re a subscriber already, feel free to do it with your own copy!
You can see a full map of [Barnes & Noble stockists here](https://substack.com/redirect/ba14f518-c741-44f0-a0c8-3168796416b2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o), and enter your Zip code to find your nearest store.
*Sorry, Wyoming, we’ll get to you!
