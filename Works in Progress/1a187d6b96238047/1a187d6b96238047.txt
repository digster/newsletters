Britain struggles to build almost anything: houses, infrastructure, data centres, and everything else. Its economy is anaemic. It has the most expensive energy of almost anywhere in the world. Its labour market looks dangerously exposed to shocks to professional services. The British state is paralysed and unable to act.
​Two years ago, when we published Foundations: Why Britain has stagnated [ https://substack.com/redirect/6409e8e3-14b6-413d-afdf-78d1ca549cbf?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ], these were important facts for anyone who cared about reviving Britain’s economy. Today, they will decide whether Britain thrives in the age of AI or goes the way of the Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth and twentieth century Argentina.
​You are invited to join us at Building Abundance: How Britain can flourish in the age of AI [ https://substack.com/redirect/55eb303c-cebe-4b5e-aebb-adc177b5be4f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG81NG4ifQ.6oeetudtJEh-zO1onUJvtadWEdbodFIw0h4xbugrh1o ], a day of ideas for rebuilding Britain’s economy and state so it can adapt to and embrace artificial intelligence and other opportunities for growth.
​It will be held on Thursday 3rd December at the London School of Economics, from 1pm to 7:30pm (doors open 1pm for a 1:20pm start). This is hosted by Works in Progress in association with the LSE Law School and School of Public Policy. Tickets are free.
